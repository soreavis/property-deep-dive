# Universal `--visa` Section

Residency-by-investment + citizenship-by-investment + property-linked visa landscape across all 44 supported countries.

**Snapshot**: April 2026.

**Anti-hallucination — CRITICAL**: many programs ENDED or restricted in 2023-2025. Verify current status before listing as open. If a memory or stale source asserts a program exists, cross-check with the government portal in this file before relaying to a user.

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:
1. **Program name(s)** (current and any recently-ended ones a user might still ask about)
2. **Status as of Apr 2026**: OPEN / RESTRICTED / ENDED with effective date
3. **Minimum property/investment threshold**
4. **Path to PR / citizenship**: years, language, presence requirements
5. **Property-linked vs not**: many programs decoupled from real-estate
6. **Key caveats**: 2025-26 changes
7. **Confidence**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW

## ⚠️ Anti-hallucination findings — programs ENDED / SUSPENDED

**Do NOT list these as open**:

1. **Spain Golden Visa** — ENDED **3 Apr 2025** via Ley Orgánica 1/2025. Existing holders grandfathered; renewals continue under old rules; no new applications.
2. **Ireland IIP** — ENDED **15 Feb 2023** with one day's notice. ~1,500 cases in pipeline grandfathered.
3. **Malta MEIN/IIP** — ECJ ruled **illegal under EU law on 29 Apr 2025** (Case C-181/23). Treat as ENDED; verify any formal Maltese repeal.
4. **Cyprus CBI** — ENDED **1 Nov 2020** (53% of issued passports later found illegal). Cyprus PR-by-investment (€300k RE) is a SEPARATE, still-open programme.
5. **Bulgaria CBI** — ENDED **Apr 2022**. New 2024 "Golden Visa" is PR-only via €512k AIF/ETF, not citizenship.
6. **Australia SIV (188C)** / entire BIIP — CLOSED **31 Jul 2024**. Replacement National Innovation Visa has **no investor stream**.
7. **UK Tier 1 Investor** — CLOSED **17 Feb 2022**. Legacy: extension cutoff 17 Feb 2026, ILR cutoff 17 Feb 2028.
8. **Portugal NHR** — ENDED **Mar 2024**. Replacement IFICI / NHR 2.0 is much narrower (research/innovation only; pensions NO LONGER exempt).
9. **Portugal Golden Visa real-estate route** — REMOVED **Oct 2023** ("Mais Habitação"). Fund/cultural/research routes still open.
10. **Hungary Guest Investor real-estate option (€500k)** — REMOVED **15 Jan 2025**. Only €250k fund and €1m donation remain.
11. **Montenegro CBI** — ENDED 31 Dec 2022.
12. **Albania CBI** — proposed 2020+ but **never launched**; do not list.

**NEW / CHANGED programs (Apr 2026)**:

- **Montenegro Residency by Property** — NEW threshold **€150,000 tax-assessed value** (not contract price) effective **17 Jan 2026**. Pre-Jan-2026 holders grandfathered.
- **NZ Active Investor Plus** — REVISED **1 Apr 2025**: NZD 5m / 3 yrs (Growth) or NZD 10m / 5 yrs (Balanced). Presence reduced to 21 days (Growth) or 105 days (Balanced). Early 2026 reform allows AIP holders to buy 1 residential property min NZD 5m.
- **Argentina CBI (Decree 524/2025, Jul 2025)** — proposed ~US$500k waiving 2-yr residency for naturalisation. **Implementing regulations not finalised** as of Apr 2026.
- **Argentina rentista** — tightened by Decree 366/2025 (29 May 2025): mandatory health insurance, no free public healthcare/education for non-PR.
- **Canada Start-Up Visa & Self-Employed** — redesign announced 19 Dec 2025; new 2026 entrepreneur pilot launching, mechanics unclear.
- **Canada Quebec QIIP** — reopened 1 Jan 2024 with French level-7 requirement (effectively dormant due to language test).
- **Romania Golden Visa** — PROPOSED Oct 2025 at €400k; **NOT YET ENACTED** as of Apr 2026.
- **Greece Golden Visa** — €800k Attica/Thessaloniki/Mykonos/Santorini/islands >3,100 pop; €400k elsewhere; €250k for commercial-to-residential conversions or listed buildings; **120 sqm minimum surface** (except conversions); single property only.
