# Ghana 🇬🇭 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `gh`. Status: PARTIALLY OPEN — non-Ghanaians are restricted to **leasehold (max 50 years renewable)** under 1992 Constitution Art. 266; Ghanaian diaspora pathways via Akwaaba / Right of Abode + Pan-African Identity Card framework. Currency-volatile (GHS sharp depreciation 2022-23, IMF ECF May 2023 – concluded 27 Jul 2026, now a non-financing Policy Coordination Instrument). Customary tenure (stool / skin / family / clan land) overlays ~78% of total land area per Lands Commission classification — single largest due-diligence trap. Researched 2026-05.

## Country profile

- **Population**: ~33.5M (Ghana Statistical Service 2024 mid-year est.; 2021 Population & Housing Census recorded 30.8M; source `https://statsghana.gov.gh/`). Greater Accra ~5.4M; Ashanti (Kumasi metro) ~5.6M; Western (Sekondi-Takoradi) ~2.1M; Central (Cape Coast) ~2.9M; Eastern, Volta, Northern, Bono, Bono East, Ahafo, Savannah, North East, Upper East, Upper West, Oti, Western North = remaining 12 of the 16 regions (post-2018 region creation).
- **Postcode**: **GhanaPostGPS digital address** (e.g., `GA-184-3589` for Accra) — National Digital Address System launched 2017 (`https://ghanapostgps.com/`); used in addition to traditional district-based postal addresses (GPO box culture predominates). Verify with seller pre-contract.
- **Admin levels**: 16 **Regions** (since 2018 creation of 6 new regions) → 261 **Metropolitan / Municipal / District Assemblies (MMDAs)**. Property rates + building permits = MMDA-level; cadastre + title registration = national (Lands Commission).
- **Currency**: **GHS — Ghanaian cedi** (₵, ISO 4217 GHS). **Régime**: managed-float; sharp depreciation 2022 (BoG inter-bank end-period GHS 6.02/USD Jan 2022 → 13.10 Nov 2022); slide to 16.30 (Oct 2024), then sharp appreciation to 10.28 (May 2025); **GHS 11.6258/USD BoG inter-bank mid-rate on 28 Oct 2026** (end-Oct 2025: 10.90, so about 6.7 % more cedis per dollar year on year) (2026-10-29 verified, source BoG). Verify daily at `https://www.bog.gov.gh/`. **The IMF Extended Credit Facility is OVER.** The USD 3 billion 36-month ECF (approved 17 May 2023, extended three months to 16 Aug 2026) closed when the Executive Board completed the **sixth and final review on 27 July 2026**, clearing a final disbursement of about USD 371 million; Ghana has moved to a 36-month **non-financing Policy Coordination Instrument** (reform monitoring and technical assistance, no further Fund money). Do NOT describe GHS management as programme-conditioned. Source: IMF Country Report No. 2026/212 (4 Aug 2026) `https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2026/212/002.2026.issue-212-en.xml` + Presidency of Ghana 15 May 2026 `https://presidency.gov.gh/ghana-successfully-completes-imf-bailout-programme-transitions-to-policy-coordination-instrument`. (2026-08-06 verified) USD informal pricing common in prime Accra (Cantonments, Airport, East Legon, Labone, Ridge, Roman Ridge) — flag in underwriting.
- **Languages**: English (official; courts, conveyancing); Twi (Akan), Ga, Ewe, Dagbani, Hausa major regional; English universally used in legal documents.
- **Legal system**: Common-law (English heritage; pre-1957 Gold Coast colonial legacy) + **customary law** (stool / skin / family / clan tenure) layered onto state framework. Property = common-law contract + Lands Commission registration + customary allocation.
- **Cadastre — Lands Commission (national)**:
  - **Lands Commission** (`https://lc.gov.gh/`) — Land Act, 2020 (Act 1036; assented and in force 23 Dec 2020; consolidates earlier land statutes — the count of 6 is not re-verified). Four divisions: Public & Vested Lands Management; Survey & Mapping; Land Registration; Land Valuation.
  - **Land Use & Spatial Planning Authority (LUSPA)** — `https://luspa.gov.gh/` — spatial planning, zoning (Land Use & Spatial Planning Act 2016, Act 925).
  - **Title Registration** (introduced by the Land Title Registration Act 1986, PNDCL 152) — **rolling out by district** (Accra, Kumasi most advanced); the remaining districts are on **Deeds Registration**, which the Lands Commission now gazettes 'in accordance with the provisions of the Land Act, 2020 (Act 1036)' rather than the Conveyancing Act 1973 (NRCD 175) — whether NRCD 175 and PNDCL 152 are fully repealed was not confirmed on a page read.   - **Two parallel registration systems coexist** during the transition: **Title** (definitive, indefeasible) vs **Deed** (record of transaction, not title) — Title supersedes Deed when district migrates.
- **Identifiers**: **Site Plan + Lithograph (cadastral plan)**, **Indenture / Lease number**, **Title Certificate Number** (in Title Registration districts).
- **Recent reforms** (verify date-stamped):
  - **Land Act, 2020 (Act 1036)** — assented and commenced 23 Dec 2020; consolidates earlier land statutes (count of 6 not re-verified); its Legislative Instrument for a National Digital Land Registry is due in 2026 per the 2026 Budget (`https://lc.gov.gh/`; 2026-10-29 verified)
  - **IMF Extended Credit Facility** approved May 2023 — **CONCLUDED 27 July 2026** (sixth and final review); succeeded by a non-financing Policy Coordination Instrument
  - **E-Levy REPEALED April 2025** via Electronic Transfer Levy (Repeal) Act 2025 (President Mahama assented 2 Apr 2025; GRA directed all charging entities to cease collection immediately). Act 1075 (2022) + Act 1089 (2022; assented 29 Dec 2022, applied by GRA from 1 Jan 2023) repealed (2026-05-27 verified, source: GRA + Presidency).
  - **Mahama 2025 fiscal reform package** (April 2025): E-Levy + Betting Tax + Emissions Levy repealed; **VAT Act 2025 (Act 1151) effective 1 Jan 2026** (overhauled scheme — see below); Income Tax Amendment 2025; Growth & Sustainability Levy Act; Gold Board Bill.   - **Year of Return 2019** + **Beyond the Return 2020-onwards** (Ministry of Tourism, Culture & Creative Arts; Ghana Tourism Authority — `https://www.ghana.travel/`) — diaspora-engagement framework continuing

## Foreign buyer eligibility

Non-Ghanaian individuals and majority-foreign companies are constitutionally restricted to **leasehold** in Ghana. The 1992 Constitution Art. 266 is the foundational rule.

### 1992 Constitution Art. 266 — the ceiling

Source: Constitution of the Republic of Ghana 1992 (`https://www.parliament.gh/`).

| Clause | Effect |
|---|---|
| Art. 266(1) | "No interest in, or right over, any land in Ghana shall be created which vests in a person who is not a citizen of Ghana a freehold interest in any land in Ghana." |
| Art. 266(2)–(5) | (2) a deed conferring freehold on a non-citizen is void; (3) and (5) non-citizen freeholds and longer leases held on 22 Aug 1969 were cut to 50-year terms from that date; **(4) no leasehold of more than 50 years "at any one time"** for a non-citizen. The text does not itself grant renewal; check the lease and the Land Act, 2020 for renewal terms. |
| Company test | ⚠️ Not in Art. 266: the published text has five clauses, none on companies. Whether a majority-Ghanaian-owned company counts as a citizen for land purposes: data not publicly available on a primary page read 2026-10-29 — verify the Land Act, 2020 (Act 1036) with a Ghanaian lawyer. |
| Art. 267 | (1) Stool lands vest in the appropriate stool in trust for its subjects; (2) the Office of the Administrator of Stool Lands (OASL — `https://oasl.gov.gh/`) collects stool-land revenue; **(3) no disposition or development of stool land unless the Regional Lands Commission certifies it** (the concurrence); (5) no freehold "howsoever described" may be created in stool land. |

### Practical foreign-buyer constraints

| Buyer type | Freehold | Leasehold |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghanaian citizen (incl. by descent / naturalised)** | ✅ Allowed (subject to underlying tenure type) | ✅ Allowed |
| **Non-Ghanaian individual** | ❌ **Constitutionally prohibited** | ✅ **Max 50 years at any one time, renewable** |
| **Company majority Ghanaian-owned** | ✅ Treated as citizen | ✅ Allowed |
| **Company majority foreign-owned** | ❌ Treated as non-citizen | ✅ Max 50 years renewable |

### Right of Abode + diaspora pathway

- **Right of Abode** (Immigration Act 2000 Act 573 ss. 17-18 as amended by Act 848 2012): renewable indefinite right to enter, live, work in Ghana; available to **persons of African descent**. Does NOT confer citizenship; does NOT remove the Art. 266 freehold bar by itself.
- **Citizenship by registration / naturalisation** (Citizenship Act 2000 Act 591) is the only route by which a non-Ghanaian gains the constitutional right to hold freehold; thresholds + procedure at Ministry of the Interior + GIS (`https://www.gis.gov.gh/`).
- **Akwaaba ("Welcome") / Year of Return / Beyond the Return**: diaspora-engagement programs — they coordinate visa, immigration, investment introductions; **they do NOT amend Art. 266 freehold restrictions**. Marketing language equating "Akwaaba" with "investor visa with property rights" oversimplifies — verify any specific package directly at GIS / Ministry of the Interior.

### Critical caveat — customary tenure overlays most parcels

Per Lands Commission classification, ~78% of land in Ghana is held under **customary tenure** (stool, skin, family, clan); ~20% public/state; ~2% private freehold/leasehold direct. "Buying" land outside major formally-allocated zones in Accra/Kumasi typically means: (a) chief/family allocation (with elders' witness + customary fee), (b) Indenture / Lease drafted by lawyer, (c) Concurrence from the Lands Commission, (d) registration. Skipping Lands Commission registration is the dominant fraud vector. Always insist on full chain (Allocation Note → Indenture → Concurrence → Registration → Title or Deed Certificate).

### Other restrictions

- **Agricultural land**: foreign acquisition framework subject to Lands Commission approval; large-scale farming via leasehold structures + investment registration with Ghana Investment Promotion Centre (`https://gipc.gov.gh/`).
- **Mineral-bearing land**: Minerals & Mining Act 2006 (Act 703) reserves mineral rights to the state regardless of surface title.
- **Coastal reserves + protected areas**: Forestry Commission + EPA approvals separately.
- **Diplomatic acquisition**: separate Ministry of Foreign Affairs framework.

### Compute

1. **Listing GHS/m² or USD/m²** = price ÷ m² built. Clarify "built-up area" vs "plot size"; **plot size convention**: 1 plot ≈ 70 ft × 100 ft ≈ 21.34 m × 30.48 m ≈ ~650 m² is the most common Accra/Kumasi residential plot — verify per listing as conventions vary.
2. Convert GHS ↔ USD at the **BoG inter-bank rate** (`https://www.bog.gov.gh/economic-data/exchange-rate/` — end-September 2026: GHS 11.71/USD; 2026-10-29 verified) — managed-float regime; spread to commercial-bank rate est. 1-3% (unsourced).
3. Cross-check 3+ listings + developer-direct quote + Knight Frank's biennial Africa Report (secondary) before underwriting.
4. **Trap — dual-currency**: Cantonments / Airport / East Legon often quote in USD with GHS for show; USD price typically binding — confirm currency in writing in Indenture.
5. **Trap — off-plan vs cash**: headline GHS 1,200,000 off-plan may be 24-48 mo installment; cash-equivalent est. 15-30% lower (unsourced) — demand cash-equivalent quote and delivery warranties.
6. **Trap — plot vs m² confusion**: "half-plot at GHS 600,000" in Pokuase ≈ 325 m² nominal but actual surveyed area can deviate (est. ±10%, unsourced) — demand registered Site Plan with surveyed coordinates.
7. **Trap — declared vs actual**: stamp-duty declared est. 20-40% below actual cash price (informal practice; unsourced); verify per Lands Commission Land Valuation Division, which assesses stamp duty.

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — asking listings abundant in Accra/Kumasi; **transaction-verified price data not publicly available** (Lands Commission records paid + parcel-by-parcel). Foreign buyers should commission a local lawyer + valuer for 3-5 sold-comparables + cross-check Knight Frank's biennial Africa Report.

### Accra-specific notes

- **Spintex Road** + **Tema Beach Road / George W. Bush Highway** + **Tetteh Quarshie Interchange / N1 corridor**: peak gridlock 6:30-9am / 4:30-7pm; 2-hour one-way commutes Tema↔central Accra possible at peak.
- **Accra-Tema Motorway (N1)**: not tolled at present — the 2022 Budget (presented 17 Nov 2021) zero-rated tolls "on all public roads and bridges" (`https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2022_Budget_Statement_v3.pdf` ¶306). The 2026 Mid-Year Review says negotiations with the selected concessionaire for reintroducing road and bridge tolls are concluded and the agreement is expected before end-2026 (`https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2026-Mid-Year-Fiscal-Policy-Review.pdf.pdf` ¶675-676); the 2026 Budget expects construction on the Accra-Tema Motorway transformation project (PPP) to start in 2026 (`https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2026-Budget-Statement-and-Economic-Policy.pdf` ¶518) (2026-10-29 verified). Peak congestion eastbound AM, westbound PM.
- **Kumasi outer ring** (Kejetia, Tech, Asokwa-Atonsu corridor): chronic peak congestion.
- **Bus Rapid Transit (Aayalolo)**: Accra Amasaman-CBD corridor (Greater Accra Passenger Transport Executive — modest network coverage; 2016 start not re-verified). The 2026 Budget says the fleet "resumed full operation in Greater Accra, carrying over 10 million passengers between January and September 2025", and plans to expand the BRT network in 2026 (`https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2026-Budget-Statement-and-Economic-Policy.pdf` ¶545-546; 2026-10-29 verified).
- **Site visit during peak hours** (Mon-Fri 6:30-9am, 4:30-7pm) is the most reliable signal for parcel-level traffic exposure.

### Confidence

LOW — per-segment AADT comparable to EU/UK datasets is **not publicly available** for Ghana. Use OSM `highway` class + qualitative congestion knowledge as coarse proxy; for high-stakes purchases, peak-hour site visit is mandatory.

### Tax on realisation gains (seller, on sale)

Per Income Tax Act 2015 Act 896 + amendments:
- **Rate**: resident individual — graduated income-tax rates, or 25 % flat by election; non-resident individual — 25 % flat (Act 896 First Schedule para 1(2)–(3), substituted by Act 1094 of 2023); GRA's CGT page still states 15 % for individuals, so sources disagree and the statute is used here (2026-10-29 verified). Company — part of business income at 25 % CIT
- **Principal-residence exemption**: none appears in GRA's income-tax exemptions list (read 2026-10-29); confirm with GRA before relying on one
- **Non-resident sellers**: a non-resident individual's chargeable income is taxed at 25 % (Act 1094); a resident buyer other than an individual must withhold **10 % of the consideration** (3 % for a resident seller) under s.116A + First Schedule para 8; the seller files a realisation return within 30 days (s.39A) (2026-10-29 verified)

### Income tax on rental income

- **Resident individual**: rent is taxed separately at a final **8 % (residential) / 15 % (commercial and non-residential)**, paid within 30 days of receipt (GRA rent-tax page); other income at graduated 0–35 % (35 % above GH₵600,000 a year under the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026, Act 1178, from 1 Sep 2026) (2026-10-29 verified)
- **Non-resident landlord**: **15 % final withholding** on rent (GRA withholding table for non-resident persons: 'Royalties, natural resources payments and rents 15'; payments to non-residents are final unless derived through a Ghanaian permanent establishment) (2026-10-29 verified)
- **Corporate landlord**: 25 % standard CIT (GRA); GRA lists a tax holiday for income of a certified low-cost housing company. A 'first-purchase buyer concession through GREDA-registered developers' is not on any GRA page read 2026-10-29 — unverified

### VAT on real estate

- **Residential sale by a non-developer** (incl. an individual selling a house): **exempt** (Act 1151 First Schedule item 16; 2026-10-29 verified)
- **Any sale by an estate developer** (anyone in the business of construction or renovation and supply of immovable property) and **commercial lettings**: 15 % VAT + 5 % NHIL/GETFund = **20 %**; developers moved from the 5 % flat rate (+1 % COVID levy) to the standard rate on 1 Jan 2026; furnished Airbnb-like dwelling lets count as a commercial rental establishment (GRA/AG/26/001, 22 Jul 2026; 2026-10-29 verified)
- **VAT (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 2022 (Act 1087**, assented 29 Dec 2022) raised the standard rate from 12.5% to 15%; **superseded by VAT Act 2025 (Act 1151) effective 1 Jan 2026** — 15 % retained but VFRS abolished, goods threshold raised to GH₵750k (no threshold for services), NHIL+GETFund recoupled as input-creditable, COVID levy repealed (2026-10-29 verified).

### Inheritance / estate

- **No inheritance or estate tax**; an inheritance is outside gift tax, which excludes gifts 'under a will, upon intestacy or by way of transfer to the spouse, child or parent' (GRA). Other gifts: 15 % (resident) / 25 % (non-resident) per GRA's gift-tax page, though Act 1094 sets the elective individual rate at 25 %, so sources disagree (2026-10-29 verified)
- **Probate / Letters of Administration** at High Court Probate Registry; Administration of Estates Act 1961 (Act 63); fees vary
- **Customary inheritance**: matrilineal (Akan ethnic groups) vs patrilineal (Ewe, Ga, Northern groups) — Intestate Succession Act 1985 (PNDCL 111) provides statutory overlay; consult lawyer for testamentary planning under customary land

### Future risk

Tax-revenue intensification (the E-Levy itself was repealed 2 Apr 2025) carried over from the 2023-2026 IMF ECF (programme concluded 27 Jul 2026; fiscal-consolidation direction now anchored by the successor Policy Coordination Instrument rather than by programme conditionality); MMDA property-rate digitization may surface previously unbilled parcels; developer sales already moved to 20 % VAT + levies on 1 Jan 2026; the 2026 Budget carries no property-specific tax measure but commits to rewriting the Income Tax Act (Act 896), with a draft bill due with the 2027 Budget, so watch rent and realisation-gain rates (`https://mofep.gov.gh/publications/budget-statements`; 2026-10-29 verified).

**Confidence**: HIGH for the national framework (Stamp Duty Act 2005, Income Tax Act 2015 Act 896 as amended to Act 1178 of 2026, VAT Act 2025 Act 1151 — all GRA-administered); MEDIUM for the individual realisation-gain rate (GRA page 15 % vs Act 1094 text 25 %); MEDIUM for MMDA-level property rate (district-variable, no national schedule); Lands Commission fees from its published schedule (verify per transaction).

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- **Governing law**: Rent Act 1963 (Act 220, assented 12 Dec 1963; applies to all premises in Ghana subject to listed exclusions) + Rent Control regulations + customary practice; no repeal shown in the judy.legal record on 2026-10-29. The Real Estate Agency Act, 2020 (Act 1047) also applies to real-estate transactions 'including a tenancy for a term of not less than one year'
- **Rent Control Department** under the Ministry of Works, Housing and Water Resources (`https://mwhwr.gov.gh/`; the old `mwh.gov.gh` now serves a host placeholder page, checked 2026-10-29; Rent Control portal `https://rentcontrol.mwh.gov.gh/`) — administers tenancy disputes
- **Annual rent-in-advance culture**: most Ghanaian residential leases require **1–2 years rent in advance** (legacy practice, est.; informally enforced beyond Rent Act limits in many premium markets). Rent Act 1963 in principle limits advance rent; the Act text was not openly readable on 2026-10-29, so the limit is not re-verified; verify with Rent Control. Government counter-schemes: the **National Rental Assistance Scheme** pays the advance and the tenant repays monthly (`https://nras.gov.gh/`), and an NHF **'Easy Rent'** programme was announced on 7 Oct 2026 (MWHWR).
- **Notice**: 6 months for yearly tenancies (per Rent Act 1963; not re-verified 2026-10-29, Act text behind a login); **eviction** through Rent Control + District Court (multi-month)
- **Deposit**: 1-2 months typical; no statutory escrow framework

### Tax on rental income

- **Resident individual landlord**: final **8 %** rent tax on residential rent (**15 %** commercial / non-residential), due within 30 days of receipt; residential lettings are VAT-exempt (GRA rent-tax page; GRA/AG/26/001 Illustration 2; 2026-10-29 verified)
- **Non-resident landlord**: **15 %** final withholding on rent (GRA withholding table for non-resident persons; final unless derived through a Ghanaian permanent establishment); a resident payer withholds and files within 15 days after month-end, but the duty does not cover an individual paying rent other than in conducting a business (GRA; 2026-10-29 verified)
- **Deductions**: an individual's 8 % / 15 % rent tax is a final tax, which leaves no room for expense deductions (inference from GRA's final-withholding list; confirm with GRA); a company landlord deducts maintenance, repairs (not capital improvements), management commission and mortgage interest against business income

### Short-term rentals (STR / Airbnb)

**Status**: undergoverned at national level; growing market in Accra (Cantonments, Airport, East Legon, Labone) + Cape Coast / Elmina heritage tourism corridor + Kumasi premium.

### Stool-land + customary tenure overlay — the dominant due-diligence trap

| Tenure type | Who allocates | DD path |
|---|---|---|
| **Stool land** (south, Akan / Ga / Ewe regions) | Stool (chief) in trust for the community; OASL collects ground rent | Verify Allocation Note from Stool + customary witnessing → Indenture/Lease drafted by lawyer → Lands Commission Concurrence → registration |
| **Skin land** (northern regions, parallel to stool) | Skin (Tindana / Earth Priest / Chief) | Same chain as stool, regional variants |
| **Family / clan land** | Family head + principal members | Allocation Note signed by family head + principal elders → Indenture → Concurrence → registration |
| **State / public land** | State, via Lands Commission / Public & Vested Lands Division | Direct lease from State; cleanest title chain |
| **Private freehold/leasehold** | Existing private owner | Indenture + chain of title check at Lands Commission |

**Critical fraud vector — double allocation**: a chief or family head sells the same parcel to multiple buyers; the buyer with first registered Indenture + Lands Commission concurrence prevails, but ahead of registration the buyer is exposed. Mitigation: parallel official Lands Commission search + Site Plan registered before payment + payment via lawyer escrow + community elders' on-site witnessing + physical site inspection.

### Projections (GMet Ghana Climate Atlas + IPCC AR6)

- **Warming**: GMet projects average annual temperature to rise by **1.5 °C (range 1.2–1.9) by 2081–2100 under RCP4.5**, from a 1991–2020 mean of 27.8 °C to about 29.3 °C, with the "most increase during the dry season", and by 3.7 °C (2.7–4.4) under its high-emission scenario (Ghana Climate Atlas; 2026-10-29 verified). - **Heat days**: Northern Ghana +20-40 additional days >40°C/year by 2050 (unverified — not in the GMet Climate Atlas summary; est.)
- **Rainfall**: GMet projects total annual rainfall "will likely increase by 1.8%" (Jul–Sep +13.9 %, Sep–Nov +3.6 %, Apr–Jun −1.7 %) and says Ghana already sees "a growing number of extreme events such as floods, droughts, and coastal erosion" → urban flood implications (Accra Odaw catchment chronic) (Ghana Climate Atlas; 2026-10-29 verified)

## Section: `--visa`

### Authority + legal basis

- **Ghana Immigration Service (GIS)** — `https://www.gis.gov.gh/`
- **Ministry of the Interior** — `https://mint.gov.gh/`
- **Ghana Investment Promotion Authority (GIPA)** — `https://gipa.gov.gh/` (the old `gipc.gov.gh` redirects) — successor to GIPC under the GIPA Act, 2026 (Act 1173, assented and in force 15 Jul 2026); investor registration + expatriate quotas
- Legal basis: **Immigration Act 2000 (Act 573)**, amended by the Immigration (Amendment) Act 2012 (Act 848), + Immigration Regulations + Citizenship Act 2000 (Act 591) + Constitution 1992 (Art. 8 dual citizenship); a 'Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2017' was not confirmed on a page read 2026-10-29

### Major pathways

1. **Tourist + Business visa**: visas issued at Ghana's 56 missions cover single or multiple journeys, valid 6 months to 5 years (GIS); permitted stay per entry est. 30–90 days, set at the port (verify); not for working/residing; GIS runs an eVisa portal (`https://evisa.immigration.gov.gh/`) plus visas on arrival and emergency entry visas; ECOWAS nationals and listed African states are visa-exempt (GIS visas page, 2026-10-29).
2. **Work Permit + Resident Permit**: tied to employer + role; 1-2 yr renewable; sponsor company application via GIS.
3. **Investor pathway via GIPA** (Ghana Investment Promotion Authority Act, 2026, Act 1173, which repealed the GIPC Act 2013, Act 865, on 15 Jul 2026):
    - **No minimum foreign capital** for wholly foreign-owned or joint-venture non-trading enterprises (the old USD 200,000 / 500,000 floors are gone); a **trading** enterprise needs at least **USD 500,000** cash equity and at least 75 % skilled Ghanaian staff (s.31); registration is renewed every year (GIPA press release 22 Jul 2026 + Act 1173 text; 2026-10-29 verified).
    - Expatriate quota by invested capital (s.49): USD 50,000–500,000 → 2 persons; to 1M → 4; to 3M → 6; to 6M → 8; to 10M → 10; above 10M → 12; quota valid 5 years, renewable.
    - GIS issues the work and residence permit on the back of GIPA registration (GIS service pages still say 'GIPC').
4. **Right of Abode (Akwaaba framework)**: GIS grants it to (a) a former Ghanaian citizen who lost citizenship by taking a foreign nationality and (b) 'a person of African descent in the Diaspora'; the Minister grants it on application with the President's approval; holders may reside permanently, enter without a visa and work without a work permit (GIS). Statutory basis Immigration Act 2000 (Act 573) ss. 17-18 as amended (section numbers not re-read). Does NOT confer Ghanaian citizenship; does NOT amend the Art. 266 freehold restriction. Diaspora applicants must show **contribution to Ghana's economic development** (shares, bank statements, audited accounts, Ghanaian employees), company documents with 3 years' audited accounts, personal and company tax clearance for 6 years, a Ghana police report and a GIS medical report (GIS requirements, read 2026-10-29). Fees and family-inclusion rules: verify at GIS.
5. **Indefinite Residence Status** (GIS's term for permanent residence): lets a foreign national remain indefinitely, enter without a visa and work without a work permit; requirements include a sponsor letter, two attestations, evidence of immovable property where applicable, company documents with 3 years' audited accounts and 6 years' tax clearance, and a Ghana police report (GIS, 2026-10-29). GIS's page states no minimum residence period, so the '7+ years' rule is unverified.
6. **Citizenship by Naturalisation** (Citizenship Act 2000 Act 591): residence est. 7+ years + language + good character + means + integration evidence (Act text not openly readable 2026-10-29; verify). Ministerial discretion. **Dual citizenship** allowed: 'A citizen of Ghana may hold the citizenship of any other country' (Constitution Art. 8, as revised 1996); a foreigner naturalising may still have to renounce under their original country's rules.
7. **Citizenship by Registration / Descent**: through Ghanaian parentage (Constitution 1992 Art. 6-8); **Pan-African Identity Card / "Year of Return" framework** has been promoted as a diaspora-engagement program. Specific PAID issuance + benefits + connection to property rights vary by program iteration; verify at GIS / Ministry of the Interior + Ministry of Foreign Affairs (`https://www.mfa.gov.gh/`).

### NO golden-visa via passive real estate

Ghana does NOT offer a residency-by-property-purchase scheme (vs Portugal Golden Visa pre-Oct 2023, Greece Golden Visa, etc.). Real-estate purchase alone gives NO residence right. The closest analog is the **GIPA investor route** (no minimum capital for non-trading foreign or joint-venture enterprises since Act 1173; expatriate quota from USD 50,000 invested), which can include a real-estate development company run as a going concern, not a passive-asset SPV. **Citizenship by investment**: Act 1173 s.39 directs the Ministry of the Interior to bring legislation on it; no such legislation found on GIPA or GIS pages read 2026-10-29.

### Diaspora pathway

- **Year of Return 2019** + **Beyond the Return 2020-onwards**: Ministry of Tourism / Ghana Tourism Authority diaspora-engagement framework; coordinates visits, investment introductions, citizenship ceremonies for diaspora applicants meeting Citizenship Act criteria.
- **Pan-African Identity Card (PAID)**: ID card framework promoted as part of African-Union Free Movement / diaspora-connection initiative; **verify current PAID issuance status, benefits, and any property-rights interaction at Ministry of Foreign Affairs / GIS — has been subject to multiple program iterations + delays**.
- **Diaspora Affairs Bureau** (Office of the President / Ministry of Foreign Affairs Diaspora unit) — coordination point for diaspora investment + return.

**Confidence**: HIGH for Immigration Act 2000 + Citizenship Act 2000 + GIPA Act 2026 (Act 1173) framework; MEDIUM for current eligibility thresholds (verify directly at GIS as recent program iterations evolve); LOW for specific Akwaaba / PAID procedural detail (secondary aggregators frequently oversimplify or conflate distinct programs — primary GIS / Ministry of Interior verification mandatory).

## Section: `--notary`

### Framework

Ghana operates **English common-law conveyancing**, not civil-law notariat:
- **Lawyer (GBA-registered, holding solicitor's licence)** drafts Indenture / Lease + due diligence on title chain
- **Commissioner for Oaths / Notary Public** administers oaths + authenticates (NOT the conveyancer)
- **Lands Commission** issues Concurrence + registers Indenture / Title
- **High Court Probate Registry** for probate (Administration of Estates Act 1961 Act 63)

### Process — Accra foreign-buyer typical flow

1. Letter of Offer / Sale Agreement + refundable deposit (5-15%); review by buyer's lawyer
2. Buyer lawyer due diligence: title search at Lands Commission Land Registration Division; chain of Indentures; Site Plan + boundary check; Stool / family allocation note review (if customary chain)
3. **Independent official Lands Commission search** — consolidated search GH₵237.50 per 0.5 ha in Greater Accra, GH₵132.50 official search in most other regions, land-certificate search GH₵163 (Lands Commission schedule, read 2026-10-29) — surfaces double-allocation + encumbrances
4. Site Plan re-survey by licensed surveyor (verify boundaries on the ground)
5. Indenture / Lease drafted by buyer lawyer + reviewed by seller lawyer
6. **Stamp Duty assessment + payment**: value assessed by the Lands Commission Land Valuation Division, duty paid to GRA: 1 % of consideration for any price above GH₵50,000 (0.25 % / 0.5 % bands only below that; Act 689 Sched. 1)
7. **Lands Commission Concurrence application** (stool land: Constitution Art. 267(3) requires Regional Lands Commission certification) — flat GH₵274 + inspection GH₵60/100 per 0.5 ha (Lands Commission schedule, read 2026-10-29)
8. **Land Title / Deed Registration** — definitive title in Title Registration districts (Accra, Kumasi pilots); evidentiary in Deed Registration districts; fees mostly flat (title first registration GH₵1,148–1,178 per acre; deed registration with consent GH₵603 + 0.13 % of land value, residential) plus 0.25 % plotting above GH₵48,000 land value; deed registration needs a GRA tax clearance certificate (Lands Commission, read 2026-10-29)
9. Final payment + handover; Title Certificate or Registered Deed issued in buyer's name
10. Property Rate transfer at MMDA; utility transfers (ECG/NEDCo, GWCL)

### Timeline

### Trampas

- **Double allocation by stool / family head**: same parcel sold to multiple buyers; mitigate via Lands Commission search + first-registered Indenture wins + payment via lawyer escrow

## Section: `--exit`

### Sale process (Accra East Legon house, USD 350,000)

1. Engage GBA-registered lawyer + agent (~5-10% commission typical, often seller-borne)
2. Listing on meQasa.com + Property24 + private agent network; **6-18 months** typical Accra premium depending on price + currency stance
3. Pre-sale docs: original Indenture / Lease + Site Plan + Lands Commission Concurrence + Title Certificate (or Registered Deed) + chain-of-title docs + property rate clearance + estate dues + utility clearances + GhanaCard
4. Buyer offer + Letter of Acceptance + deposit (5-15% refundable)
5. Buyer due diligence — own lawyer + Lands Commission search + surveyor
6. Stamp Duty + Lands Commission Concurrence + Registration (Accra: 3-9 months on Concurrence + Registration)
7. Realisation-gain tax settled at GRA (seller): return within 30 days; resident individuals at graduated rates or 25 % flat by election, non-resident individuals at 25 % (Act 1094; GRA's CGT page still says 15 %); a resident corporate buyer withholds 3 % (10 % for a non-resident seller) of the price
8. Final payment + Indenture / Lease assignment + handover
9. Title / Deed updated at Lands Commission

### Seller tax obligations

- **Realisation-gain tax**: graduated rates or 25 % elective flat rate for resident individuals, 25 % for non-resident individuals (Act 896 as amended by Act 1094; GRA's CGT page says 15 %, so sources disagree); no private-residence exemption on GRA's income-tax exemptions page (read 2026-10-29)
- **No national property-tax exit penalty** — settle outstanding MMDA property rate
- Lawyer fees ~1-2% seller; agent commission ~5-10% (often shared)
- Stamp Duty + Concurrence Fee are buyer-side (no seller transfer tax beyond CGT)

### Repatriation

- **BoG-stamped inbound advice + authorized-dealer SWIFT confirmation at original purchase is critical** — without it, USD repatriation structurally restricted
- AML: banks report cash transactions of GH₵50,000 or more and every transfer above US$1,000 to the FIC (AML Act 2020, Act 1044); legal but documented
- Conversion at BoG-authorized dealer rate; spread typically 1-3%
- Outbound transfer via authorized dealer: documentation-heavy but legal for documented inflows

### Liquidity

- Accra premium (Cantonments, Airport Residential, East Legon, Trasacco, Roman Ridge): 6-18 months for fairly priced in stable currency stance
- **GHS depreciation freezes market** (USD-sellers wait, GHS-buyers flee) — 2022-23 crisis saw sharp slowdown
- Off-plan resale: thin secondary market
- Other regions: 12-36 months not uncommon for premium

### Seller cost (Accra, typical)

Agent 5-10% (often seller-borne) + realisation-gain tax (25 % elective or graduated, Act 1094) + lawyer 1-2% + property rate / utility clearances minor → net to seller ~75-92% of gross depending on agent-cost split + CGT applicability.

**Confidence**: HIGH for the Stamp Duty Act; MEDIUM for the realisation-gain rate (GRA page 15 % vs Act 1094 25 %); MEDIUM for Accra closing-timeline (Concurrence variable per district); LOW for second-tier region liquidity.

## Caveats unique to GH (HEAVY — read all)

- **1992 Constitution Art. 266**: NO freehold for non-Ghanaians; **max 50 years leasehold renewable** — foundation of every foreign-buyer Ghana property transaction.
- **Customary tenure (~78% of land)**: stool / skin / family / clan land overlays dominate outside major formally-allocated zones; **Allocation Note → Indenture → Lands Commission Concurrence → Registration** is the chain — skipping any step is the dominant fraud vector.
- **Double-allocation fraud**: same parcel sold to multiple buyers by chief / family head; first-registered Indenture + Concurrence prevails — pre-payment Lands Commission search + lawyer escrow + on-site witnessing mandatory.
- **Parallel registration systems** (Title vs Deed) during transition — Accra + Kumasi advanced on Title; many districts still on Deed registration, now processed under the Land Act, 2020 (Act 1036) (Lands Commission notices, Oct 2026).
- **GHS volatility**: BoG inter-bank end-month GHS 6.02 → 13.10/USD in 2022, 16.30 at end-Oct 2024, 10.28 at end-May 2025; 11.6258 on 28 Oct 2026; **USD informal pricing dominates prime Accra suburbs** (est., listing observation).

- **Akwaaba / Right of Abode + PAID**: African-descent diaspora pathway (Immigration Act 2000 ss. 17-18 as amended Act 848 2012); does NOT confer freehold rights despite secondary-aggregator language often equating it; verify directly at GIS.
- **No golden-visa via passive RE**: the GIPA route (Act 1173) has no minimum capital for non-trading foreign enterprises (USD 500,000 for trading) and needs a registered going concern; citizenship-by-investment legislation is mandated (s.39) but none was found enacted on 2026-10-29.
- **BoG capital-flow documentation**: foreign capital inflow MUST be authorized-dealer-bank-documented for future USD repatriation — #1 procedural risk.
- **Energy / water autonomy** standard cost overlay (borehole + polytank + generator + LPG + increasingly solar): est. GHS 1,500-4,500/mo (no primary price source) for mid-tier Accra premium home.
- **Accra urban floods** (Odaw catchment — Kaneshie, Circle, Alajo, Achimota, Adenta corridors): chronic during the main rains (e.g. 29 Jun 2026 — NADMO, PURC); verify drainage + insurance flood wording.
- **Coastal erosion**: West African shoreline retreat 1-3 m/yr in places (rate unverified; Keta, Ada Foah, Aboadze) — verify EPA Ghana coastal report + Climate Central for parcels < 200 m from shore.
- **GhanaCard + TIN integration**: required for property + bank transactions.
- **MMDA property rate** (0.5-3% assessed value range, est.; AMA / KMA digitization underway via ghana.gov).
- **Stool succession + family-elders disputes**: surface customary chain disputes pre-purchase via Houses of Chiefs + family elders + lawyer.
- **State-acquisition overlay**: certain parcels under historical state acquisition with compensation history disputes; verify Lands Commission Public & Vested Lands.
- **Galamsey (illegal small-scale mining) overlay**: certain inland regions affected by water pollution + legal challenges to mineral-bearing surface lands.
