# Malta (MT) — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

**Status**: ✅ fully populated as of 2026-04-26
**Currency**: EUR (eurozone since 2008)
**Lang**: English + Maltese (both official); both used in legal/notarial work

---

## Country profile

Malta is the smallest EU state (~588k pop — 588,254 at end-2025, 2025 data, source NSO via MaltaToday 9 Jul 2026), archipelago (Malta + Gozo + Comino), limestone-built, no central heating tradition. **MEIN/IIP citizenship-by-investment ENDED Apr 2025** (ECJ Case C-181/23, 29 April 2025) — replaced by ministerial-discretion "Citizenship by Merit" (Act XXI 2025 + LN 159 of 2025). MPRP residence-by-investment continues. **Land Registration was historically NOT compulsory** — hybrid Public Registry (deeds 1863) + Land Registry (title 1982); the National Strategy for the Reform of the Land Registration System 2025–2035 targets compulsory registration of all territory by **2035** (a strategy target, not a statutory deadline; 54% of territory designated a compulsory registration area — 2024 data, source Lands Minister via BusinessNow 25 Nov 2024 — rates/figures may have changed since). Critical 2025-26 reforms: stamp duty inheritance threshold raised to €400k (28 Oct 2025); UCA/vacant 0% duty + 0% transfer-tax on first €750k (1 Jan 2025–31 Dec 2026); STR Tourism Accommodation Regulations 2026 (LN 92 of 2026, S.L. 409.24, in force 15 Jun 2026) + 3-yr licensing ban on unlicensed operators that also attaches to the property.

## Section: `--tax`

### Stamp Duty (= primary purchase tax in Malta)

- **Standard rate**: **5%** of higher of price/market value. The promise of sale (konvenju) is registered with the MTCA within 21 days with 1% provisional duty (20% of the final duty); the 4% balance is paid on the final deed (2026 data, source MTCA Buying Property / Promise of Sale pages)
- **First-time buyer (FTB) exemption**: **0% on first €200,000**, then 5% on excess. Open to a buyer who does not need an AIP permit (DDTA Cap. 364 art. 32(4)(a)), acquiring a sole ordinary residence, with no previous inter vivos acquisition of residential property (an undivided share under 25% is disregarded); for transfers from 28 Oct 2025 the order sets no end date (2025 data, source LN 305 of 2025 amending S.L. 364.12 art. 3). Plus Housing Authority grant of €10,000 paid €1,000/yr over 10 years (first property acquired after 1 Jan 2022; €20,000 for EU Disability Card holders buying after 1 Jan 2026; also open to second-time buyers who own no other residence and buy after 1 Jan 2026) (2026 data, source Housing Authority First-Time Buyer Scheme page)
- **UCA / vacant property** (transfers 12 Oct 2021–31 Dec 2026, notice to the Commissioner by 31 Jan 2027): **0% stamp duty AND 0% transfer tax on first €750,000**. "Vacant" = building completed ≥20 years before the transfer AND vacant ≥7 continuous years (ARMS report + architect's report). **New builds developed to approved traditional-Maltese criteria**: duty only, refunded after approval — no transfer-tax relief. Not available to a buyer who needs an AIP permit (or would need one outside an SDA); relief is clawed back if the property is demolished or subdivided (2025 data, source S.L. 123.203 rules 3–8 as amended by LN 284 of 2025). Budget 2027 speech (Oct 2026) announced that all 2026 schemes will be renewed for 2027 (per Belair, secondary); no amending legal notice found as of 2026-10-23
- **First-time buyer + UCA / vacant / traditional-criteria property**: the S.L. 123.203 relief already covers duty on the first €750,000; the buyer still files proof of no earlier inter vivos acquisition with the Commissioner by 31 Jan 2027 (rule 13). Ministry for Finance purchase grant on these property types: **€15,000 (Malta) / €40,000 (Gozo)**, deed signed 1 Jan 2025–31 Dec 2026 (2026 data, source finance.gov.mt Grants page)
- **Inherited dwelling** occupied by the heir as ordinary residence: reduced 3.5% on first **€400,000** (raised from €200k effective **28 Oct 2025**); where it was also the deceased's ordinary residence, the first €35,000 stays exempt and 3.5% applies to €35,001–€400,000; to a surviving spouse/cohabitant: no duty at the time of transfer (2026 data, sources PwC Malta tax summary reviewed 30 Sep 2026; KPMG Malta, Feb 2026; DDTA Cap. 364)
- **Gozo**: the 2% duty rate on Gozo residential property (S.L. 364.12 art. 4) covered only final deeds made by 31 Jan 2024 or promises of sale notified by that date — lapsed; standard rules now apply, with the €40,000 Gozo grant above for qualifying UCA / vacant / traditional-criteria purchases (2025 data, source S.L. 364.12 consolidated text)

**CORRECTION to common assumption**: **No "12% non-resident transfer tax" or "5% with permit"** exists. Standard 5% applies equally to non-residents. What's specific to non-EU/non-resident is the **AIP permit + minimum-price thresholds**, not a different tax rate — but a buyer who needs an AIP permit is excluded from the FTB €200,000 exemption and the UCA / vacant €750,000 relief (DDTA art. 32(4)(a) proviso; S.L. 123.203 rule 3).

### Property Transfer Tax (= "CGT" replacement on sale)

- **Final-withholding regime since 1 Jan 2015**. **Standard 8%** on **transfer value (higher of price and market value, NOT gain)**. Brokerage fees paid by the seller are deductible; no other deductions, no taper (2025 data, source MTCA New Property Tax System FAQ, last updated 29 Aug 2025)
- **5%** if sold **within** 5 years of acquisition and not part of a project (since 20 Mar 2020 not available where works needing development permission were carried out in those 5 years, unless acquired as sole ordinary residence); **10%** if acquired before 1 Jan 2004; **2%** if sold within 3 years of an acquisition declared on the deed as sole ordinary residence and the seller owns no other residential property; **5%** on UCA / scheduled property restored under a PA permit applied for on or after 1 Jan 2015 (2025 data, source MTCA New Property Tax System FAQ)
- **Inherited after 24 Nov 1992**: seller may elect 12% on the excess of transfer value over the value declared on inheritance (denunzja) OR final withholding 10/8/5% by date of inheritance; **inherited before 25 Nov 1992**: 7% final on transfer value (2025 data, source MTCA New Property Tax System FAQ)
- **Exempt**: own residence owned and occupied ≥3 consecutive years immediately before the transfer and sold within 12 months of vacating (Income Tax Act art. 5A(4)) (2025 data, source MTCA New Property Tax System FAQ)
- **Gain-basis (35%-era) route** ended 1 Jan 2015 and is NOT a routine alternative. Elections out of the final tax survive only for co-owner-to-co-owner transfers, compulsory acquisition by Government, business property replaced within one year, and project sales by companies with listed bonds; a non-resident taxed on the gain in their country of residence may opt for art. 5 capital-gains treatment, with the 7% provisional tax non-refundable (2025 data, source MTCA New Property Tax System FAQ)

### Annual Property Tax
**None.** Malta has no recurring municipal/state property tax on residential — MTCA: "The legal system of Malta does not impose a tax on ownership of property" (2025 data, source MTCA New Property Tax System FAQ no. 20). (Annual fees exist only for ground-rent / temporary emphyteusis under old contracts.)

### Rental Income

- **Option A — TA24 final 15%** flat on **gross rent** (no deductions), residential or commercial; 10% final on gross residential rent from property restored under a MEPA scheme; a registered long private residential lease (≥2 years) taxed at 15% may also earn a rebate (2026 data, source PwC Malta tax summary reviewed 30 Sep 2026). Election made annually via TA24. Most landlords use this
- **Option B — Standard progressive** on net rent (deductions allowed) — only attractive at very low income or high expenses

### VAT

- **Transfer of immovable property = exempt without credit (VAT Act Cap. 406, Fifth Schedule Part Two item 1(2))**. **No VAT on resale OR new build to end-buyer.** Developers cannot reclaim input VAT (2026 data, source Cap. 406 consolidated text)
- Long-term residential lease also exempt (Fifth Schedule Part Two item 1(1)); **accommodation in premises that must be licensed under the Malta Travel and Tourism Services Act (MTA) is 7%** (Eighth Schedule item 1) (2026 data, source Cap. 406 consolidated text)
- **Restoration "VAT refund"** = Grant on the Restoration and Finishing of Privately Owned Residential Properties: **18% and/or 10% of restoration and finishing costs, max €54,000 per property**, one application per property, 1 Jan 2025–31 Dec 2026 (2026 data, source Ministry for Finance Grants page); at 18% the cap is reached at €300,000 of costs (computed: 18% × €300,000 = €54,000). Eligible property types (UCA / 20-year-old vacant) per secondary guides — confirm in the scheme document

## Section: `--rental`

### STR / Airbnb

- **Mandatory licence** from **Malta Tourism Authority (MTA)**: the **Short-Let Rented Accommodation** licence (the Holiday Furnished Premises category until 15 Jun 2026); operating a short let without prior MTA authorisation is expressly prohibited. https://mta.com.mt/licenses-applications/short-let-rented-accommodation/ — licence check: https://validator.mta.com.mt/
- **Licensed stock**: **8,748 active short-let licences** as of Sep 2026, up from 7,116 in 2025 (2026 data, source data tabled in Parliament by the Tourism Minister, via Amphora Media 15 Oct 2026 — secondary reporting of primary data). Unlicensed share: **~1 in 5** Airbnb listings in Oct 2025 (9,376 Inside Airbnb listings; Gżira 46%, Sliema 30%, St Paul's Bay 28%); Oct 2026 estimate 1,294–4,508 unlicensed listings, depending on whether only entire units are counted (Amphora Media, Oct 2025 and Oct 2026 — investigative media, secondary)
- **Requirements** (S.L. 409.24, Category E): licence issued only to the proprietor in title (owner, mandated agent, lessee, emphyteuta or usufructuary); valid Planning Authority development permission (or Pre-1968 Compliance Certificate) plus an architect's certificate of conformity and habitability; valid insurance also covering common parts (earlier guidance cited ≥€250,000 third-party liability — not in the S.L. 409.24 text; verify the current minimum with MTA); Sixth Schedule standards; waste collection management plan; a named natural person reachable 24/7; exterior sign with licence number and 24/7 contact; written notice to the condominium administrator once the licence is issued (a notification, not a consent — check the condominium regulations and title for use restrictions). MTA timeframes: vetting 5 days, approval 60 days (MTA, 2026)
- **2026 reform — Tourism Accommodation Regulations 2026** (LN 92 of 2026, S.L. 409.24; published 15 Apr 2026 after a Nov 2025 public consultation, in force 15 Jun 2026 with some standards phased in; consolidates four earlier accommodation regulations):
  - **3-year ban**: a person found operating a short let without a licence is disqualified from applying for 3 years, and the dwelling itself is ineligible for any short-let licence for the same period, whoever applies — check the MTA licence register before buying a former holiday let
  - **Operating conditions**: max 2 persons per PA-approved bedroom and 10 per unit (unless the unit has independent road access); no basement or underground bedrooms; individual bookings capped at **90 consecutive days**; licences can be suspended or withdrawn for noise, waste or public-health breaches
  - **Buyer flag**: minimum bedroom sizes (9 m² for one person, +4 m² per extra person, min internal width 2.75 m — Sixth Schedule Standard 2.1) apply only where the contract of sale was signed after the regulations came into force, unless the promise of sale predates it; existing licensed properties have two years to install air conditioning (MTA circular, 2026)
  - MTA may set **locality-specific licensing criteria** (published in the Gazette and on the MTA website), weighs the locality's existing bed stock when deciding on new accommodation, and local-council bye-laws can add conditions; the regulations name no specific localities
  - EU Reg 2024/1028 (STR data-sharing) applies from **20 May 2026** (Art. 19); MTA is the competent authority, and each operator receives a **Registration Number**, distinct from the licence number, to be shown on every listing and on the exterior sign; the national implementing legal notice was still in draft per the MTA circular (2026)
- **Hotspot pushback**: Sliema had 1,779 Airbnb listings (1,307 entire units) as of Sep 2026, against 1,030 licensed short lets (2026 data, source Amphora Media 15 Oct 2026 — investigative media, secondary). Per-street and per-building counts of unlicensed lets: data not publicly available — verify a specific building with the MTA STR licence check https://validator.mta.com.mt/
- **Tax**: STR income → 15% final tax on gross rent via TA24 OR standard progressive rates on net; VAT 7% on accommodation in licensable premises, VAT registration required with no threshold (Art. 11 small undertakings under €35,000 turnover charge no VAT) (2026 data, source Airbnb Malta tax guide 2026 — platform-published, secondary)
- **Eco-contribution**: €1.50 per guest aged 18+ per night, capped at €22.50 per person per visit, since 1 Jul 2026 (MTA; Eco-Contribution Act Cap. 473 as amended by Act III of 2026). Budget 2027 (speech 26 Oct 2026) announced €10 per night capped at €80 for short lets (€8 / €64 for collective accommodation) from 1 Jan 2027 — announced, not yet enacted (Budget Speech 2027, finance.gov.mt; PwC Malta Budget 2027)

## Section: `--risks`

### Seismic
- ThinkHazard rates Malta **LOW** (2% chance of potentially damaging shaking in the next 50 years; SHARE-EU dataset — 2026 data, source ThinkHazard). **No active fault crosses islands directly**, BUT **Pantelleria/Sicily Channel Rift Zone (SCRZ)** runs ~80–150 km NW
- Historical felt earthquakes catalogued back to 1530; no deaths officially recorded, serious building damage several times (source: Galea 2007 catalogue, University of Malta, Annals of Geophysics 50(6)). Major events:
  - 11 Jan 1693 E. Sicily Mw 7.4 — EMS-98 intensity VII–VIII in Malta, the catalogue maximum; structural damage
  - 20 Feb 1743 Ionian Sea (M 6.3 in the paper's text, Mw 6.9 in its CPTI-2004 table) — intensity VII; heavy damage to Mdina Cathedral, St John's (Valletta) and Gozo churches
  - 1886: Sicily Channel swarm (16 shocks felt in Aug 1886) and 27 Aug 1886 Aegean Sea Mw 7.3 (intensity VI–VII, damage in Valletta and Mdina); 30 Sep 1911 Sicily Channel (intensity VII); 2003 Sicily (not in the catalogue table, which ends 1972 — verify with the Malta Seismic Network)
- Modern seismic risk = "low-to-moderate from regional sources." Eurocode 8 + Malta's National Annex (MSA EN 1998-1) bind only government-funded and EU-funded projects; private developments are not currently required to apply them. Malta sits in the EC8 **low**-seismicity band (SHARE PGA 0.05–0.075 g; <0.1 g and >0.04 g = simplified design provisions), while 2013 probabilistic studies gave 0.09–0.18 g for the 475-yr return period, which would read as moderate (2023 data, source dhi Periti technical note — secondary — rates/figures may have changed since)
- **Issue**: buildings have low intrinsic seismic resistance — amplification of distant events on soft fill/old construction

### Flood
- Inland flood risk LOW (no rivers, karstic drainage)
- **Localized flash-flood "valley" flooding** in wied (dry valleys) — Msida, Birkirkara, Qormi notorious. EWA's 2nd Flood Risk Management Plan names 11 catchments with Areas of Potentially Significant Flood Risk (APSFR): Birkirkara-Msida, Birżebbuġa, Burmarrad, Gżira, Kalkara, Marsascala, Qormi (Malta); Marsalforn, Mġarr ix-Xini, Ramla, Xlendi (Gozo) (2024 data, source EWA 2nd FRMP — rates/figures may have changed since)
- **Coastal storm surge**: EWA rates localised storm-surge / seiche flooding as of limited risk to urban areas; 5% of the land lies below 7.6 m and 1% at about 1 m altitude (2024 data, source EWA 2nd FRMP — rates/figures may have changed since). Earlier rating "moderate — Marsaxlokk, Marsascala, Sliema/Gżira fronts" (superseded)
- Source: EWA 2nd Flood Risk Management Plan for the Malta River Basin District (Feb 2024) + 2nd-cycle Flood Hazard and Flood Risk Maps (Dec 2020) — https://energywateragency.gov.mt/integrated-river-basin-management/

### Wildfire — essentially nil (limited vegetation)
- Vegetation fires occur in summer in Buskett/Mizieb but not threat to housing

### Water scarcity — SEVERE
- Among the most water-stressed countries in the EU (no significant surface freshwater; supply leans on desalination + over-abstracted groundwater — see blend below)
- WSC blend: **70.7% desalinated (four reverse-osmosis plants), 29.3% groundwater** of 39.5 million m³ potable water produced; RO:groundwater moved from 66:34 in 2024 to 71:29 in 2025, and groundwater abstraction (11.5 million m³) was the lowest in a decade (2025 data, source WSC Annual Report 2025). In 2017 the blend was 57% RO / 43% groundwater (14.4 of 33.3 million m³; 2017 data, source WSC Annual Report 2017)
- The Malta and Gozo mean-sea-level aquifers are in **poor quantitative status (over-abstracted)**; all groundwater bodies are in poor chemical status (mainly nitrate), and 11 also fail on chloride, an indicator of **saline intrusion** (2016–2021 assessment, source 3rd River Basin Management Plan, ERA/EWA, Mar 2024 — rates/figures may have changed since). Earlier claim "groundwater chloride exceeds 2,000 mg/L in coastal mean-sea-level aquifer" (superseded)
- Tap drinkable; WSC stabilised average tap salinity at ~270 mg/l chloride in 2025 after a ~30% reduction (2025 data, source WSC Annual Report 2025) — most households use bottled or filtered

### Subsidence/sinkholes
- Localized — limestone is karstic; isolated collapses (Naxxar 2010, Mosta) but not systemic

### Climate
- Sea-level rise relevant for low-lying coastal SDAs (Tigné, Smart City) over 30–50 yr horizon. Climate Central Risk Zone tool covers Malta

### Mandatory diagnostics

- **EPC** (transposes EPBD 2002/91/EC, 2010/31/EU): **compulsory for sale and rental**. Issued by certified assessor; valid 10 years residential. As-built EPC required for the BCA "Buy Sustainable Property Scheme 2026" grant (second edition: Maltese/EU buyers of a residential property bought Oct 2025–Sep 2026; €4,500 or €9,000 over 3 years by energy performance; the BCA scheme page now lists applications as closed — 2026 data, per BCA scheme page + Times of Malta, secondary). EPC scale A–G
- **No central heating** is the norm (mild winters) — EPC ratings emphasize cooling, ventilation, hot water, insulation
- **No equivalent of French "diagnostic" pack** — asbestos surveys not generally required pre-sale; due diligence is buyer-side
- **Planning permit verification** via Planning Authority (https://www.pa.org.mt/) eAppeals/eApplications — confirm PA permits for any extension/works (very common to find unpermitted alterations, especially in older Sliema/Valletta apartments)

## Section: `--mains`

### Cadastre / Land Registry — HYBRID + IN REFORM

- **Land Registration Agency** (LRA; `landregistry.gov.mt` and `landsregistry.mt` both 301-redirect here): https://lra.mt/ (updated 2026-10-23)
- **Public Registry (separate)** — handles deeds, marriage, birth; the notary searches both
- **CRITICAL**: Malta has hybrid system:
  - **Public Registry (deeds-based, since 1863)**
  - **Land Registry (title-based, since 1982)**
  - Mandatory registration only inside designated **Compulsory Registration Areas** — **54% of the territory**, registration voluntary in the other 46% (2024 data, Lands Minister at the strategy launch, per Times of Malta / TVM — secondary)
  - Outside these: registration was voluntary — title established by chain of notarial deeds
- **2025–2035 reform — National Strategy for the Reform of the Land Registration System 2025–2035**: public consultation launched 25 Nov 2024, closed 28 Feb 2025 (per BusinessNow, 25 Nov 2024 — secondary); target = **all of Malta + Gozo become a compulsory registration area by 2035**. No enacting instrument appears on the LRA legal-framework page, which lists only the Land Registration Act (Cap. 296), S.L. 296.01–296.13 and S.L. 595.31 (2026 data, source lra.mt) — verify the reform's legal status with the LRA. LN 150 of 2025 is the Protection of Agricultural Land Regulations under the Agriculture Act (Cap. 639), not a land-registration measure. The online Land Registration System (LRS, https://lrs.lra.mt) went live in Aug 2026 for site plans and Form E searches (2026 data, per Kamra tal-Periti circular CIR 06/26 of 10 Aug 2026). Until 2035, expect plots with no Land Registry title number; due diligence relies on the notary's chain-of-deeds search at Public Registry
- **Foreigner-accessible**: Yes — anyone may file Form E on any plot (with an architect-signed site plan; €30 official search fee, €6 per official site plan — 2026 data, source lra.mt FAQs); Land Registry only returns data on registered titles. For unregistered land you commission a notary to do Public Registry search (~€100–250 est. typical notary search fee — confirm with your notary)

### Utilities

- **Electricity**: **Enemalta** — https://enemalta.com.mt/ — single national operator. New service installation: €300 single-phase, €900 triple-phase (routes >150 m from supply and three-phase >60 A per phase are quoted individually); annual service charge €65 single-phase / €195 triple-phase (incl. 5% VAT) (2026 data, source Enemalta residential property services). Residential tariff **banded** on cumulative annual consumption, incl. 5% VAT: **€0.1047/kWh** for 0–2,000 kWh, €0.1298 (2,001–6,000), €0.1607 (6,001–10,000), €0.3420 (10,001–20,000), €0.6076 (20,001+), plus an eco-reduction of up to 25% at a primary residence below per-person thresholds (2026 data, source Enemalta). **Consumers MUST register actual residents on ARMS form to qualify for residential tariff** (otherwise the domestic tariff applies: €0.1365 / €0.1673 / €0.2023 / €0.4180 / €0.6860 per kWh across the same bands, with no eco-reduction = 13–30% higher per kWh, computed from the two Enemalta tables; 2026 data, source Enemalta domestic property services)
- **Malta–Sicily interconnector (IC1)**: 200 MW HVAC submarine cable, single circuit (Magħtab–Marina di Ragusa), in service since 2015. Net imports supplied **32.1% of 2025 electricity supply** (1,031.0 GWh imported via the interconnector of 3,207.1 GWh supplied; July 2025 peak demand 637 MW) (2025 data, source NSO NR 178/2026). **Second interconnector (IC2: ~122 km, 225 MW, 220 kV HVAC, parallel route) under construction**: submarine cable laying scheduled to start Q4 2026, commissioning planned from Q1 2027 (2026 data, source Interconnect Malta project page). **Single point of failure risk = major**
- **Water/Sewer**: **Water Services Corporation (WSC)** — single operator. Tariff banded similarly to electricity, ARMS-billed
- **Gas**: **No mains gas grid** in Malta — **bottled LPG only** (Liquigas Malta or Easygas). Cooking, sometimes water heating
- **Internet**: GO, Melita, Epic — fibre rollout near complete urban Malta; copper/4G in rural Gozo
- **Sewer connection**: mandatory in main areas, but **older Gozo and rural villages still on cesspits/soakaways** — verify per-property

## Active fiscal incentives (Oct 2026)

- **No annual property tax** — already a structural incentive
- **No VAT on residential** — every transfer of immovable property, new builds included, is VAT-exempt (VAT Act Fifth Schedule Part Two item 1(2))
- **FTB scheme**: 0% stamp duty on first €200k (no end date for transfers from 28 Oct 2025, LN 305 of 2025) + Housing Authority €10k grant (€1,000/yr × 10)
- **UCA / vacant**: 0% stamp duty + 0% transfer tax on first €750k (transfers 12 Oct 2021–31 Dec 2026, S.L. 123.203); **traditional-criteria new builds**: duty refund only on first €750k; FTB purchase grant €15k Malta / €40k Gozo (deeds 1 Jan 2025–31 Dec 2026); Budget 2027 speech announced renewal for 2027 (per Belair, secondary — legal notice pending as of 2026-10-23)
- **Restoration and finishing grant** ("VAT refund"): 18% and/or 10% of costs, max €54k per property, 1 Jan 2025–31 Dec 2026 (Ministry for Finance)
- **Buy Sustainable Property Scheme** (BCA): €3,000/yr or €1,500/yr for 3 years depending on the home's EPC classification; second edition covers Maltese and EU citizens buying a residential property Oct 2025–Sep 2026 (2026 data, per Times of Malta — secondary; check BCA for a later edition)
- **Inherited dwelling stamp duty**: 3.5% on first €400k (raised 28 Oct 2025)

## Foreign-buyer rules (AIP)

**Acquisition of Immovable Property (AIP) permit** — Chapter 246, Laws of Malta. Required for:
- **Non-EU citizens** buying any property outside an SDA (third-country nationals with long-term resident status under the Immigration Act fall outside Cap. 246's "non-resident person" definition)
- **Maltese / EU citizens without 5 years' continuous residence in Malta** buying for **secondary-residence purposes** (primary residence and property needed for their business are permit-free)
- **Maltese / EU citizens with ≥5 years' continuous residence at any time before the acquisition**: no permit needed (2026 data, sources Cap. 246 art. 2–3 consolidated text; MTCA Conditions for Buying Immovable Property)

**Minimum-price thresholds (updated annually by the NSO Immovable Property Price Index, Cap. 246 art. 6(1)(b) — not CPI)**:
- Flat/maisonette: **€174,274**
- Any other immovable property (house/villa/townhouse): **€300,619**
- Figures as published by the MTCA under LN 174 of 2024 (index 254.78) (2025 data, source MTCA AIP FAQ, last updated 29 Aug 2025). LN 202 of 2026 (Gazette 17 Jul 2026) set the index at 287.89 on 1 Apr 2026 (+6.7% on 2025), so the operative 2026 minimums are expected to be higher (inference from the index rise); no updated euro figures found as of 2026-10-23 — confirm with the MTCA AIP Section before signing

**Conditions**: residential use only, one property per non-resident buyer (unless in an SDA), **may NOT be rented out**, no sale in parts or split into more than one dwelling; acquisition within 6 months of the permit and a certified copy of the deed to the Commissioner within 3 months (2026 data, sources MTCA AIP FAQ; Cap. 246 Second Schedule).

**Fee**: €233, independent of property value; the AIP Section's Quality Service Charter commits to issue the permit within 35 days of a complete and correct application (2025 data, source MTCA AIP FAQ, last updated 29 Aug 2025).

**MPRP (Malta Permanent Residence Programme)** — separate from AIP, residence-by-investment route. Property requirement (**LN 310 of 2024**, in force 1 Jan 2025): **€375,000 minimum purchase** anywhere in Malta or Gozo (standardised from prior €350k Malta / €300k South-of-Malta-and-Gozo split). Lease alternative: **€14,000 p.a.** anywhere. Property held at least 5 years, then a residential property must still be kept. Fees (**LN 146 of 2025**): non-refundable administrative fee **€60,000** + Government contribution **€37,000** (same for purchase and lease routes) + €7,500 per adult dependant other than the spouse + **€2,000** NGO donation + €500 residence card per person. Capital assets ≥€500,000 (€150,000 financial) or ≥€650,000 (€75,000 financial). Purchased SDA property may be leased out. Implementing agency: Residency Malta Agency (2026 data, sources Residency Malta Agency MPRP page; S.L. 217.26 as amended by LN 310 of 2024 and LN 146 of 2025).

## Country-specific quirks

- **Globigerina limestone (lower coralline + globigerina + upper coralline)** — traditional building material; soft, workable, very porous. **Salt crystallization is the #1 deterioration mechanism** (airborne marine salts). Modern construction = reinforced concrete with limestone facing/blockwork. Pre-1960s village cores = full-stone construction
- **Damp/mould — severe seasonal issue**: year-round humidity ~60–80%, peaking ~85% in winter (est. — verify against Malta Meteorological Office climate normals). Lack of central heating + porous walls + limited insulation + no through-ventilation in narrow Sliema/Valletta apartments = mould can bloom quickly (within days) on cold-bridged walls. **Buyers should inspect after a wet winter**; check north-facing rooms, behind wardrobes, ground-floor walls
- **Narrow streets / parking**: Sliema, Valletta, Three Cities, Mdina/Rabat UCAs have no off-street parking; on-street is permit-zoned and saturated. Confirm garage/parking title (**garage = separately-titled, often separate deed**) — common buyer surprise
- **Gozo separate market**: ferry from Ċirkewwa–Mġarr (~25 min, every 45 min); Fast Ferry Valletta–Mġarr (~45 min, passenger only). Slower turnover, lower prices, higher renovation share. **Gozo Tunnel** project repeatedly proposed and shelved — **do NOT price tunnel into investment thesis**
- **"Three Cities" gentrification**: Birgu (Vittoriosa), Senglea (Isla), Cospicua (Bormla). Strong heritage UCA appeal, marina (Grand Harbour), ~15–30% appreciation 2022–2025 (est. — per agency reports, secondary; no per-locality NSO index exists, see §--price gap note). Bidding wars common on character townhouses. Risk: speculative restoration, structural unknowns under Knights-era buildings
- **Beach-town dynamics**: Marsaxlokk (fishing village, weekend market), Mellieħa (north, beach access), Buġibba/Qawra/St Paul's Bay (mass tourism, high-density, lower price tier). Marsascala (southeast) emerging
- **Knights of St John heritage (1530–1798)**: UCAs in Valletta/Mdina/Three Cities have **Superintendence of Cultural Heritage (SCH)** consent layered over **PA** permit — any external alteration on a scheduled property requires both. Discovering a property is "scheduled Grade 1/2" can lock down all renovation
- **Ground rent / temporary emphyteusis**: common encumbrance on older properties — verify whether title is **freehold (perpetual)** or **emphyteusis (lease for term, often 99/150 years)**. On expiry of a temporary emphyteusis of a dwelling, a Maltese-citizen emphyteuta occupying it as ordinary residence may stay on under a lease (or, for some pre-21 Jun 1979 grants over 30 years, convert to perpetual emphyteusis) under the Housing (Decontrol) Ordinance, Cap. 158 art. 12; where the house is let to a tenant at expiry, Cap. 69 does not apply to that lease and a Maltese-citizen tenant occupying it as ordinary residence may continue under a new lease from the directus dominus (art. 12(3)) (2026 data, source Cap. 158 consolidated text)
- **Notarial process**: mandatory for all property transfers. Buyer chooses notary. Konvenju (Promise of Sale, registered with the MTCA within 21 days) → 1% provisional stamp duty + 10% deposit → searches → Final Deed (publication) → balance + 4% stamp duty + transfer tax (seller side). The konvenju's effect ceases on the date the parties agreed, or 3 months from when the sale could be carried out if none was agreed, unless the buyer files a judicial intimation before expiry and a sworn application within 30 days after it (Civil Code Cap. 16 art. 1357(2))
