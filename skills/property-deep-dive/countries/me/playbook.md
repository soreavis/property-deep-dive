# Montenegro 🇲🇪 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `me`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-04).

## Country profile

- **Postcode**: 5 digits (`81000` Podgorica, `85320` Tivat, `85330` Kotor, `85310` Budva, `81250` Cetinje)
- **Admin levels**: 25 opštine (municipalities, incl. Glavni grad Podgorica); no provinces. MONSTAT groups them into Podgorica plus three statistical regions: coastal (Bar, Budva, Herceg Novi, Kotor, Tivat, Ulcinj), central (Cetinje, Danilovgrad, Nikšić, Tuzi, Zeta) and northern (13 municipalities) (2026 data, source MONSTAT)
- **Currency**: **EUR (€)** — unilaterally adopted **2002** (Deutsche Mark parallel tender from Nov 1999, sole tender from Jan 2001); no national issuance; as of 2004 no EU/Eurosystem agreement on its use, the same set-up as Kosovo, so not unique in Europe (source ECB Occasional Paper No. 11, Feb 2004). The EU common position on chapter 17, provisionally closed 12 Oct 2026, keeps the euro from accession, with Montenegro a Member State with a derogation until the Council decides on euro-area entry (2026 data, source Vijesti, secondary — verify at eu.me)
- **Languages**: Montenegrin (official); Serbian, Bosnian, Albanian and Croatian also in official use (Constitution art. 13, Sl. list CG 1/2007)
- **Cadastre**: **Uprava za nekretnine (UzN)** — `https://www.gov.me/en/uzn`
  - **eKatastar**: `https://ekatastar.me` — covers ~98% of registered properties (Sept 2025) — rates/figures may have changed since
  - **Geoportal CG**: `https://geoportal.co.me/`
- **Identifier**: parcela + KO (katastarska opština)
- **Key document**: **list nepokretnosti** (property sheet/extract)
- **EU accession negotiations active** — all 33 chapters opened; 21 provisionally closed after the 12 Oct 2026 accession conference (chapters 17, 18, 19), 12 still open (computed) (2026 data, source EEAS); EU member states decided in April 2026 to begin drafting the Accession Treaty; Government target: close all chapters by end-2026, membership 2028 (2026 data, source US State Dept 2026 Investment Climate Statement)

### Compute

1. EUR/m² = price / **korisna (stambena) površina** (usable living area, the basis MONSTAT reports on)
2. **Currency**: EUR unilaterally adopted = no FX risk for EUR holders
3. **Coastal vs mountain divergence**: ~5-10× est. at the extremes (Tivat luxury vs interior, from the est. rows); MONSTAT new-build regional averages are much closer — coastal 2,838 vs northern 2,145 = 1.3× (computed, Q2 2026)
4. **UNESCO** premium: Kotor Old Town strict construction = scarcity premium

## Section: `--traffic`

### Sources

- **Uprava za saobraćaj** (Transport Administration; manages state magistral and regional roads; 65 automatic counters at 63 locations; annual PGDS tables per section): `https://www.gov.me/uzs/brojaci-saobracaja` (2025 data, source Uprava za saobraćaj)
- **Monteput** (operator of the Sozina tunnel and the Bar–Boljare motorway; daily and annual vehicle counts): `https://monteput.me/promet-vozila-tunel-sozina/` (2025 data, source Monteput)
- **Putevi d.o.o.** (`https://putevi.me/`) is the Capital City Podgorica road-works company: municipal works notices, no traffic counts
- City traffic data Podgorica, Budva

### Key term

**PGDS (Prosječni godišnji dnevni saobraćaj)** = AADT-equivalent: total vehicles counted on a road section during the year ÷ days in the year (definition per Uprava za saobraćaj).

2025 PGDS reference points (2025 data, source Uprava za saobraćaj PGDS 2025 table on data.gov.me): M3 Danilovgrad–Podgorica (Novo selo counter) 16,858 v/d, the highest in the published network; M4 Podgorica–Tuzi 15,072; M1 Meljine–Kamenari (Kumbor) 14,997; M2 Virpazar–Podgorica (Golubovci) 13,382; M10 Podgorica–Cetinje 12,939; M1 Kotor–Krtolska raskrsnica (Vrmac) 12,287; M10 Cetinje–Budva 10,312. The Budva–Tivat counters are missing from the 2025 table because of boulevard works; Uprava za saobraćaj puts those sections above 25,000 v/d on average in the summer months. Network PGDS rose about 8% on 2024. **Sozina tunnel**: 4,140,109 vehicles in 2025 = 11,343 v/d average (computed: 4,140,109 ÷ 365); busiest 2025 day 22,230 vehicles (10 Aug) (2025 data, source Monteput).

### Verdict bands

- 🟢 < 1,500 v/d (residential, rural, mountain)
- 🟡 1,500–10,000 v/d (regional roads, coastal off-season)
- 🟠 10,000–25,000 v/d (Podgorica approach roads M3/M4/M10 at 12,939–16,858 PGDS; M1 Kumbor 14,997; Sozina tunnel 11,343 average with summer peak days up to 22,230 — 2025 data, sources Uprava za saobraćaj + Monteput)
- 🔴 > 25,000 v/d (M1 Budva–Tivat sections in the summer months — 2025 data, source Uprava za saobraćaj)

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax — Porez na nepokretnost

- **0.25–1.00% of market value** (art. 9), set by each opština in its own Odluka; **0.3–1.5%** for a secondary residential building or flat (art. 10) — secondary = not the owner's prebivalište or permanent residence, or owned by a legal person; the owner's only dwelling in Montenegro is not secondary when the owner has registered residence in Montenegro (art. 11) *(2026 data, source Zakon o porezu na nepokretnosti, Sl. list CG 25/19 as amended by 49/22, 152/22, 118/25 and 133/25, sluzbenilist.me)*
- Main-residence relief: tax cut by 20% for the owner plus 10% per household member, capped at 50% (art. 12)
- Coastal example, municipality-wide (not parcel-level): Bar 0.26% for a dwelling of an owner resident in Bar / 0.60% for a secondary residential object (owner resident elsewhere, or a legal person) *(2026 data, source Opština Bar Odluka o porezu na nepokretnosti, consolidated to Sl. list CG – opštinski propisi 65/25)* — other opštine set their own rates
- Inland Podgorica: 0.25-0.5%
- Paid in **two equal instalments** due **30 June** and **31 October**; the municipal assessment decision issues by 30 April (art. 15)

### Transaction taxes — PROGRESSIVE since 1 Jan 2024

**MAJOR CORRECTION**: previously flat 3%; now progressive **marginal** RETT — EUR 4,500 + 5% on the part above EUR 150k; EUR 22,000 + 6% on the part above EUR 500k (bands below are marginal, not whole-price slabs; art. 11 as replaced by Sl. list CG 28/2023, in force 1 Jan 2024, verified on sluzbenilist.me 2026-10-21):

| Property value (EUR) | Rate |
|---|---:|
| Up to **150,000** | **3%** |
| **150,001–500,000** | **5%** |
| Above **500,000** | **6%** |

- **15-day deadline**: the buyer files the PR-OPPN return and pays the tax with it within 15 days of the contract date (off-plan: of handover) (arts 15-16)
- Late-payment interest (zatezna kamata): ECB main refinancing rate + 3 percentage points, set per half-year and published by the tax authority — replaced the former 0.03%/day (Zakon o poreskoj administraciji art. 95 as amended by Sl. list CG 104/2026, in force 18 Jul 2026; applies to RETT via RETT law art. 20) *(2026 data, source sluzbenilist.me)*
- **Buyer pays** transfer tax (taxpayer = acquirer, art. 7(1))

### VAT (PDV)

- **21% standard**; first-sale residential subject to 21% VAT in lieu of RETT (NOT a reduced rate)
- **15% reduced bracket** (eff **1 Jan 2025**) (art. 24a(2) as replaced by Sl. list CG 94/2024) for accommodation in hospitality accommodation facilities defined by the tourism law, food and drink service (excl. alcohol, sugared drinks and coffee), books/serial publications, author rights and education/arts services, art and antiques, cultural/sport tickets, non-profit sports facilities, marina services (until EU accession), solar panels and hairdressing — **material for short-let yield modelling**; the EUR 30,000 12-month small-supplier threshold (art. 42) does not apply to taxpayers seated outside Montenegro *(2026-10-21 verified, source Sl. list CG 94/2024 + Poreska uprava consolidated VAT text)*
- **7% reduced** (art. 24a(1), 12 categories): basic food, medicines, orthotic/prosthetic aids, textbooks, non-bottled drinking water, daily press, public passenger transport, public hygiene, funeral services, animal feed and farm inputs, menstrual products, baby diapers; **0%** (art. 25): exports and Health Insurance Fund list medicines, among other listed supplies; the temporary 0% on bread, flour and sunflower oil ended 31 Dec 2022 (art. 71f, Sl. list CG 65/2022), and basic food has been in the 7% category since *(2026 data, source Sl. list CG 94/2024 and 65/2022 on sluzbenilist.me + PwC Tax Summaries ME, reviewed 7 Aug 2026)*
- New build: 21% VAT included in price (first transfer of a newly built object is taxable; later real-estate turnover is VAT-exempt, art. 27(2))
- Building land: sale of building land (land with a building-permit act) by a VAT taxpayer is a VAT supply from 1 Apr 2026 (Sl. list CG 12/2026)
- New VAT law Sl. list CG 104/2026 (in force 25 Jul 2026) applies only from the day of EU accession — same 21/15/7 rates *(2026-10-21 verified, source sluzbenilist.me)*
- Resale: subject to RETT, not VAT
- Building land (građevinsko zemljište) on which VAT is paid: outside RETT from 1 Apr 2026 (RETT law art. 6 as amended by Sl. list CG 33/2026) *(2026-10-21 verified, source sluzbenilist.me)*

### Personal income tax (PIT) — Porez na dohodak fizičkih lica

Salary (gross/month) brackets effective since 1 Jan 2022 (Europe Now I), unchanged through 2026 (pending Government Euro-model bill of 18 Sep 2026 would drop the 15% band — 0% up to €700 / 9% above from 1 Jan 2027 — not gazetted in Sl. list CG and not on any autumn-2026 plenary agenda through the 29 Oct 2026 sitting; brackets restated by Sl. list CG 160/2025, applying from 1 Jan 2026):

| Salary | Rate |
|---|---:|
| Up to €700 | **0%** |
| €700.01–€1,000 | **9%** |
| Above €1,000 | **15%** |

Plus **municipal surtax (prirez)** 13% of PIT due (15% in Podgorica and Cetinje) → top marginal rate on salary 16.95% (15% × 1.13), 17.25% in Podgorica and Cetinje (computed). Passive personal income (rental, dividends, royalties, capital gains) → flat **15% PIT** (art. 10(1)(3) as replaced by Sl. list CG 160/2025). *(2026 data, source Sl. list CG 160/2025 on sluzbenilist.me + PwC Tax Summaries ME, reviewed 7 Aug 2026)*

### Capital gains

- **15% PIT** on the gain (sale price minus acquisition price)
- **Exempt**: real estate used as the taxpayer's only and main residence; transfers between spouses linked to marriage, divorce or inheritance; gifts to first-degree relatives (spouse, children) *(2026 data, source PwC Tax Summaries ME, reviewed 7 Aug 2026 — secondary; confirm the conditions in the PIT law)*

### Total transaction cost (buyer side)

- Resale ≤€150k: est. ~3.9-4.4% at €50k-€150k (3% RETT + notary ~0.4-0.9% + ~0.5% other). Notary = tariff fee plus 21% VAT: €350 at €50k, €480 at €80,000-€119,999.99, then +€13 per started €15,000 (€480 + 3 × €13 = €519 at €150k), capped at €8,000 (Tarifni broj 1 and art. 12(2), Sl. list CG 49/2026; 2026 data, source Notarska komora). The ~0.5% other (translation, legal help, €15 cadastre filing) has no published benchmark; the range assumes the buyer bears the whole notary fee, so check the contract
- Resale €150-500k: est. ~3.9-5.1% (RETT effective 3.0% at €150k to 4.4% at €500k = €4,500 + 5% of the excess over €150k, plus notary with VAT ~0.4% at €150k to ~0.2% at €500k, fee €480 + 26 × €13 = €818, and ~0.5% other per the line above)
- Resale >€500k: est. ~5.1-6.2% up to €2M, rising towards ~6.5% above (RETT effective 4.4% at €500k, 5.2% at €1M, 5.6% at €2M = €22,000 + 6% of the excess over €500k, plus notary with VAT ~0.2% falling to ~0.1%, fee €480 + 59 × €13 = €1,247 at €1M and €480 + 126 × €13 = €2,118 at €2M, and ~0.5% other)
- New build: 21% VAT in price + est. ~0.6-1.1% other costs at €100k-€2M (notary with VAT ~0.1-0.6% + ~0.5% other per the resale lines; no published benchmark for developer-side charges, so ask for an itemised statement)

### Future risk

- EU accession: two enacted laws apply only from the day of accession — the new VAT law (Sl. list CG 104/2026, same 21/15/7 rates) and equal treatment of EU natural and legal persons as property buyers (Zakon o svojinsko-pravnim odnosima art. 415(4), Sl. list CG 29/2025)
- Property-based residence tightened in 2026 (€150k tax-assessed threshold from 17 Jan 2026 — see below)

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- Flat leases fall under **Zakon o obligacionim odnosima** (Sl. list CG 47/08, as amended): an open-ended lease ends on notice, 3 months when the contract sets none (art. 634); the landlord may terminate without notice after two consecutive unpaid rents (art. 631) or an unauthorised sublet (art. 632) (consolidated text, source paragraf.me)
- **Zakon o održavanju stambenih zgrada** (Sl. list CG 41/16 … 84/24) covers etažni vlasnici and common parts, not tenancy; it repealed the Zakon o stanovanju i održavanju stambenih zgrada (4/11, 1/14), which sluzbenilist.me lists as nevažeći
- Tenant protection moderate
- Standard 1-3 yr contracts; coastal seasonal common

### Short-let (Airbnb)

**MANDATORY before any platform listing** (Zakon o turizmu i ugostiteljstvu, Sl. list CG 2/18 … 76/20, consolidated text per paragraf.me; later amendments incl. 84/24 not checked — verify with the opština):
- **Rješenje o upisu u Centralni turistički registar** from the opština's local administration, on a prijava filed at least 8 days before the first guest (art. 73(2), 77, 100(6)); a natural person may let at most 10 rooms / 20 beds (art. 100(1))
- **Kategorizacija** (stars) by rješenje of the local administration, requested within 5 days of the registry rješenje and valid 3 years, for rooms, tourist apartments and houses; a *stan za iznajmljivanje turistima* is exempt (art. 84(1), (9), (10), (12))
- Advertising, including on Booking.com and Airbnb, is allowed only for objects holding the rješenje (art. 8)
- **Pending**: a Government bill for a new Zakon o turizmu i ugostiteljstvu (sent to Parliament, reported 8 Jul 2026) would require natural persons to register with the tax authority and make unapproved operation a criminal offence; not applied to hosts as of 8 Oct 2026 (2026 data, sources biznis.me + trinity.estate, secondary/agency — verify at zakoni.skupstina.me)

#### Tourist tax

- **€0.10–€1.00 per person per day**, rate set by each opština (Zakon o boravišnoj taksi art. 7, as amended by Sl. list CG 48/15); children under 12 exempt, ages 12-18 pay 50% (art. 3); household accommodation may pay a yearly lump sum set from the tourist-place coefficient (A 1.0 / B 0.8 / C 0.6 / D 0.5), the number of beds and an assumed 45 days of full occupancy (art. 7) (2015 consolidated text, source Nespa catalogue 2016 — rates/figures may have changed since)
- Tivat: €1.00 per person per day (2026 data, source Turistička organizacija Tivat); Ulcinj: €1.00 per adult per day (source Turistička organizacija Ulcinj, undated)
- **24-hour guest registration**: the host registers each arrival with the local tourist organisation within 24 h and deregisters within 24 h of departure (art. 7b)
- A higher tourist tax was in the news in 2026 but had not taken effect for hosts as of 8 Oct 2026 (source trinity.estate, agency — verify with the opština)

#### Rental income tax

- **15% PIT** on rental income less documented costs, or less a standard 30% without records; lets of immovable property for tourism use get standardised costs of 50% or 70% (2026 data, source PwC Tax Summaries ME, reviewed 7 Aug 2026)
- Fines for a natural person: €200–€2,000 for letting without the registry rješenje (Zakon o turizmu i ugostiteljstvu art. 130(1)(1)); €250–€1,500 for not collecting or reporting tourist tax or not registering guests within 24 h (Zakon o boravišnoj taksi art. 11) — same consolidated texts as above, verify current amounts

### Tax on rental

- 15% PIT on net rental income (30% / 50% / 70% standard deductions above)
- Tourist tax collected from the guest with the accommodation price and shown separately on the bill; a host who does not collect it pays it in (Zakon o boravišnoj taksi art. 4-5)

### Specific risks

#### Earthquake

- **15 April 1979 earthquake**: M7.0 per ZHMS (Mw 6.9 in the international catalogue entry, secondary), epicentre in the Adriatic about 15 km off the coast between Ulcinj and Bar, intensity IX (Mercalli) along the whole coast; 101 dead in Montenegro and 35 in Albania; Ulcinj, Bar, Petrovac, Budva, Tivat, Kotor, Risan and Herceg Novi hit, 250 settlements destroyed (1979 event data, source ZHMS)
- Coast remains seismic: ZHMS rates the whole coastal belt well above the interior in seismogenic potential (Ulcinj, Budva, Boka Kotorska and Skadar Lake source zones); the interior is quieter apart from Piva (induced seismicity) and Berane–Plav (Plav M5.0, 4 Jan 2018) (source ZHMS)
- **Bay of Kotor** historic damage zone: strong earthquakes on 13 Jun 1563 and 25 Jul 1608; the 1667 Dubrovnik earthquake (intensity X) devastated Boka (source ZHMS)
- Seismic design code: Eurocode 8 with the Montenegrin national annex MEST EN 1998-1:2015/NA:2015, four zones by reference ground acceleration agR (10% exceedance in 50 years, 475-year return period) (source ZHMS). The zone map is regional; the design action for a parcel depends on its ground class from a geotechnical study

#### Wildfires

- **Mediterranean coastal critical** Jul-Aug
- Karst pinewood = high fire intensity
- EFFIS-mapped burnt area (fires of about 30 ha or larger), national total: 51,531 ha in 2017 (highest since 2006), 39,498 ha (2020), 42,187 ha (2021), 24,579 ha (2022), 454 ha (2023), 23,054 ha (2024), 27,658 ha (2025), 43,351 ha in 2026 (partial year) (2017-2026 data, source EFFIS) — national figures, not parcel-level

#### Karst hazards

- **Durmitor, Lovćen, Orjen** — sinkholes, drainage
- **Coastal hinterland**: cave systems
- Construction requires soil study

#### Floods

- **Bojana river**, mountain torrents
- Coastal flash flooding

## Active fiscal incentives + visa programs (2025-2026)

### Investor residency (effective 17 January 2026)

- Property-based temporary residence (Zakon o strancima art. 56, in the law since 2018), tightened by the 2026 amendment; it is not a citizenship route — the CBI Programme ended **31 Dec 2022** (historical only)
- **€150k minimum tax-assessed property value** — Art 56 sets the floor at the local tax authority's transfer-tax base per *rješenje o porezu na promet nepokretnosti*, **NOT** the contract/purchase price (low amounts shown on the title deed do not count); the base must be at least €150,000 (art. 56(1) and (4) as amended by Sl. list CG 3/26, adopted by Parliament 31 Dec 2025, published 9 Jan 2026, in force **17 January 2026**). A first-sale new build bought with 21% VAT is outside RETT (RETT law art. 6), and the amendment text does not say how such a purchase proves value — confirm with the Ministry of Interior before relying on a VAT-charged purchase
- **EU/EEA/CH exemption**: the €150k threshold does NOT apply to nationals of EU member states (or their family members of any nationality), Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland (art. 56(5))
- **50% minimum ownership share**: a co-owner qualifies only with at least a ½ share (art. 56(2), added by Sl. list CG 3/19 — predates the 2026 amendment); qualifying property = family house, weekend house, villa, flat, hospitality object, residential-business building or business premises (art. 56(3)) — bare land does not qualify
- Grandfathering: renewal requests for property-based permits issued before 17 Jan 2026 are decided under the pre-amendment law, so without the €150k test (art. 217b as added by Sl. list CG 3/26)
- Path: temporary permit valid up to 1 year, renewable a year at a time (arts 63-64); renewing a property-based permit now also needs proof that taxes were paid during the permit (art. 64 as amended by 3/26; renewals of permits issued before 17 Jan 2026 are decided under the old law, art. 217b) → permanent residence after 5 years' continuous lawful residence (art. 86), with means, health insurance, accommodation, basic Montenegrin and, since 3/26, proof of tax compliance (art. 88(1)) → naturalisation after 10 years' lawful and continuous residence, with release from the other citizenship and basic Montenegrin (Zakon o crnogorskom državljanstvu art. 8(1))
- Absence rule: a temporary permit lapses when the holder stays outside Montenegro for more than 30 days during the permit (art. 65(1)(3)); 3/26 rewrote the art. 65(2) exception, which now covers only family-reunification permits on humanitarian grounds — the former 90-day notified-absence exception is no longer in the text (read from the amendment; confirm with the Ministry of Interior) *(2026 data, source Zakon o strancima Sl. list CG 12/18 + 3/19 + 3/26 and Zakon o crnogorskom državljanstvu Sl. list CG 13/08, sluzbenilist.me; later amendments 33/26 and 154/26 (in force 27 Oct 2026) leave arts 56, 63, 64, 65(1)(3), 65(2), 86 and 88 unchanged — 154/26 rewrites only art. 65(1)(5))*

## Caveats unique to ME

- **EUR unilaterally adopted 2002** — no national issuance; as of 2004 no EU/Eurosystem agreement, the same set-up as Kosovo (source ECB Occasional Paper No. 11); the EU chapter 17 common position (provisionally closed 12 Oct 2026) keeps the euro from accession, with a derogation until a Council decision on euro-area entry (2026 data, source Vijesti, secondary); **no FX risk** for EUR holders
- **CBI Programme ABOLISHED 31 Dec 2022** (investment of at least EUR 450k in a listed development project in Podgorica or the coastal region, or EUR 250k in the northern or central region, plus EUR 100k to the fund for less-developed municipalities; Government Decision Sl. list CG 79/18, end date moved from 31 Dec 2021 to 31 Dec 2022 by 143/21, sluzbenilist.me) — major historical reference
- **NEW investor residency (effective 17 January 2026)** (€150k property → 5 years to permanent → 10 to citizenship)
- **Progressive RETT since 1 Jan 2024**: 3% / 5% / 6% marginal bands — €4,500 + 5% above €150k, €22,000 + 6% above €500k (not flat 3% as previously)
- **VAT restructure (eff 1 Jan 2025)**: new **15% bracket** added (accommodation, food and drink service, books and seven further categories); 21% standard; 7% for 12 listed categories; 0% for exports and Fund-list medicines (bread, flour and sunflower oil in the 7% category since the temporary 0% ended 31 Dec 2022); 21% on first-sale residential remains in lieu of RETT
- **Europe Now 2 (eff 1 October 2024)**: NET minimum wage raised to **€600** (jobs up to qualification level V) / **€800** (level VI and higher); employee pension-and-disability contribution cut 15% → 10%; employer pension-and-disability contribution eliminated (was 5.5%). **Pending "Euro model"** (Government bills published 18 Sep 2026, to apply from 1 Jan 2027): NET minimum wages EUR 1,000 / 1,250 / 1,400 (qualification levels I-II / III-IV / V and higher); pension-and-disability, additional-service and unemployment-insurance contribution rates to 0%; PIT on employment income 0% to EUR 700 / 9% above — not gazetted in Sl. list CG (no 2026 PIT, contributions or Labour Law amendment carries it) and not on any autumn-2026 plenary agenda through the 29 Oct 2026 sitting, verify at skupstina.me (2026 data, source gov.me bills + sluzbenilist.me + zakoni.skupstina.me session agendas)
- **Foreign-buyer rules** (Zakon o svojinsko-pravnim odnosima art. 415): a foreign person CANNOT own natural resources, goods in general use, agricultural land, forests or forest land, cultural monuments of exceptional or special importance, immovables within **1 km of the land border** or **on islands**, or areas closed to foreigners by a special law; long-term lease, concession, BOT and PPP rights over the first six categories stay open on domestic terms. **Exception**: a foreign natural person may acquire agricultural land, forest or forest land up to **5,000 m²** only where the contract's subject is a residential building standing on that land *(2026 data, source Sl. list CG 19/09 art. 415, sluzbenilist.me)*. **From EU accession only**: natural and legal persons from EU member states acquire ownership on the same terms as domestic persons (art. 415(4) added by Sl. list CG 29/2025, in force 29 Mar 2025, applies from the day of accession per art. 422a). **Workaround**: incorporate a Montenegrin **d.o.o.** (domestic legal entity faces no restriction) — the dominant vehicle for agricultural/coastal portfolio buyers *(2026-05-27 verified, source Chambers Real Estate 2025 ME)*
- **Russian buyer concentration post-2022** + EU buyer surge
- **Coastal vs mountain divergence**: Adriatic luxury ~5-10× inland asks (est.); MONSTAT new-build regional averages are closer — coastal 2,838 vs northern 2,145 EUR/m² = 1.3× (computed, Q2 2026)
- **UNESCO Old Towns** (Kotor, Cetinje) — strict construction, premium scarcity
- **1979 Adriatic earthquake** as historical reference
- **Bay of Kotor restoration** (UNESCO) constraints
- **Tivat Porto Montenegro / Lustica Bay** = ultra-luxury markets ~€5-12k/m²+ (est., listing asks; MONSTAT coastal new-build average 2,838 EUR/m², Q2 2026)
- **Coastal wildfires** Jul-Aug
- **Karst hazards** (Durmitor, Lovćen, Orjen)
- **15-day deadline** post-contract to file and pay RETT; late-payment interest = ECB main refinancing rate + 3 pp since 18 Jul 2026 (was 0.03%/day; Sl. list CG 104/2026)
