# Gibraltar 🇬🇮 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `gi`. Status: ✅ Standalone playbook (UK Overseas Territory; own tax authority, land registry, courts).

## Country profile

- Postcode: single Gibraltar code `GX11 1AA` (covers the entire territory; not granular like UK postcodes)
- Area: ~6.8 km²; population ~32,700 (Gibraltar Statistics Office, 2023)
- Currency: Gibraltar Pound (GIP), 1:1 pegged to GBP; sterling and GIP both legal tender locally; only GBP accepted in UK/EU
- Language: English (official); Spanish + Llanito widely spoken
- Legal system: English common law (with Gibraltar-specific Acts); Court of Appeal + Supreme Court of Gibraltar; ultimate appeal to JCPC (Judicial Committee of the Privy Council, London)
- Land registry: **Gibraltar Land Property Services + Supreme Court Land Registry** under [Land Titles Act](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/) (often referenced as Land Registration Act regime; primary citable Act: confirm specific title at gibraltarlaws.gov.gi)
- EU status: **OUTSIDE EU** since 31 January 2020 (left with UK). Schengen entry under UK–EU Gibraltar Treaty — **provisional application from 15 July 2026** ([EU Council greenlight 01 Apr 2026](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/04/01/eu-uk-relations-member-states-greenlight-eu-uk-deal-on-gibraltar/); see § Frontier Treaty below)

⚠️ **Do not conflate**: Gibraltar is a **UK Overseas Territory**, not part of the UK itself and not part of Spain. Gibraltar has its own tax authority (Gibraltar Income Tax Office), its own GFSC-regulated banks, its own Supreme Court — references to "HMRC" or "Land Registry England & Wales" do **not** apply.

## Section: `--foreign-buyer-rules`

**Data sources** (priority order):

1. **Gibraltar Income Tax Office — Qualifying Individuals**: [gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/individuals-and-employees/qualifying-individuals](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/individuals-and-employees/qualifying-individuals)
2. **Gibraltar Finance Centre (Cat-2/HEPSS administration)**: [gibraltarfinance.gi](https://www.gibraltarfinance.gi/) — Suite 761 Europort, +350 20050011
3. **Gibraltar laws portal (primary statutes)**: [gibraltarlaws.gov.gi](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/)
4. **Estate-agent market-class signposting** (Open vs Restricted): Century 21 Gibraltar, 1704 Luxury Real Estate, Richardsons Properties (cross-checked)

**Key rules — TWO-TIER residential property market**:

Gibraltar operates a **two-tier residential property market**. Failing to identify which tier a listing sits in is the single most consequential error a foreign buyer can make. Roughly half of the stock by unit count is **not** accessible to non-residents.

- **Open Market** — no nationality or residency restriction; any buyer eligible. Carries a price premium versus Restricted-Market stock at comparable size/location.
- **Restricted Market** (a.k.a. "3-year residency" market / pre-owned Government Affordable Housing / certain estate-by-estate covenants) — purchaser must demonstrate **≥3 years continuous, ordinary Gibraltar residency immediately prior to purchase**; **owner-occupation only**; resale also restricted (subject to the same eligibility test for successor buyers, and to the Affordable Housing buyback regime where applicable). Non-residents, Cat-2/HEPSS pre-arrival applicants, and overseas investors are **categorically excluded** from this tier.

**Foreign-buyer reality**: non-residents and Cat-2/HEPSS pre-arrival applicants are **effectively limited to the Open Market**. Restricted-Market listings often appear at headline-attractive prices but **cannot be acquired without 3-year prior Gibraltar residency**.

**Verification path BEFORE making an offer**:
- Confirm each listing's market class with the **estate agent in writing** AND cross-check with the **title deed** (any covenant referencing residency eligibility / Government buyback / Affordable Housing scheme tier = Restricted)
- Confirm current residency category eligibility with **Gibraltar Finance Centre** (info@financecentre.gov.gi, +350 20050011) — Cat-2 / HEPSS / Self-Sufficiency / Employment
- Confirm cross-border tax position with both Gibraltar Income Tax Office AND home-country revenue (Gibraltar has **no tax treaty network of the breadth UK has**; check bilateral arrangements)

**Other foreign-buyer notes**:

- **Residency is decoupled from ownership** (even on the Open Market) — buying property does NOT confer right to live in Gibraltar. Non-EU/non-UK buyers wanting to reside need a separate **residency permit** (Cat-2 Individual / HEPSS / Self-Sufficiency / Employment-based).
- **Self-employment route is discretionary and frequently refused** — community signal: r/gibraltar 1opdfxf (Q2 2026), where an applicant detailed refusal despite 12-month residential + office leases in place. Engage a Gibraltar-licensed immigration lawyer **pre-relocation**; do **not** rely on a property purchase or lease as evidence of eligibility.
- **EU/EEA buyers post-Brexit**: Free-movement rights from EU into Gibraltar **ended 31 Jan 2020** (Brexit). EU/EEA nationals now require entry under the same routes as third-country nationals, **pending entry into force of the UK–EU Treaty (provisional 15 Jul 2026)**.
- **Frontier Treaty implications**: Once provisionally applied (15 Jul 2026), Schengen border checks relocate from the land border (La Línea/La Verja crossing) to Gibraltar's airport and port; daily commuters from Spain (~15,000+ pre-treaty) gain streamlined crossing. **Treaty does NOT auto-grant Schengen residency rights to Gibraltar property owners** — see § Frontier Treaty for verbatim Government statement.

**Confidence**: HIGH on the two-tier (Open / Restricted) market framework (4 estate-agent sources + r/AskUK community confirmation). HIGH on Brexit/Schengen status. HIGH on self-employment refusal pattern (community signal + corroborated by absent-Cat-2 enforcement chatter). MEDIUM on Frontier Treaty operational details (provisionally applied but date subject to ratification — re-verify at gibraltar.gov.gi closer to purchase).

## Section: `--tax`

**Statutory framework**: **Income Tax Act 2010** (consolidated at [gibraltarlaws.gov.gi](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/)) administered by **Gibraltar Income Tax Office**. Subsidiary legislation cited below — **Qualifying (Category 2) Individuals Rules 2004** and **High Executive Possessing Specialist Skills Rules 2008** — both made under the Income Tax Act 2010.

### Income tax — dual system

Gibraltar operates **two parallel income tax systems**; the taxpayer elects whichever produces lower tax (Income Tax Office, 2025/26 rates per [PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Gibraltar Individual](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gibraltar/individual/taxes-on-personal-income), last updated 10 March 2026):

**Allowance-Based System (ABS)** — taxable income after personal allowances:
- First **£4,000**: 14%
- Next **£12,000**: 17%
- Balance: **39%**
- Tax-exempt threshold: £11,450; taper relief £11,451–£19,500

**Gross Income Based (GIB) system** — applies to gross income (no major allowances), tiered bands:

For gross income ≤ £25,000:
- First £10,000: 6%
- Next £7,000: 20%
- Balance: 28%

For gross income £25,001–£100,000 (assessable):
- First £17,000: 16%
- Next £8,000: 19%
- Next £15,000: 25%
- Next £65,000: 28%
- Remainder: 25%

(Higher-band structure for assessable income >£100,000 also exists — verify at gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office.)

### What Gibraltar does NOT have

Confirmed by [PwC Gibraltar — Other Taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gibraltar/individual/other-taxes) (2025/26):
- **No VAT** ("There is no VAT in Gibraltar")
- **No Capital Gains Tax** (on personal-asset disposals including property — verify scope at gibraltar.gov.gi)
- **No Wealth Tax**
- **No Inheritance Tax / Estate Duty**
- **No Gift Tax**

This is Gibraltar's distinguishing tax draw versus UK / Spain / most of EU. ⚠️ **Caveat**: tax-residency-of-origin matters. A UK-domiciled buyer remains subject to UK IHT on worldwide assets including Gibraltar property unless domicile changes — separate from Gibraltar's domestic rules.

### Stamp Duty on property (Stamp Duties Act, amended by Act No. 37 of 2024, effective 23 December 2024)

**Standard residential rates** ([PwC Gibraltar Other Taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gibraltar/individual/other-taxes)):
- ≤ £200,000: **0%**
- £200,001–£350,000: **2%** on first £250,000 + **5.5%** on balance
- £350,001–£800,000: **3%** on first £350,000 + **3.5%** on balance
- ≥ £800,001: **3%** on first £350,000 + **3.5%** on next £450,000 + **4.5%** on balance

**First/second-time buyer relief**: no stamp duty on properties ≤ **£300,000** (threshold raised from £260k → £300k effective 11 July 2023)

**Affordable Housing scheme**: 0% on initial purchase; **7.5% on resale within 10 years** (exemptions for forced sale / divorce / death)

**Mortgage stamp duty**:
- Under £200,000: 0.13% of secured sum
- ≥ £200,000: 0.20%

**Spouse-to-spouse transfers**: nil duty

**Worked example** (£500,000 standard purchase, not first-time buyer):
- First £350,000 @ 3% = £10,500
- Balance £150,000 @ 3.5% = £5,250
- **Total stamp duty: £15,750** (= 3.15% effective)

(Verify: confirm tier interaction edge cases — the "between bands" structure means rate cliff-edges exist near £350k and £800k; consult a Gibraltar conveyancer for tier-boundary purchases.)

### Annual rates (general rates)

Levied by **Government of Gibraltar Treasury / Rates Office** on domestic and commercial property; **payable quarterly**. Calculated on the **net annual value** of the property as assessed by **Land Property Services** (Angloinfo confirms structure; verify exact band-by-band tariff at primary source). Residential premises receive a **10% discount** versus commercial.

Net annual value reassessments are issued by the Land Property Services Office; appeal route runs to the Rates Tribunal. ⚠️ Tariff-per-pound-of-NAV for 2025/26 is **not publicly indexed in one canonical location** — request the seller's most recent rates demand pre-purchase AND confirm current tariff with Gibraltar Treasury / Land Property Services directly.

Sources: [PwC Gibraltar Other Taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/gibraltar/individual/other-taxes), [Angloinfo Gibraltar — General Taxes](https://www.angloinfo.com/how-to/gibraltar/money/gibraltar-taxes), [EY Gibraltar Tax Facts 2025–2026](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gi/tax-alerts/documents/ey-gibraltar-tax-facts-2025-2026.pdf).

### Category 2 (Cat-2) Individual — high-net-worth resident scheme

Statutory basis: **Qualifying (Category 2) Individuals Rules 2004** (subsidiary legislation under the Income Tax Act 2010 — [gibraltarlaws.gov.gi](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/)).

Source: [Gibraltar Income Tax Office — Qualifying Individuals](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office/individuals-and-employees/qualifying-individuals) + [Hassans International](https://www.gibraltarlaw.com/expertise/private-client/category-2-status-in-gibraltar/) (verbatim wording confirmed) + Gibraltar Finance Centre administration:

- **Minimum net worth**: **"at least an estimated total net worth in excess of £2 million"** (Hassans, citing the Qualifying (Category 2) Individuals Rules 2004)
- **Taxation method**: ABS only (Cat-2s cannot elect GIB)
- **Worldwide taxable income cap**: **£118,000/yr** (taxed only on first £118k regardless of true global income — 2025/26)
- **Minimum annual tax**: **£37,000**
- **Maximum annual tax**: **£42,380** (2025/26)
- **Residency conditions**: must maintain approved residential accommodation in Gibraltar **for the whole year of assessment** (purchased OR rented; cannot be sub-let to third parties); must NOT have been resident in Gibraltar in the **5 years immediately preceding** the year of assessment (≥183 days in any single year, or average ≥90 days across 3 of the 5 years = "resident" for this test)
- **Application pack** (per Hassans verbatim): CV + certified passport copy + evidence of £2M+ net worth + private medical insurance + 2 character references (one from a banker) + proof of approved accommodation
- **Fees**: **£1,168 non-refundable application fee** + **£42,380 refundable deposit** (matches the maximum annual tax)
- **Certificate issued by**: Gibraltar Finance, Suite 761 Europort, +350 20050011, info@financecentre.gov.gi

### HEPSS (High Executive Possessing Specialist Skills)

Statutory basis: **High Executive Possessing Specialist Skills Rules 2008** (subsidiary legislation under the Income Tax Act 2010 — [gibraltarlaws.gov.gi](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/legislations/high-executive-possessing-specialist-skills-rules-2008-2152/download)).

- **Minimum Gibraltar earnings**: **> £160,000/yr** (2025/26)
- **Assessable income cap**: **first £160,000** only — income above is not assessed
- **Fixed annual tax liability**: **£39,940** (2025/26) regardless of actual earnings above £160k
- **Skills test**: skills must "promote and sustain economic activity of particular value to Gibraltar" and **not be readily available** within Gibraltar — Government discretion via Gibraltar Finance Centre
- **Residency conditions**: exclusive use of approved residential accommodation in Gibraltar for the whole year of assessment; must NOT have been resident or working in Gibraltar within the **3 years preceding** application (note: 3 years for HEPSS vs 5 years for Cat-2)
- **Both Cat-2 and HEPSS certificates issued by Gibraltar Finance** (not automatic; case-by-case approval)

⚠️ **Verification path**: Cat-2 and HEPSS thresholds change with the annual budget — re-verify minimum tax, max tax, income cap, fees, and skills test at [gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office) and the consolidated **Qualifying (Category 2) Individuals Rules 2004** / **HEPSS Rules 2008** at [gibraltarlaws.gov.gi](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/) before relying on any figure. Community signal (r/gibraltar 1tkeoa5, Q2 2026) flags **anticipated Cat-2 / residency rule changes**; no statute yet — monitor through Q3 2026.

**Confidence**: HIGH for stamp duty bands and the "no VAT / no IHT / no wealth tax / no CGT" framework (PwC + cross-checked vs Stamp Duties Amendment Act 37/2024). HIGH for Cat-2 (£2M net worth, £118k cap, £37k–£42,380 tax band, £1,168 fee, £42,380 deposit — all cross-confirmed Hassans + Income Tax Office). HIGH for HEPSS (£160k earnings floor, £160k assessable cap, £39,940 fixed annual tax — cross-confirmed multiple Gibraltar professional sources + statutory Rules 2008).

## Section: `--ownership-and-tenure`

**Data sources**: [Gibraltar laws portal](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/) (primary statute lookup); Supreme Court Land Registry; Land Property Services.

**Tenure types** in Gibraltar:

1. **Freehold** — outright ownership; least common in central Gibraltar due to limited land
2. **Crown leasehold** — substantial portion of Gibraltar's developed land is held on **long lease from the Crown** (Government of Gibraltar). Typical lease lengths: 99, 125, or 150 years. Ground rent often nominal.
3. **Private leasehold** — sub-lease from a freeholder (less common)
4. **Commonhold** — rare; verify specific scheme applicability

**Pre-contract checks** (engage Gibraltar-qualified conveyancer/barrister — see § notary section):

- Confirm tenure type with a copy of the title deed
- Confirm **market-class designation** (Open vs Restricted — see § `--foreign-buyer-rules`)
- Crown lease: verify unexpired term, ground rent, service charge, renewal/extension rights, alterations consent regime
- Service charges for apartments (most central Gibraltar stock): confirm budget + reserve fund status
- Any Crown lease covenant restricting sublet / short-let / commercial use

**Affordable housing scheme**: Government-subsidised housing schemes (Mons Calpe Mews, etc.) sit in the **Restricted Market** tier and carry **resale restrictions for 10 years** with 7.5% stamp duty on early resale and a buyback right to Government. Confirm the scheme tier before purchase.

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — tenure-type framework is well-established, but lease-length and ground-rent specifics are property-by-property; require title deed review.

**Regulation**:

**Yield expectations**:
- **Estate-agent aggregates suggest 4–6% gross yield** on Gibraltar apartments — **no primary rental index publicly indexed**. Verify against live Rightmove / Chestertons / Century 21 comps at the **parcel level**; do not treat the 4–6% range as a planning number.

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — regulatory framework confirmed; yield numbers are estate-agent aggregates (no primary index) and must be verified at the parcel level against live comps.

## Section: `--risks`

**Data sources**:
- Government of Gibraltar Department of Environment + Climate Change: [gibraltar.gov.gi/environment](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/environment)
- Seismic: **EMSC / European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre** + USGS — Azores-Gibraltar fault zone monitoring
- Coastal/sea-level: Gibraltar Met Office (royalnavy.mod.uk maintains regional)
- Listing condition: physical surveyor — engage RICS-equivalent (RICS members operate in Gibraltar)

**Flood**: Gibraltar's drainage system was historically limited in some areas; localised flash flooding in heavy rainfall events (typically October–March) has been documented in low-lying areas (Catalan Bay flats, parts of Westside reclaimed land). Verify property elevation + drainage at the parcel level with Gibraltar Land Property Services.

**Seismic**: Gibraltar lies within the **Azores–Gibraltar Fault Zone (AGFZ)** — a moderately active plate boundary. Historical major events include the **1755 Lisbon earthquake (M~8.5–9.0)** with significant Gibraltar impact, and the 1969 Cape St Vincent earthquake (M7.8). Seismic risk for the territory: **MEDIUM-LOW to MEDIUM** (verify at: EMSC historical catalogue + Gibraltar building codes — modern construction post-1980 follows seismic-resistant design but older Old Town / Casemates stock may not).

**Subsidence / geology**: Gibraltar is dominated by Jurassic limestone (Rock of Gibraltar). Limestone karst → potential for **sinkholes, cave systems, and differential settlement**. The Rock has 50+ km of natural and tunnelled caverns. ⚠️ Verify subsidence history at parcel level with a structural survey; common issue in older Town/Upper Rock stock.

**Climate**: Mediterranean — hot summers, mild wet winters. Sea-level rise projection (IPCC AR6, 2021): regional Mediterranean +0.3–0.7m by 2100 (RCP4.5 / 8.5). Properties near Westside reclaimed land + Eastside should factor longer-term sea-level exposure.

**Air quality**: bunkering port emissions + cross-border (Spanish) industrial sources have caused historical AQ issues; Gibraltar Air Quality Monitoring Network reports at [gibraltar.gov.gi/environment](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/environment).

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — seismic and karst risks are well-documented at the territory level; parcel-specific exposure (flood, subsidence) requires survey. Climate projections are IPCC-band consensus.

## Section: `--notary` / Conveyancing

**Data sources**:
- [Supreme Court of Gibraltar](https://www.gibraltarlawcourts.gov.gi/) — civil registry
- [Gibraltar Bar Council](https://www.gibraltarbarcouncil.org/) (verify URL) — qualified conveyancers register

**Process**:

1. **Offer accepted** → instruct a **Gibraltar-qualified barrister or solicitor**. There is no "notary" institution in the civil-law sense — Gibraltar uses common-law conveyancing.
2. **Pre-contract enquiries**: title, planning, search of the **Supreme Court Land Registry**, Crown lease consents (if applicable), **market-class designation** (Open vs Restricted)
3. **Exchange of contracts** with deposit (typically 10%)
4. **Stamp Duty assessment** by HM Commissioner of Stamp Duty under the Stamp Duties Act
5. **Completion** + registration of the new title at the Supreme Court Land Registry
6. **Conveyancing fees**: typically **1–1.5% of price + disbursements** for residential — verify with quoted firm

**Foreign-buyer extras**:
- Gibraltar-based AML/KYC checks under GFSC and Financial Services (Implementation of EU Anti-Money Laundering Directive) Regulations — source of funds documentation mandatory
- Non-EU buyers: residency permit application is **separate from purchase** and runs in parallel (often pre-completion to ensure occupancy)
- Post-Brexit, EU/EEA buyers face the same documentary regime as third-country nationals
- **Restricted-Market eligibility evidence** (3-year residency) required at exchange if the property sits in that tier

**Typical timeline**: 6–12 weeks from offer to completion (longer for complex Crown lease properties).

**Confidence**: HIGH on legal framework (common-law conveyancing, Supreme Court Land Registry registration); MEDIUM on fee ranges (firm-to-firm variation).

## Section: `--frontier-treaty` (UK–EU Gibraltar Treaty)

**Status as of 2026-05-27**:

- Draft text published **26 February 2026** ([UK Government + EU Commission joint publication](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10572/))
- **EU Council greenlight: 1 April 2026** ([Consilium press release](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/04/01/eu-uk-relations-member-states-greenlight-eu-uk-deal-on-gibraltar/))
- **Provisional application date: 15 July 2026** (subject to UK Constitutional Reform and Governance Act 2010 ratification + parallel EU procedures)
- Source: [European Commission proposal IP/26/428](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_428)

**Provisions affecting property buyers**:
- Schengen border checks **relocated from land frontier (La Verja / La Línea) to Gibraltar airport + port** — operated under dual UK/EU control architecture
- Physical fence at land frontier to be removed; routine vehicle / pedestrian checks eliminated
- Customs union between EU and Gibraltar for goods movement
- **Gibraltar residents exempt from EU Entry/Exit System (EES) wet-stamping + ETIAS** (per Government of Gibraltar press release 148/2026)
- **Treaty does NOT grant EU free movement to Gibraltar** — verbatim Government of Gibraltar release 148/2026: _"Those wishing to reside in Gibraltar will continue to be subject to Gibraltar's own immigration laws and procedures."_ Residency permit + Cat-2 / HEPSS / employment / self-sufficiency route still required for occupancy. Responsibility for determining residency remains with the Government of Gibraltar.

**Absent-Cat-2 enforcement caveat**: the treaty does **not** override Gibraltar's domestic Cat-2 / HEPSS residency conditions. Cat-2 holders are required to **maintain approved Gibraltar accommodation for the whole year of assessment** and to physically reside there — relocating the Schengen border to airport/port does **not** relax that physical-presence test. Community signal (r/gibraltar treaty thread Q2 2026, "All that property owned by absent tax residents who now can't be residents…") suggests treaty implementation may tighten **enforcement** against absent-tax-resident Cat-2 holders. Re-confirm Cat-2 occupancy compliance post-15-Jul-2026 with Gibraltar Finance and (if non-trivial) a Gibraltar-licensed tax advisor.

**Verification path** (essential — re-check before purchase):
- [gibraltar.gov.gi/press-releases](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/press-releases) for Government-of-Gibraltar implementation announcements
- [House of Commons Library briefing CBP-10572](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10572/) — UK Parliament background

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — treaty is well-publicised but provisional application is contingent on ratification completion; numbers and operational specifics may shift. Re-verify at gov.gi closer to purchase date.

## Section: `--integrity`

**Anti-hallucination notes specific to Gibraltar**:

1. **Don't conflate with UK**: Gibraltar tax (no VAT, no IHT, no CGT) ≠ UK tax (20% VAT, 40% IHT, CGT). Gibraltar has its own Income Tax Office, NOT HMRC.
2. **Don't conflate with Spain**: Spain's IBI / Plusvalía / Impuesto sobre el Patrimonio do **not** apply in Gibraltar.
3. **Don't conflate Gibraltar pound (GIP) with GBP**: 1:1 peg, but GIP banknotes are **not legal tender in UK** — convert before travel.
4. **Brexit timing**: Gibraltar left EU **31 January 2020** with UK. EU/EEA pre-Brexit access rules no longer apply. Use 2025–2026 primary sources, not 2020-era news.
5. **Frontier Treaty timing**: provisional application **from 15 July 2026** — anything pre-treaty (e.g. EES border checks April 2026) is being superseded; re-verify operational status.
6. **TWO-TIER market is real and load-bearing**: "no nationality restriction" applies to Open Market only. Restricted Market = 3-year prior Gibraltar residency, owner-occupation only, ~50% of stock. Never describe Gibraltar as "no foreign-ownership restrictions" without the Open / Restricted caveat.
7. **Cat-2 net worth**: £2M confirmed verbatim by Hassans citing the Qualifying (Category 2) Individuals Rules 2004 — _"potential applicants must have at least an estimated total net worth in excess of £2 million"_. The Gibraltar Income Tax Office page itself states the income cap (£118k) and tax band (£37k–£42,380) but does not restate the £2M figure publicly — re-confirm at the Gibraltar Finance Centre at application.
8. **HEPSS vs Cat-2 are distinct schemes**: HEPSS = employed senior executives with skills not locally available (£160k earnings floor, £160k assessable cap, £39,940 fixed tax, 3-year non-residency window). Cat-2 = high-net-worth passive residents (£2M net worth, £118k income cap, £37k–£42,380 tax band, 5-year non-residency window). Don't conflate.
9. **No mortgage stamp duty on small loans**: 0.13% on secured sums <£200k vs 0.20% on ≥£200k. Easy to elide vs the property stamp duty bands.
10. **Treaty does NOT relax Cat-2 physical-presence test**: relocating Schengen checks to airport/port has zero effect on Cat-2 / HEPSS accommodation-and-presence conditions. Absent-tax-resident enforcement may in fact tighten post-15-Jul-2026.

## Caveats unique to Gibraltar

- **Two-tier residential property market** (Open / Restricted) — non-residents categorically limited to Open Market; ~50% of stock by unit count is Restricted (3-year prior residency, owner-occupation only)
- **Single-postcode addressing** (GX11 1AA territory-wide) — locator data must be parcel/street-level, postcode is not a discriminator
- **Crown-leasehold ubiquity**: most central Gibraltar developed property is on long Crown lease (99–150yr); freehold is uncommon
- **Affordable Housing buyback**: 7.5% stamp duty + 10-year Government buyback right on subsidised schemes (sits in Restricted Market tier)
- **Tax-residency-of-origin overhang**: UK-domiciled buyers retain UK IHT exposure on Gibraltar property unless domicile changes (not a Gibraltar issue, but commonly misunderstood)
- **Frontier Treaty (15 Jul 2026 provisional)** changes commuting & border friction but does NOT change residency law and does NOT relax Cat-2 physical-presence
- **Cat-2 / HEPSS thresholds change with annual budget** — always re-verify with Gibraltar Finance Centre directly; community signal flags anticipated Cat-2 reform (no statute yet)
- **Self-employment work-permit route is discretionary and frequently refused** — do not rely on a property lease/purchase as evidence of eligibility

## Quirks to know

- **Llanito** local code-switching English/Spanish is everyday; legal/transactional documentation is **English only**
- **British driving licence + UK passport are sufficient** for UK nationals; EU nationals need full residency stack post-Brexit
- **Mains gas does not exist** — LPG cylinders are standard; factor in if relocating from a UK gas-heated home
- **Property surveys**: RICS-registered surveyors do operate in Gibraltar; engaging a UK surveyor on a Gibraltar property may need local-knowledge supplementation (Crown lease, karst geology)
- **No public DVF/DLA-equivalent transaction price database** — comparables come from estate agents and Land Registry filings on request (less open than UK / FR)
