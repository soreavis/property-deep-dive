# Cyprus (CY) — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

**Status**: ✅ fully populated as of 2026-04-26
**Currency**: EUR (RoC, eurozone since 2008)
**Lang**: Greek/Turkish official; English widely used in property/legal

---

## Country profile

Cyprus is a **divided island**: SOUTH = Republic of Cyprus (RoC), EU+eurozone since 2004/2008. NORTH = TRNC (only Turkey recognizes), complex titles for pre-1974 properties. **Focus this playbook on RoC; explicitly flag North title risk for any property anywhere on island.** **CRITICAL**: comprehensive Cyprus Tax Reform took effect **1 January 2026** — many pre-2026 assumptions superseded. **Hydrological year 2024/25 brought the lowest dam inflow of the past ten years** (18.7 MCM in the Water Development Department ten-year inflow table; 4th-lowest of 38 years since dam-inflow records began in 1987/88, and the 8th-driest year for rainfall since records began in 1901, per kitasweather.com, 14 Oct 2025 — secondary). Major-dam storage stood at 10.6% on 3 Nov 2025 and, after a wetter 2025/26 (117.1 MCM inflow), at 35.0% (101.9 of 290.8 MCM) on 3 Nov 2026 (2026 data, source Water Development Department reservoir-storage sheet, https://www.gov.cy/moa-wdd/en/documents/dams-and-off-stream-ponds/reservoir-storage/); water scarcity remains a transaction-level concern. Russian buyer post-2022 sanctions enforcement is heavy in Limassol. **NPL legacy + "Trapped Buyers" crisis** (Amendment Law 110(I)/2025, gazetted 4 Jul 2025) is the single largest CY-specific DD risk on existing developer-encumbered stock.

## Section: `--tax`

### Property Transfer Fees (DLS) — UNCHANGED by 2026 reform

Applied on assessed market value (DLS valuation, not necessarily contract price), progressive:
- **3%** on first €85,000
- **5%** on €85,001–€170,000
- **8%** above €170,000

**Reductions**:
- **VAT-charged properties (typically new builds): 0% transfer fee** — exempt entirely
- **Resale (no VAT): 50% reduction** → effective **1.5% / 2.5% / 4%**
- **No 50% reduction** when the seller acquired the property through a forced-sale procedure under Parts VI and VIA of the Transfer and Mortgage Law 9/1965 — the full 3% / 5% / 8% scale applies
- Scale, VAT exemption and reductions (2026 data, source Department of Lands and Surveys — https://portal.dls.moi.gov.cy/en/application_forms/polisi-antallagi)

### Stamp Duty — `2026 REFORM`

- **FULLY ABOLISHED from 1 Jan 2026**: **Law 239(I)/2025** (ο περί Χαρτοσήμων (Καταργητικός) Νόμος του 2025, Official Gazette Αρ. 5070 of 31 Dec 2025) repeals the Stamp Duty Laws in full — **no real-estate-specific exceptions retained**. Fees that other ministries / services / departments collect by stamps under their own legislation may continue until the competent authorities make new arrangements (2026 data, source Cyprus Tax Department — https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/chartosima + Tax Department Tax Reform 2026 presentation + CyLaw text of Law 239(I)/2025 — https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2025_1_239.pdf)
- Documents drawn up and signed by at least one party **by 31 Dec 2025** stay dutiable under the old Law 19/1963 scale: €0 up to €5,000; €1.50 per €1,000 (0.15%) from €5,001 to €170,000; €2.00 per €1,000 (0.20%) above €170,000; maximum €20,000 (2026 data, source Cyprus Tax Department — https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/chartosima)

### Immovable Property Tax (national)
**ABOLISHED 1 January 2017.** Confirmed still abolished as of 2026; not reinstated by 2026 reform.

### Local Immovable Property Tax (municipal/community)
- **Still in force.** Annual, paid to municipality based on 1980 (1/1/1980) value
- ~**€55–€185/year** for residential (est. range, varies by municipality — verify per locality)
- **Sewerage and stormwater fees** are billed separately from the municipal fee. In Limassol district the **District Local Government Organisation (EOA Lemesos, Law 37(I)/2022)** took over the Sewerage Board of Limassol-Amathus, the community sewerage boards and the Water Board of Lemesos on **1 Jul 2024**; the fee falls on the registered owner as at 1 January and is based on the DLS general valuation at 1 Jan 2021 prices (2026 data, source EOA Lemesos — https://eoalemesos.org.cy/en/who-we-are + https://eoalemesos.org.cy/en/faqs-sewerage) — check the billing body and basis for other districts

### Capital Gains Tax — `2026 REFORM`

- **Rate: 20%** on gains from CY-situs immovable property + shares in CY companies directly owning CY immovable property — UNCHANGED
- **Lifetime general exemption per individual: €30,000** (up from €17,086 — **2026 reform**)
- **Private residence exemption: €150,000** if owner-occupied as main residence for ≥5 years in total (≥10 years if it is not the owner's first principal-residence disposal), on land of up to 1,500 m², and sold within 1 year of moving out; split by the seller's ownership share (up from €85,430)
- **Agricultural land (disposed of by a farmer) exemption: €50,000** (was €25,629 — **2026 reform**)
- **Overall lifetime exemption cap: €150,000** (up from €85,430 — **2026 reform**)
- All four uplifts (general / agricultural / principal residence / overall cap) enacted by **Amending Law 242(I)/2025**, Official Gazette No. 5070 of 31 Dec 2025 (2026 data, source Cyprus Tax Department — https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/kefalaioychika-kerdi/) (2026-08-07 verified, re-verified 2026-10-23; prior secondary cross-checks: Sovereign / Harneys / Chambers&Co / IBCCS Tax / Mondaq)
- Exemption from CGT for property acquired between 16 Jul 2015 and 31 Dec 2016 still applies (one-off historical relief)
- **0.4% Central Agency for Equal Distribution of Burdens fee** (separate from CGT; paid by the seller, collected by the Tax Department): on the sale price of property for which the DLS has set a general-valuation value, and on sales of shares in unlisted companies that directly or indirectly own such property (on the last DLS value attributable to the shares) — Law 172(I)/2022, from 18 Nov 2022; the first version (Law 12(I)/2021) applied from 22 Feb 2021; exemptions apply (2026 data, source Cyprus Tax Department FAQ — https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/kentrikos-foreas-isotimis-katanomis-varon/sychnes-erotiseis-gia-to-telos-kentrikoy-forea-isotimis-katanomis-varon)

### Rental Income — `2026 REFORM`

- **SDC on rental income: ABOLISHED from 1 Jan 2026** as part of the Tax Reform package in force 1 Jan 2026. (Pre-2026: 3% × 75% of gross rent = effective 2.25% for CY tax-resident AND domiciled individuals; non-doms exempt under 17-year regime) (2026-08-07 verified, source: Ministry of Finance / Cyprus Tax Department — enacting law: ο περί Έκτακτης Εισφοράς για την Άμυνα της Δημοκρατίας (Τροποποιητικός) (Αρ. 4) Νόμος του 2025, Law 245(I)/2025, Official Gazette Αρ. 5070 of 31 Dec 2025, in force 1 Jan 2026 — it deletes the rent definitions, replaces s.3 with a dividends-only charge and deletes «ή τα ενοίκια» from the s.4(2) proviso (2026-10-21 verified, source CyLaw https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2025_1_245.pdf); **Law 242(I)/2025 amends the Capital Gains Tax Law and is NOT the SDC instrument**)
- **Income tax** (progressive, above) still applies to rental profit — deductions: 20% of gross rents from buildings (expense allowance), capital allowances and loan interest on the rented property (2026 data, source Cyprus Tax Department form IR59 2026 — https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/29/2026/01/IR59_2026_English__.pdf)
- **GHS contribution (GeSY)**: **2.65% on gross rents**; GHS contributions are payable on total annual income up to €180,000 (2026 data, source Cyprus Tax Department guide to the 2025 income tax return — https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/167/2026/06/Guide-for-completion-of-tax-return-2025.pdf + Health Insurance Organisation)

### VAT on Property — partially `2024 REFORM`

- **Standard 19%** on new-build sales
- **From 1 Sep 2026** (Κ.Δ.Π. 103/2026): a building and its plot supplied **before its first establishment** is taxed at 19%. "First establishment" = the first use after delivery or construction that continues on a systematic basis (owner-occupation, own use, letting or other use); "first use" = systematic use for at least **18 months**. Buildings whose planning-permit application was filed before 1 May 2004 are outside this rule
- **Reduced 5% for primary residence** (Law 42(I)/2023 + Law 55(I)/2024):
  - **5% on the first 130 m²** of buildable area, **up to €350,000 of value**, provided the **total transaction value is ≤ €475,000** and the **total buildable area ≤ 190 m²** — the 5% covers the first 130 m² only
  - Above 190 m² OR above €475k: **standard 19% on entire amount** ← cliff
  - Large families: total-area limit +15 m² per child beyond the third (value caps unchanged). Persons with disability: 5% on the first 190 m² whatever the total area, up to €475,000
  - **10-year claw-back**: a beneficiary who stops using the home as their residence within 10 years must notify the Tax Commissioner within 30 days and pay the 5% / 19% difference for the period not owner-occupied (exceptions: death, transfer to a child)
- **Transitional rule (VAT Law art. 63)**: homes whose planning permit was issued, or applied for, by **31 Oct 2023** get 5% on the first **200 m²**. Where the building permit was issued by 31 Dec 2024, the responsible declaration had to reach the Tax Commissioner by **15 Jun 2026**; where it was issued after 1 Jan 2025 or will not be issued by 31 Dec 2026, the Commissioner accepts declarations, and **Law 109(I)/2026** (Official Gazette Παρ. Ι(Ι) Αρ. 5089 of 24 Apr 2026) lets the Commissioner examine those held up by planning-authority delays until **31 Dec 2026**
- Rules above: 2026 data, source Cyprus Tax Department — https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/f-p-a-kai-akiniti-idioktisia + announcement of 4 May 2026 (https://www.gov.cy/oikonomia/exetasi-ypefthynon-diloseon-gia-tin-epivoli-meiomenou-syntelesti-fpa-5-gia-tin-agora-i-anegersi-katoikias-apo-ton-eforo-forologias-mechri-31-dekemvriou-2026/) + CyLaw text of Law 109(I)/2026 (https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2026_1_109.pdf) + consolidated VAT Law, Fifth Schedule Table C (claw-back)
- Resale (second-hand) properties past their first establishment: NO VAT (transfer fees only)

## Section: `--rental`

### STR / Airbnb — self-catering accommodation register (Deputy Ministry of Tourism)

**Register of Self-Service Accommodation** (Μητρώο Αυτοεξυπηρετούμενων Καταλυμάτων), kept by the **Deputy Ministry of Tourism** (Υφυπουργείο Τουρισμού — still a deputy ministry per gov.cy/tourism, read 2026-11-03):
- Entry: https://www.gov.cy/tourism/ — one online application per unit (villa, house or apartment): https://www.gov.cy/en/service/application-solemn-declaration-for-grant-of-special-label-and-registration-number-for-self-service-sharing-economy-accommodation-establishments/
- **Legal basis**: Regulation of the Establishment and Operation of Hotels and Tourist Accommodation Law, N. 34(I)/2019 — Part IIIA (art. 16A) and Fifth Schedule, inserted by N. 9(I)/2020 (Official Gazette Αρ. 4741, 7 Feb 2020) and amended by N. 7(I)/2022 and N. 11(I)/2023 ([CyLaw consolidated text, amendments to N. 110(I)/2026](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2019_1_34/full.html))
- **Mandatory registration**: since 7 Feb 2020 for new units; units already operating then had 3 years to register (art. 16A(2)), which Airbnb's Cyprus help page dates as the duty applying from 6 Feb 2023. After that transition neither the owner nor a platform may advertise a unit without a registration number
- **Fee**: €222 per unit per three years (Deputy Ministry information note, 2021; same figure in Cyprus Mail 27 Oct 2026); the registration permit is valid 3 years and is renewed every 3 years, applying within the 3 months before expiry (Fifth Schedule)
- **Registration number must appear in every advertisement and promotion and in all related transactions** (art. 16A(2)) (Airbnb, Booking, etc.)
- **Penalties**: operating without a registration permit, or after it is revoked: up to 1 year imprisonment or a fine up to €5,000, or both; if the offence continues after conviction, up to €200 for each further day (art. 22(1)(b); restated in the Deputy Ministry press release of 26 Apr 2025, https://www.gov.cy/tourism/en/mi-katigoriopoiimeno/registration-of-self-service-accommodations-in-the-registry-of-the-deputy-ministry-of-tourism/)
- **EU Regulation (EU) 2024/1028** (short-term rental data sharing, applies from 20 May 2026): Part IIIB (art. 16B, added by N. 184(I)/2025) requires hosts and platforms to comply and names the Deputy Ministry of Tourism as competent authority
- **Register vs market**: 4,765 registered units (Apr 2023) → 8,375 (mid-May 2025) → 8,464 (6 May 2026, Audit Office special report on the Deputy Ministry of Tourism, 2 Jul 2026) → 8,478 (29 Jun 2026, Deputy Ministry reply); ~12,000–13,000 units estimated to be offered (parliamentary briefing reported by Cyprus Mail, 26 May 2025). Of 20 online listings the Audit Office could identify, 6 held a valid matching registration, 10 had none and 4 showed an invalid or mismatched number; inspectors filed 88 complaints against unregistered premises in the year to mid-2026 (2026 data, source Audit Office report and Deputy Ministry reply as reported by Politis, Cyprus Mail and KYPE/cyprustimes.com; 2023–2025 series via karakontis.com — secondary)
- **Overnight-stay fees**: the Deputy Ministry note says rented self-catering units pay income tax / VAT to the State and, where applicable, overnight-stay fees (τέλη διανυκτέρευσης) to the local authority — confirm with the municipality or community council (2021 data, source Deputy Ministry of Tourism information note — rates/figures may have changed since); the note lists no national tourist tax
- **Rental-income taxation**: see `--tax` § Rental Income — **SDC on rental income abolished from 1 Jan 2026** (2026 Tax Reform package; SDC amending Law 245(I)/2025, Official Gazette Αρ. 5070 of 31 Dec 2025, in force 1 Jan 2026); income tax + GHS still apply

## Section: `--risks`

### Seismic — HIGH for southern coast

- **Tectonics**: Cyprus Arc subduction (African plate under Anatolian) + **Paphos Transform Fault**. Larnaka thrust, Limassol thrust faults documented
- **Hazard map (SHARE / ESHM13 model, Giardini et al. 2013, as used in the NRA-CY national risk assessment, Cyprus University of Technology project 004/2018)**: PGA for the 475-year return period (10% in 50 years) runs from ~0.20–0.30 g around Nicosia and the north to ~0.40–0.55 g along the Paphos–Limassol coast (read off the map legend); the deck sums it up as 0.3–0.5 g, above the 0.15–0.25 g design values of the Eurocode 8 National Annex zoning map — high European hazard (2020 data, source ETEK / Cyprus University of Technology NRA-CY deck — rates/figures may have changed since)
- **Historical M6+**:
  - **11 Jan 2022**: Mw 6.6 west of Paphos; USGS places it 48 km WNW of Polis at 21 km depth (USGS event us7000gaqu); described as the largest in the Mediterranean Sea since the 2003 Boumerdès quake (Wikipedia — secondary)
  - **9 Oct 1996**: Mw 6.8, 36 km SW of Paphos (USGS event usp0007r4u); the NRA-CY deck also gives Mw 6.8, with limited building damage and 20 injuries
  - **1953**: Mw 6.1, extensive damage in Paphos district, 40 fatalities, 100 injuries (NRA-CY deck, ETEK 2020)
  - **1222 AD**: Mw 6.8 in the NRA-CY seismicity catalogue (ETEK 2020); generated tsunami, devastated island
- **Building code**: Cyprus adopts Eurocode 8 with a National Annex; the NRA-CY exposure model treats reinforced-concrete buildings from after 1992 as earthquake-resistant design and older stock as non-ERD (2020 data, source ETEK / NRA-CY deck, citing Chrysostomou et al. 2014) — rates/figures may have changed since — critical DD point for older Limassol/Paphos villas
- ETEK seismic risk assessment: https://etek.org.cy/uploads/Blog/2020/67600d021P.pdf

### Flood
- **Pedieos River (Nicosia)** — Basin 10 — highest flood risk basin: 38 floods recorded in urban Nicosia 1960–2012, 3 of them from the river itself and the rest from urban runoff (2016 data, source Pedieos River Basin Adaptation Plan, Ecologic — https://www.ecologic.eu/sites/default/files/publication/2016/rbap_pedieos_final.pdf — rates/figures may have changed since); the Politiko–Nicosia Municipality stretch of the Pedieos is a WDD Area of Potential Significant Flood Risk (Politis, 23 Oct 2026, citing WDD); major events 2010 + 2014 inundated hospitals, schools, intercity roads in northern Nicosia
- **Coastal**: Larnaca Airport, Vasilikos/Dhekelia power stations, Larnaca/Paphos desalination plants in low-lying surge zones
- **Map authority**: **Water Development Department (Τμήμα Αναπτύξεως Υδάτων / WDD)** — the Cyprus competent authority under the Floods Directive 2007/60/EC, publishing flood hazard/risk mapping and the WDD geodata portal ([gov.cy/moa-wdd](https://www.gov.cy/moa-wdd/)). Civil Defence (Πολιτική Άμυνα, [gov.cy/moi/politiki-amyna](https://www.gov.cy/moi/politiki-amyna)) is response/preparedness, not the map publisher (2026 data, source Water Development Department; 2026-09-18 verified)
- **Flood-risk areas (APSFRs)**: WDD's Hydrology Service added 5 Areas of Potential Significant Flood Risk in October 2026, taking the national total from 38 to 43; the new areas are already in WDD's interactive flood-risk maps, but the updated list was not yet formally published. The areas sit in the urban centres (none in mountain areas) and are mostly exposed to river flash floods. WDD's historical database (2011 and 2020 assessments) counts 588 major flood incidents 1859–2018 and treats coastal and groundwater flooding as minor threats (2026 data, source Politis, 23 Oct 2026, citing WDD — secondary). An APSFR is a district-level designation: check the parcel itself on the WDD flood maps

### Wildfire — CRITICAL & WORSENING

- **2021 Limassol wildfires (3–4 July 2021)**: the worst fire on record at the time, ahead of Vavla 2000 (52 km²); since surpassed by the 2025 Malia fire. Burned **~55 km² (5,500 ha)** in Larnaca/Limassol foothills (Arakapas/Ora/Odou/Eptagonia/Vavatsinia villages). **4 dead** (Egyptian agricultural workers near Odou), scores of homes burnt across ten communities (2021 data, source Cyprus Mail, 6 Jul 2021; Copernicus EMS EMSR515 mapped the burnt area, but its activation page gives no figure) — rates/figures may have changed since. National 2021 total: 111 forest fires, 6,612 ha (2021 data, source Department of Forests via JRC/EFFIS "Forest Fires in Europe, Middle East and North Africa 2023") — rates/figures may have changed since
- **2023 Paramytha/Limassol fire (4 Aug 2023)**: 882 ha (8.8 km²) burned, mostly wild vegetation, shrubland and crops; livestock facilities destroyed and houses damaged; the most destructive fire of 2023 (2023 data, source Department of Forests via JRC/EFFIS 2023 report) — rates/figures may have changed since
- **2023 season**: 131 forest fires over the full year, 2,216 ha burned, 8 of them over 50 ha (2023 data, source Department of Forests via JRC/EFFIS "Forest Fires in Europe, Middle East and North Africa 2023" — https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/bitstream/JRC139704/JRC139704_01.pdf) — rates/figures may have changed since; the mid-September tally was 83 fires and 2,073 ha (IAWF, citing the Department of Forests)
- **2025 Malia/Limassol fire (23 Jul 2025)**: broke out near Malia during a heatwave and spread through the wine-producing villages north of Limassol (Souni, Lofou among those hit); **2 dead**. Burnt area ~100 km² on the Copernicus Sentinel-3 burn-scar image of 25 Jul 2025 (EU Space Programme, europa.eu); 125 km², 211 homes completely destroyed and 440 properties affected per Politis' one-year review (Jul 2026 — secondary for the damage counts). Either figure is about twice the 55 km² of the 2021 fire. Cyprus recorded a record annual high in wildfire emissions in 2025 (2025 data, source Copernicus C3S European State of the Climate 2025)
- **Implication**: any property within 200 m of pine forest, agricultural fringe, or chaparral on Limassol/Larnaca foothills = HIGH wildfire premium / insurance flag / defensive-space mandatory

### Water Scarcity — UNPRECEDENTED 2025

- **2025/26 drought low**: dam inflows at their lowest since hydrological records began in 1901, and reserves down to 13.7% of capacity in February 2026 (2026 data, source EU Space Programme / Copernicus, 25 Feb 2026, citing national authorities). The 2024/25 hydrological year brought only 18.7 MCM of dam inflow, the lowest of the 2016/17–2025/26 seasons in WDD's inflow table (2026 data, source WDD)
- **Recovery since**: 2025/26 inflow reached 117.1 MCM, 96.4 MCM of it in February–May 2026; the 18 major dams held **101.9 of 290.8 MCM = 35.0%** on 3 Nov 2026, against 10.6% a year earlier (2026 data, source WDD daily dam-storage bulletin — https://www.gov.cy/moa-wdd/documents/tamieytires-neroy-fragmata-ydatodexamenes/plirotita-fragmaton/). Storage swings sharply from year to year: re-check the bulletin at offer stage
- **Kouris reservoir (largest, 115 MCM)**: 36.4% (41.8 MCM) on 3 Nov 2026 vs 9.6% a year earlier (2026 data, source WDD); it had fallen to 12.2% in February 2026 (Guardian, 16 Feb 2026 — secondary), re-exposing the submerged church of St Nicholas
- **Limassol peak demand**: 96,000 m³/day (2025 data, source Smart Water Magazine, citing WDD — rates/figures may have changed since). The Moni mobile plant (15,000 m³/day, built with UAE-donated units) began phased operation in July 2025, reached full output in December 2025 and is extended to 31 Dec 2029 by a Special Development Order; Kissonerga (12,000 m³/day) started in August 2025 and Limassol Port (10,000 m³/day) reached full output in April 2026 (2026 data, source Philenews)
- **5 permanent desalination plants**: Dhekelia, Vasilikos and Larnaca 60,000 m³/day each, Episkopi 40,000, Paphos 15,000 (20,000 from March 2026) — combined 235,000 m³/day (2026 data, source Philenews; the plant list matches the WDD weekly water bulletin). Desalination covered ~80% of drinking-water supply in March 2026 (2026 data, source Cyprus Mail, citing WDD). On 2 Sep 2026 the Cabinet approved permanent plants at Dhekelia (80,000 m³/day), Ayia Thekla (30,000) and Polis Chrysochous (10,000), alongside a planned 60,000 m³/day Limassol plant: 180,000 m³/day combined (2026 data, source Cyprus Mail)
- **€31M emergency package** approved by the Council of Ministers on 23 Jan 2026, within water projects worth more than €200M; households asked to cut use by 10%, farmers told to expect 30% less irrigation water than in 2025 (2026 data, source Euronews, 26 Jan 2026 — secondary)
- **Property impact**: New builds in Paphos/Limassol/Famagusta-RoC face increasing connection lead times; rural off-grid wells often dry. Higher water bills (desalinated 3–4× reservoir cost). Cisterns/rainwater harvesting going from optional to standard

Demolition/major renovation triggers DLI notification + licensed remover. **Buyer DD: visual + sample-test if pre-1985 build**
- **Planning permit verification — CRITICAL CY-SPECIFIC RISK**:
  - **"Trapped Buyers" crisis**: 9,497 buyers remain trapped without title transfer (developer mortgages, planning violations, missing roads/infrastructure): 5,417 of them have no title deed issued at all, largely for missing final town-planning or building permits, and most of the remaining 4,080 paid in full but are blocked by the developer's mortgage on the land (2025 data, source Cyprus Mail, 14 May 2025, citing official figures — rates/figures may have changed since)
  - **Transfer and Mortgage of Immovable Property (Amendment) Law 110(I)/2025** (gazetted 4 Jul 2025, Official Gazette Αρ. 5045): rebuilds the Part VIB trapped-buyer transfer route struck down in Civil Appeal 285/2018 (20 Jun 2024) — covers sale contracts deposited at the District Lands Office by 31 Dec 2014 (or concluded by then and deposited under a court order applied for by 31 Dec 2024) where a separate title deed exists; the prior mortgagee's written consent is now required, or (price fully paid) a court order on an application filed within 45 days of refusal finding the refusal abusive and unjustified; applications pending before the law proceed only if the title deed issues within 2 years 8 months of commencement ([Law 110(I)/2025 text](http://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2025_1_110.pdf)) (2025 data, source Official Gazette of the Republic of Cyprus)

## Foreign-buyer rules

- **EU/EEA/Swiss**: no restrictions since 1 May 2009
- **Non-EU citizens** (incl. UK post-Brexit, Russians, Israelis, Lebanese, etc.) **and foreign companies or companies controlled by non-EU nationals**: **permission required** under the Acquisition of Immovable Property (Aliens) Law, Cap. 109 — a Council of Ministers consent given through the District Officer
  - Limit: **one plot or unplotted land of up to 4,000 m²** to build an owner-occupied dwelling, **OR up to two units**, which may be at different stages of development (2 dwellings, OR 1 dwelling + 1 shop ≤100 m², OR 1 dwelling + 1 office ≤250 m²). Couples get one joint permission and the limit applies to the couple in total
  - Apply on **form COMM 145** to the District Administration of the district where the property lies; **no fee**, processing **2–3 weeks** (2026 data, source Ministry of Interior — https://www.gov.cy/moi/en/documents/purchasing-property)
  - **Cap. 109 amendment — pending, NOT enacted** (news-tier, confidence LOW): four private-member bills — two AKEL, one cross-party DIKO/DISY/DIPA, one DISY bill on how applications are filed — were deferred at the last minute before an April 2026 plenum vote and reopened in the House Interior Committee on 3 Sep 2026. The DIKO/DISY/DIPA bill would limit a third-country national to one home or apartment on a single plot, require at least 51% Cypriot / EU / EEA control of acquiring legal entities and ban forest and agricultural land; the AKEL bills would exempt one home up to 200 m², one shop up to 200 m² or one office up to 300 m² from Cabinet approval and ban forest and agricultural land and purchases next to the ceasefire line or critical infrastructure. At the 22 Oct 2026 session no comprehensive government bill had been tabled, MPs pressed for companies to be covered, the government had 15 days to submit supplementary proposals and the committee chair aimed to finish by the end of 2026; no plenum vote (Cyprus Property Buyers news 2 Sep 2026 + Cyprus Mail 22 Oct 2026 + Politis 23 Oct 2026, secondary). If enacted, the limits above change — verify on parliament.cy and in the Official Gazette Παράρτημα Ι(Ι)
  - Permit normally granted; refusals rare except for sanctions, criminal record, or restricted military zones
- **Russian buyers post-2022**: subject to EU sanctions Reg. 833/2014 (Art. 5h as amended in 2025 — flagged to regulated entities by CySEC Circular C728 of 23 Sep 2025); OFAC secondary; banks require enhanced CDD; many CY banks no longer onboard Russian-passport new clients regardless of residency. ## Country-specific quirks

### TRNC vs RoC — North Title Risk (CRITICAL)

- **Pre-1974 Greek-Cypriot-owned land in TRNC**: ECtHR Loizidou v. Turkey (1996) + Cyprus v. Turkey (2001) ruled **Greek-Cypriot owners retain full legal title**; Turkey held responsible for property rights violations
- **TRNC Immovable Property Commission (IPC)**: semi-judicial body since 2006; ECtHR-recognized as "domestic remedy" but **does not extinguish original Greek-Cypriot title**
- **TRNC titles ("kocan")** — three categories:
  - **Pre-74 Turkish** (safest, originated before 1974)
  - **TMD/Exchange** (allocated to Turkish-Cypriot displaced from south, contested)
  - **Foreign** (granted post-74, highest legal exposure)
- **Buyer in TRNC at risk of**: (a) civil claim by Greek-Cypriot owner in RoC courts (enforceable in other EU member states — CJEU Apostolides v Orams, C-420/07, 28 Apr 2009, decided under the Brussels I Regulation (EC) No 44/2001), (b) inability to sell to EU buyer, (c) reunification scenario revisits all post-74 transfers
- **DO NOT BUY IN TRNC** unless title is Pre-74 Turkish-Cypriot AND lawyer (RoC-qualified, not just TRNC) has cleared the chain. **Preferably avoid TRNC entirely for due-diligence-grade buyers**

### Buffer Zone (UN Buffer / Green Line)
UN-administered, ~180 km long; only a few metres wide in parts of old Nicosia and a few kilometres wide elsewhere (UNFICYP — https://unficyp.unmissions.org/about-buffer-zone). Property within or immediately adjacent has restricted access, no resale market.

### British Sovereign Base Areas (Akrotiri & Dhekelia)
- **British Overseas Territory** (Akrotiri SBA near Limassol; Dhekelia SBA near Larnaca). Total ~254 km²
- Civilian residents (mostly Cypriot) — property regime mostly mirrors RoC but governed by SBA Administration; SBA courts; SBA Customs
- **SBA Land Registry separate from DLS RoC**

### Famagusta / Varosha
- **Varosha** (Famagusta beach district) — ghost-town since 1974; partial reopening announced October 2020 by TRNC, condemned by RoC + UN. **Properties remain titled to pre-74 Greek-Cypriot owners**

### Regional ethno-economic tinge
- **Limassol**: Russian/CIS HNW ("Limassolgrad"); growing post-2022 due to "good Russian" relocations; tech/forex/fintech (post-Wirecard/CySEC migration)
- **Paphos**: British retiree concentration; Israeli buyers growing 2022+
- **Nicosia**: Greek-Cypriot middle class; least foreign-buyer presence; lowest price growth
- **Larnaca**: mixed; airport hub; lowest entry prices on coast

### Pre-1974 deeds in RoC
Some properties in RoC have pre-1974 Turkish-Cypriot owners (now under "Custodian for Turkish-Cypriot Properties"). Cannot be freely sold; rented out by State. **Buyer DD: confirm seller is not "Custodian-administered"**.
