# Luxembourg (LU) — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

**Status**: ✅ fully populated as of 2026-04-26
**Currency**: EUR (eurozone founder)
**Lang**: Luxembourgish / French / German official (notary acts most commonly French; sometimes German; occasionally Lëtzebuergesch); **English not an official notarial language**

---

## Country profile

Luxembourg is a small Grand Duchy (~691k pop: 690,959 on 1 Jan 2026, +1.3% y/y — 2026 data, source STATEC) with the most expensive property market in Europe at peak (mid-2022; Switzerland/Monaco have since edged ahead). **Sharp 2022-24 correction: −16.3% nominal on the STATEC hedonic housing index from the Q3 2022 peak to the Q1 2024 trough** (2022–24 data, source STATEC–Observatoire de l'Habitat, Logement en chiffres n°17). Annual prices −9.1% in 2023, −5.2% in 2024, +1.6% in 2025; Q2 2026 −2.2% y/y and +1.1% q/q, the y/y dip being a base effect after the Q2 2025 rush ahead of expiring tax measures (2025–26 data, source STATEC, Logement en chiffres Q4 2025 and Q2 2026). Non-resident cross-border workers = **~237,200** in 2025 (annual average; 230,200 of them salaried: FR 124,900, DE 53,400, BE 51,900) out of 522,200 domestic jobs (computed: 237.2k ÷ 522.2k ≈ 45% of domestic employment; salaried 230.2k ÷ 490.9k ≈ 47%) (2025 data, source STATEC, Le Luxembourg en chiffres 2026) — but commuters live in FR/DE/BE precisely because LU housing is unaffordable, so they don't pressure rental demand. Bëllegen Akt registration-duty credit: **€40k per acquirer in force** (permanent cap in Art. 6 of the loi modifiée du 30 juillet 2002 since July 2025); **bill n°8806** (deposited 24 Jul 2026, in Commission des Finances, not voted as of its 13 Oct 2026 dossier update) would raise it permanently to **€45k** for notarial deeds passed from 16 Jul 2026 (2026 data, source chd.lu dossier 8806 and gouvernement.lu release of 16 Jul 2026). Major tax-base reform (IFON + IMOB in Bill 8082A, INOL in Bill 8082B) still in parliamentary committee as of Oct 2026; ministers' timing for 8082A: in force 2028, first real tax year 2030. Cross-border commuter dynamic, trilingual notary-act language reality, and Esch-Belval contamination-legacy zones are key country-specific quirks.

## Section: `--tax`

### Transfer tax on purchase (acquisition)

- **Standard total 7%** = **6% droit d'enregistrement + 1% droit de transcription**; the 6% is 5% majoré de 2/10ème (statutory basis listed by guichet.lu « Droits d'enregistrement »: **Loi modifiée du 7 août 1920 portant majoration des droits d'enregistrement, de timbre, de succession, etc.**) (2026-10-23 verified, source: guichet.lu Bëllegen Akt page + « Droits d'enregistrement »)
- **PENDING — VEFA built-share exemption (bill n°8807, not voted)**: for an off-plan (VEFA, Code civil art. 1601-3) purchase by one or more natural persons, the value of the constructions already built at signing would be exempt from the 6% + 1%, so the duties fall on the land value only, where the construction is not more than 80% complete, it is assigned to residential use as stated in the notarial deed, and the notary certifies these conditions in the deed. Window = notarial deeds passed 16 Jul 2026 to 16 Jul 2029; deeds passed from 16 Jul 2026 up to entry into force get the duties recalculated on a written request the notary files for the parties with the AED Director within the two-year limit of art. 61, 1° of the loi modifiée du 22 frimaire an VII (bill Art. 3). The government release of 16 Jul 2026 limits it to a main residence; the deposited bill text requires residential use only — buy-to-let eligibility unsettled until enactment. Deposited 24 Jul 2026, in the Commission des Finances (chd.lu dossier last updated 12 Oct 2026: four professional-chamber opinions, no Conseil d'État opinion, no vote). Until enactment the 7% is due on the land plus the constructions existing at signing (2026 data, source: gouvernement.lu release 16 Jul 2026 + chd.lu dossier 8807 + bill 8807 deposit text)
- **LU-Ville surtaxe communale** (non-residential only): **+3% of the price** on top of 7% for acquisitions of buildings classed for commercial, mixed or any other non-housing use located in Luxembourg-Ville, e.g. retail-on-ground + apartments above, offices, garages (2026-10-23 verified, source: guichet.lu « Droits d'enregistrement », page last modified 27.06.2018; the former '+3.6% (mixed-use)' split is not on that page). Building plots in residential zones where the buyer does NOT commit to building within 5 years: data not publicly available on guichet.lu — verify with notaire. **Purely-residential acquisitions are EXEMPT** (ALJB 2023 practitioner deck, secondary: surtax 'sauf pour les immeubles destinés à du logement'; the same deck also lists Mamer) — verify with notaire if any commercial element.
- **Bëllegen Akt** ("cheap act") tax credit:
  - Increased from €30k → **€40,000 per natural person** (€80k per couple, computed 2 × €40,000) by Law 2024; €40,000 is the amount in force (2026-10-23 verified, source: AED pfi.public.lu « Bëllegen Akt » page citing the Loi du 3 juillet 2025, Mémorial A n°279 du 4 juillet 2025; guichet.lu)
  - **PENDING — raise to €45,000 per acquirer (bill n°8806, not voted)**: announced in the 'Booster fir de Wunnengsbau' package (government release 16 Jul 2026: "porté de 40.000 euros à 45.000 euros par personne physique ... applicable à partir du 16 juillet 2026") and in the 2027 budget release (7 Oct 2026). Bill 8806 replaces '40 000' with '45 000' in Art. 6 of the loi modifiée du 30 juillet 2002; its Art. 2 applies it to notarial deeds passed from 16 Jul 2026; per the bill commentary a buyer who has already used part or all of the €40,000 gets the top-up on a later purchase; deeds signed before entry into force get the increase on a request to the AED. Deposited 24 Jul 2026, in the Commission des Finances (chd.lu dossier last updated 13 Oct 2026: four professional-chamber opinions, no Conseil d'État opinion, no vote). Fiche financière: about €15m a year. If enacted: €90,000 per couple (computed, 2 × €45,000); covers the 7% duties on a price up to ~€642,857 per buyer (computed, €45,000 / 7%; the release says "environ 640.000 euros") (2026 data, source: gouvernement.lu + chd.lu dossier 8806 + bill 8806 deposit text)
  - Amendment trail: **Loi du 22 mai 2024** (initial 30k→40k temporary); **Loi du 4 avril 2025** (extended temporary window to 30 Jun 2025); **made PERMANENT for deeds executed from 1 July 2025 by Loi du 3 juillet 2025** (project deposited June 2025) (2026-05-27 verified, source: gouvernement.lu / Legilux)
  - At €40k/person, effectively wipes out transfer tax on first ~€570k per buyer (€40k / 7% ≈ €571k). Couple buying ~€1.14M pays only the €100 minimum duty the AED always collects (guichet.lu)
  - Conditions: principal residence, occupied personally within 2 years of the deed (4 years for a building plot or a building under construction) and for a continuous period of at least 2 years; LU-resident OR non-resident who undertakes to move in (third-country residents get the credit only once they produce a Luxembourg residence certificate) — no nationality restriction but must occupy; second homes, weekend houses and buildings for rental or commerce are excluded (2026-10-23 verified, source: guichet.lu EN + FR)
  - Source: https://guichet.public.lu/en/citoyens/aides/logement-construction/aides-indirectes/credit-impot-actes-notaries.html

### Annual property tax (impôt foncier) — REFORM IN FLIGHT

- **Currently very low** — est. €100-€500/yr typical for residential, often <€200 in Luxembourg City (practitioner wisdom, not data: no consolidated official table — compute from the ACD unit value and the commune's rate published each year in the Mémorial B). Formula: unit value (1 Jan 1941 rental values, indexed) × assessment rate × communal rate (2026-10-23 verified, source: guichet.lu « Property tax »)
- **Reform (Bill 8082A)**:
  - 17 July 2025: 66 amendments accelerating IMOB/IFON
  - **IMOB (mobilisation tax on unbuilt building land)**: not yet voted (Bill 8082A in commission as of Oct 2026); per the 17 Jul 2025 commission presentation the first bulletin would go to owners in 2030 and the progressive rate applies for the first time only after five years
  - **New IFON (revalued land tax): entry into force 2028, test year 2029, first real tax year 2030 (ministers' timing in commission, 17 Jul 2025) — Bill 8082A still in commission as of Oct 2026, no plenary vote**
  - **INOL (vacant-housing tax)**: split off into separate Bill 8082B on 3 Jul 2025, still in commission and dependent on Bill 8086 (national and communal building and housing registers) — no date set; data not publicly available — verify at chd.lu dossier 8082B
- **Practical**: 2026 buyers still pay legacy near-trivial impôt foncier; **budget for a real rise late-decade**

### Capital Gains Tax — corrected from common assumption

- **Principal residence**: fully exempt regardless of holding period or gain — unchanged; the sale must still be declared (2026-10-23 verified, source: guichet.lu « Déclarer la vente ou l'échange d'un bien immobilier »)
- **Other property**:
  - Held **≤5 years (speculative gain, Art. 99bis LIR)**: ordinary progressive rate, max 42% before the employment-fund surcharge; 45.78% at the 9% surcharge band (computed, 42% × 1.09) (2026-10-23 verified, source: impotsdirects.public.lu « Vente d'un immeuble » + « Fonds pour l'emploi »)
  - Held **>5 years (bénéfice de cession, Art. 99ter LIR)**: half the global rate, max **21%**; **22.89%** with the 9% employment-fund surcharge (computed, 21% × 1.09), plus the 1.4% dependency-insurance contribution on the gain where the seller is in the dependency-insurance circle. The acquisition price is revalued with the ACD coefficient table and the gain is reduced by a ten-year allowance of €50,000 (€100,000 for jointly taxed spouses/partners) (2026-10-23 verified, source: impotsdirects.public.lu « Vente d'un immeuble », « Fonds pour l'emploi », « Assurance dépendance »)
  - **Expired temporary regime**: quarter rate (max 10.5%) on bénéfices de cession realised **1 Jan 2024 → 30 Jun 2025**, and a 2-year (instead of 5-year) speculation threshold for disposals **1 Jan → 30 Jun 2025** (2026-10-23 verified, source: impotsdirects.public.lu « Vente d'un immeuble » + modèle 700F 2025). The temporary housing tax measures were extended to notarial deeds passed 1 Jul → **30 Sep 2025** where the compromis or reservation contract was registered with the AED by 30 Jun 2025 (chd.lu, séance publique 25 Jun 2025; plusvalue.lu and tevaxia.lu, secondary, apply this to the seller-side quarter rate and 2-year threshold too). All expired after 30 Sep 2025
- **CORRECTION**: LU uses a **5-year** speculative threshold (Art. 99bis LIR) — the 2-year threshold was only the H1-2025 derogation. >5yr → half-rate. **No exoneration after any holding period (unlike France's holding-period relief) — only the €50,000 / €100,000 ten-year allowance.**

### Rental income

- **Progressive personal rate** — 0% up to €13,230, 8% from €13,230 up to **42% above €234,870** (class 1 base tariff from TY 2025, still the latest tariff adaptation) + Employment Fund Surcharge (7%; 9% above €150,000 adjusted taxable income in classes 1/1a or €300,000 in class 2) → top marginal 45.78% (computed, 42% × 1.09); rental income also bears the 1.4% dependency-insurance contribution (2026-10-23 verified, source: impotsdirects.public.lu tariff, « Barèmes », « Fonds pour l'emploi », « Assurance dépendance »)
- Standard expenses deductible (mortgage interest — no cap for a let property, per guichet.lu; repairs, management, depreciation). Accelerated depreciation for rental housing (Art. 32ter LIR): **4%** a year while the building's completion dates back less than 5 years at the start of the year, also for renovation spending above 20% of the acquisition or cost price; **6%** kept for buildings acquired or built before 1 Jan 2021 with completion less than 6 years back (2026 data, source: ACD LIR coordinated text in force 1 Jan 2026, updated 08/05/2026)
- **PENDING — 'trois fois 6' depreciation (not enacted)**: announced 16 Jul 2026 — 6% a year for 6 years where the depreciable base is at most €600,000 per building, otherwise 2% a year on the whole base with no time limit; 2026 acquisitions may opt for the current or the new regime; from 1 Jan 2027 the only regime for new acquisitions, earlier regimes running to their end (gouvernement.lu release 16 Jul 2026). Carried by the 2027 budget bill n°8800 per tax-adviser summaries (secondary, e.g. Kleyr Grasso: 6% where the base is ≤ €600,000 and completion < 6 years; the 2% figure is not in those summaries); bill 8800 deposited 7 Oct 2026, in the Commission des Finances, not voted (chd.lu dossier update 26 Oct 2026)

### Mortgage interest deductibility (major 2024 change)

- **Own home (principal residence), from TY 2024**: interest is fully deductible while the home is not yet at the owner's disposal; after that it is **fully deductible for the year the rental value is fixed and the following year**, then capped at **€4,000** for the 2nd following year and the next three years, **€3,000** for the following five years and **€2,000** thereafter (rule from TY 2024, source: ACD newsletter 31 Dec 2024 on the règlement grand-ducal du 20 décembre 2024, Mémorial A n°596; trigger.lu, secondary, describes the same caps for 2025-2026). The caps rise by their own amount for a jointly taxed spouse/partner and for each child (guichet.lu, page last modified 2018 — verify the multiplier against the 2024 règlement)
- Up to TY 2023 the own-home caps were €2,000 / €1,500 / €1,000 a year by occupation period (guichet.lu, rule from TY 2017)
- **Let property**: interest on the loan financing it is deductible **without cap** against the rental income (guichet.lu « Déclarer le revenu d'un immeuble donné en location »)
- Trigger date is when the home is at the owner's disposal (occupied or continuously available), not move-in

### VAT new-build / renovation

- Standard 17% (since 1 Jan 2024)
- **Super-reduced 3%** where the dwelling is used as a principal residence — the owner's own (creation or renovation) or a third party's such as a tenant's (renovation only) — capped at **€50,000 of tax advantage per dwelling** (per distinct cadastral unit; once reached, no further VAT advantage for that dwelling, even for a new owner). No m² cap is stated by the AED; a dwelling also used for other purposes gets the full advantage only if the principal-residence share exceeds ¾ of the surface, otherwise pro rata. Renovation qualifies within 5 years of acquisition or for a dwelling at least 20 years old (2026-10-23 verified, source: AED pfi.public.lu « TVA Logement » pages, last updated Jul/Dec 2024, + VAT FAQ)
- **Direct 3% invoicing needs the AED's prior agrément** (request filed by the contractor, countersigned by the client); without it the works are invoiced at 17% and the owner claims the refund down to 3% afterwards (pfi.public.lu)
- **Clawback** (with statutory interest) if property not used as principal residence for at least 2 years from 1 Jan following completion
- Second home, or creation of a dwelling that is not the owner's principal residence (e.g. a new-build to let): **full 17%**; renovation of a let dwelling that is the tenant's principal residence can qualify at 3% (pfi.public.lu)
- **PENDING — 8% VAT for rental homes 'à vocation sociale' (not enacted)**: announced 16 Jul 2026 for creating rental homes of at most 120 m², priced per m² at or below the Observatoire de l'habitat median for the region and size, rental yield capped at 4% of net invested capital, let for at least 10 years to a tenant certified eligible by the Housing Ministry; applies from the enacting law's entry into force, no retroactive effect (gouvernement.lu release 16 Jul 2026). Legal vehicle UNVERIFIED (no bill identified)
- Source: https://pfi.public.lu/fr/citoyen/tva/logement/application-directe-taux-3.html

## Section: `--rental`

- **Advertised rents** (Observatoire de l'Habitat indicators built from Immotop.lu listings): apartments **+5.8% y/y** in Q2 2026 vs CPI +2.4%, accelerating from +2.0% (Q4 2025) and +4.4% (Q1 2026); houses −3.1% y/y; furnished rooms +4.9% y/y and ~14% of rental listings. ### STR / Airbnb (2023 permit threshold + 2025 guest-form law)

- **Law of 28 February 2025 relative aux fiches d'hébergement** (bill 8253), in force **1 September 2025**, replaced the Law of 24 June 2008 on traveller control. It is a **guest-registration law, not an STR licensing or listing-number scheme** (2025 law, source guichet.public.lu "Fiches d'hébergement", updated 22.01.2026):
  - Covers every operator of paid short-stay tourist accommodation (hotels, other short-stay lodging, campsites), short-let hosts included
  - One **electronic** form per traveller aged 15+ per stay, via the Lux Héberge app, pre-fill links, the MyGuichet.lu professional space or a webservice from booking software
  - Data go to the Tourism Minister's central file within **24 h** of arrival and to STATEC within **5 days**; personal data are deleted at the latest **6 months** after recording; fines **€251–25,000** for not making or not sending the forms (source: Chamber committee text and consolidated dossier of bill 8253)
  - Before first use the operator declares the establishment to STATEC via MyGuichet.lu and receives a unique identification number for the forms; the guichet page does not say it must appear in listings. Whether Luxembourg runs a registration-number procedure under Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 is not verified — check guichet.public.lu
- **Zoning**:
  - Zones d'habitation 1 (HAB-1): parliamentary question n°1145 (5 Sep 2024) describes STR there as limited to **89 nights/yr**; the legal text setting that cap was not located, and Observatoire de l'Habitat Note 46 (Oct 2025) finds most communes' HAB-1 zone texts do not mention STR explicitly — read the commune's PAG written part
  - Some HAB-2 zones list "établissements d'hébergement" among permitted uses (e.g. Ville de Luxembourg, per Note 46); other zones depend on each commune's PAG
- **90 or more nights/yr, cumulated over a year** = commercial accommodation activity under the droit d'établissement reform in force since September 2023: requires an **autorisation d'établissement**, and the accelerated HORECA training must be passed within **6 months** of reaching the threshold (source guichet.public.lu "Exploitant d'un établissement d'hébergement", updated 31.08.2023)
- **Tourist tax**: per-night, commune-set
- **Luxembourg City**: aligned with national rules; commune declaration mandatory

## Section: `--risks`

### Flood

- **`map.geoportail.lu/theme/eau`** (200) — official flood hazard & flood risk maps of the Administration de la gestion de l'eau (AGE), 10-year, 100-year and extreme scenarios. The HQ10 and HQ100 layers are the 2021 edition, published as "RGD cartes des zones inondables" (HQ100 dataset created 2021-07-09, source: geocatalogue.geoportail.lu); raw data on data.public.lu (`hwgk2021-hq10` / `hwgk2021-hq100`, updated 13 Jun 2022)
- **`inondations.public.lu`** (200; `inondations.lu` redirects there, updated 2026-10-23) — AGE flood alerts (Sud, Nord, Moselle) + real-time gauge stations per river
- **Highest-exposure rivers** (AGE flood-risk management plan 2021-2027): **Moselle**, **Sûre** and **Alzette** carry the highest damage potential. Every Moselle commune is strongly affected, Schengen and Remich most; on the Sûre mainly the lower course from Bourscheid (Echternach is protected at HQ10 but exposed at HQ100); on the Alzette exposure rises downstream from Hesperange, and Bettembourg's damage potential is also very high. Confluence hotspots: Mersch (Alzette/Mamer/Eisch) and the Nordstad (Alzette/Sûre/Wark). Potentially affected residents at HQ100, top communes: Diekirch 1,162 · Luxembourg 1,152 · Erpeldange-sur-Sûre 719 · Schengen 678 · Ettelbruck 664 · Bettendorf 655 · Mersch 541 · Echternach 521 · Remich 519. Nationally 12,392 people (2% of residents) at HQ100, and 30 communes have more than 100 (2023 data, source: AGE Hochwasserrisikomanagementplan 2021-2027 — figures may have changed since). These are commune totals: check the parcel itself on the HQ10/HQ100/extreme layers
- **July 2021 flood**: 100-year discharge exceeded at 10 gauging stations — Alzette at Livange, Hesperange, Pfaffenthal, Steinsel and Mersch, Sûre at Rosport, plus the Mamer, Our, Ernz Blanche and Chiers — and record water levels at 15 stations (2021 data, source: AGE press conference, 19 Jul 2021) — rates/figures may have changed since. The second-cycle draft hazard maps went to public consultation on 17 Jun 2019 (source: eau.gouvernement.lu, Directive inondation page)

### Seismic
- Hazard **low**: Luxembourg lies well inside the Eurasian Plate and its territory shows no significant present-day or historical seismicity; most recorded events are quarry blasts, traffic and industrial activity (2025 data, source: ECGS Annual Report 2025)
- ECGS's 2010-2026 catalogue holds 17 natural earthquakes on or within 10 km of the territory, none felt; the largest were ML 2.0 (Mondorf/Schengen, 2019) and ML 1.9 (Hesperange/Alzingen, 2019). Stronger quakes 100-150 km away are felt, e.g. Roermond 1992, M5.4 (2025 data, source: ECGS Annual Report 2025). Monitoring network: LuxSNet, https://www.ecgs.lu/seismic-network/
- PGA ~0.39 m/s² peak (unverified — not stated by ECGS; check a primary seismic hazard model)
- Trier-Luxemburg fault traverses but is not a major active source (unverified — ECGS reports small tectonic events recurring in the same faulted areas without naming this fault)
- Eurocode 8 still applies for new builds — flag for post-2010 construction (unverified — confirm the Luxembourg national annex to EN 1998-1 with the structural engineer)

### Industrial legacy / contaminated land — important

- **South (Minette region)**: 1,200 hectares fell derelict in the south after the steel-industry restructuring, and the government chose to redevelop 650 ha of them (source: Fonds Belval, "la reconversion des friches industrielles", undated page)
- **Esch-Belval**: flagship reconversion of a 120 ha former steelworks site; the state owns 27 ha, on which the Fonds Belval has built ~290,000 m² for a total of ~€1bn (c. 2022 data, source: Agora interview with the Fonds Belval director — figures may have changed since) — University, Rockhal, Cité des Sciences, mixed residential. **Some Belval residential is on remediated/sealed industrial fill** — ask the notaire/seller for any soil study and request the parcel's CASIPO entry (below). There is no point-of-sale soil certificate yet: bill 7237 on soil protection and contaminated-site management, deposited 26 Jan 2018, would add an information duty at transfer and a "certificat de contrôle du sol" (as deposited), but it is still in commission with no vote (source: chd.lu dossier 7237; Administration de l'environnement). The Halle des Soufflantes (1910, 11,200 m²) stands empty and closed, and is on Europa Nostra's 7 Most Endangered list for 2026 (per L'essentiel, 17 Mar 2026 — secondary press)
- **Administration de l'environnement** operates **CASIPO**, the cadastre of former landfills and contaminated sites: ~12,000 sites listed (2018 data, source: Administration de l'environnement — figures may have changed since). An entry means contamination is possible given past or current use, not that it is proven; remediated sites stay listed with a note on the work done (source: environnement.public.lu). Per-commune layer on geoportail (`map.geoportail.lu/communes/<commune>/aev_casipo/`); detailed parcel information on written request to the Administration de l'environnement — surface this as explicit checklist for any Esch / Differdange / Dudelange / Schifflange purchase

### Mandatory diagnostics

- **Passeport énergétique / CPE / Energiepass**: mandatory for sale, rental, new construction, extension, transformation. Sale and rental ads in commercial media must show the energy-performance class and the thermal-insulation class from the valid CPE, so it must exist **before publishing any listing**. Validity 10 years. Classes A+ (best) to I (worst) (source: guichet.lu CPE page, last modified 03.05.2022) — **fines reportedly up to ~€24,789 for advertising without one** (unverified; statutory amount is index-linked — verify current figure in the governing CPE règlement / with the Ministère de l'Économie, Direction générale Énergie). Since 2024, banks weight class in mortgage decisions
- **Radon**: national reference level 300 Bq/m³ for homes and workplaces, set when Directive 2013/59/Euratom was transposed in 2019; it is an action level, not a limit value. The second national radon action plan runs 2021-2028. The 2016 radon zone map uses three classes (<1%, 1-5%, >5% of houses above 300 Bq/m³), and an estimated 5-10% of houses could exceed the reference level (source: santesecu.public.lu radon pages, last modified 23.02.2026). Regional averages ~50 Bq/m³ south, ~150 north, Oesling/Ardennes higher (unverified — not stated on the Ministry of Health pages; use the per-commune map). Per-commune map at https://map.geoportail.lu/communes/Luxembourg/carte_radon_communes/. **Not yet a mandatory transaction-time diagnostic** like FR ERP — advisory only
- **No mandated termite/asbestos certificate** at national level for individual residential transactions (asbestos governed by waste/work-safety regs at renovation time, not point-of-sale)

## Section: `--mains`

### Cadastre + Land Registry — SPLIT

- **ACT (Administration du Cadastre et de la Topographie)** runs the cadastral plan. https://act.public.lu/
- **Geoportail**: https://geoportail.lu/ — citizen-friendly map viewer with cadastral overlay, flood, radon, PAG (commune zoning) layers
- **AED (Administration de l'Enregistrement, des Domaines et de la TVA)** runs **Bureaux des hypothèques** (mortgage offices = transcription/inscription registers, ownership chain, charges). NOT under ACT. Three offices: 1st Luxembourg office (cantons Luxembourg, Mersch, Grevenmacher, Remich), 2nd Luxembourg office (cantons Esch-sur-Alzette, Capellen), Diekirch office (cantons Diekirch, Clervaux, Echternach, Vianden, Redange, Wiltz) (2024 data, source: pfi.public.lu Hypothèques page, updated 12/11/2024) — rates/figures may have changed since. https://pfi.public.lu/fr/citoyen/hypotheques.html
- **Public access to ownership data**: anyone (resident or non-resident) can request a copy of a transcribed act at the competent bureau des hypothèques — the conservateurs must deliver copies of transcribed acts and of subsisting inscriptions, or a certificate that there are none, to anyone who asks (Code civil art. 2196, per pfi.public.lu). Two-step: (1) request "état des inscriptions" against named person, (2) request copies of specific acts. **Paid service**, not free online lookup
- **Direct online register consultation** restricted to professionals (notaries, network operators) on contract; private buyers go through their notaire
- **Buyers cannot self-serve title search** the way they can in UK — instruct the notaire, who pulls the état hypothécaire as part of deed prep

### Utilities

- **Electricity grid**: **Creos Luxembourg** (Encevo group) is the sole transmission operator and the distribution operator on most of the territory; the other three DSOs are Sudstroum, Ville de Diekirch and Ville d'Ettelbruck, plus one industrial network, Sotel (2025 data, source: ILR Chiffres-clés électricité 2025). Sudstroum's network is Esch-sur-Alzette (source: ILR Chiffres-clés électricité 2022). Creos share ~95% of the country (unverified — ILR says only "la majeure partie du territoire")
- **Gas**: Creos is the sole transmission operator and one of three DSOs, with SUDenergie S.A. and Ville de Dudelange; 92,678 delivery points on the distribution network and 26 on the transport network. Supply is open to competition: the Encevo group holds 50.49% of residential gas supply, SUDenergie is second (2025 data, source: ILR Chiffres-clés gaz naturel 2025)
- **SUDenergie S.A.**: gas DSO and electricity/gas supplier; the Esch-sur-Alzette electricity network is run by Sudstroum (source: ILR Chiffres-clés 2022 and 2025)
- **Water**: **intercommunal syndicates + communes** — SEBES (Syndicat des Eaux du Barrage d'Esch-sur-Sûre) treats Upper-Sûre lake water at Esch-sur-Sûre and supplies, over a ~175 km adduction network, the DEA (Distribution d'Eau des Ardennes), the Syndicat des Eaux du Sud, the Syndicat des Eaux du Centre, the eastern-region intercommunal syndicate and the Ville de Luxembourg (source: sebes.lu); communes run local distribution. Rates set commune-by-commune
- **Wastewater**: SIDEN, SIDERO, SIVEC — connection fees can be material on rural/new-build plots
- **District heating**: **LuxEnergie** runs the Kirchberg energy plant that feeds the Ville de Luxembourg heat network there (since 2001; wood-pellet biomass base load since 2017, gas CHP modules replaced by wood boilers in 2023) and has a plant project at Cloche d'Or (ECCO building) (source: luxenergie.lu). **Sudcal S.A.** (State shareholding) serves Belval, Nonnewisen and Sommet in Esch, with base heat recovered from an ArcelorMittal furnace since May 2018 (2026 data, source: Trésorerie de l'État) — relevant for any apartment on those grids (network connection mandatory in some PAP-NQ zones — unverified). Outside these, gas + heat-pump combinations dominant; oil being phased out
- **Internet/fibre**: **POST Luxembourg** incumbent; very-high-capacity network (VHCN) coverage 95.5% and 5G coverage 99.9% (2026 data, source: European Commission Digital Decade 2026 report, per gouvernement.lu release of 17 Jun 2026). FTTH-only coverage not confirmed from a primary source; check the address on the geoportail.lu broadband coverage map

## Active fiscal incentives (2025-2026)

- **Bëllegen Akt €40k credit per person** (permanent since 1 July 2025) on transfer tax for principal residence; raise to €45k for deeds from 16 Jul 2026 pending in bill n°8806 (in committee, not voted)
- **VAT 3% super-reduced** on principal-residence creation/renovation (cap €50k advantage per dwelling; renovation also qualifies where a tenant lives there as principal residence)
- **Own-home mortgage interest fully deductible** in the year the home becomes available and the following year, then €4,000 / €3,000 / €2,000 caps (from TY 2024); let-property interest uncapped
- **PIT scale 2.5 index brackets** added (inflation neutralisation, TY 2025) (Ministry of Finance « Nouveautés 2025 »; still the latest tariff adaptation per ACD « Barèmes »)
- **Pending, not enacted**: VEFA built-share duty exemption (bill n°8807, deeds 16 Jul 2026–16 Jul 2029), 'trois fois 6' rental depreciation (from 1 Jan 2027, budget bill n°8800), 8% VAT on rental homes 'à vocation sociale' (vehicle unverified) — see `--tax`

## Foreign-buyer rules

- **No nationality restrictions whatsoever** — EU and non-EU treated identically for ownership rights
- **No quotas, no preapproval, no foreign-buyer surcharge.** Same 7% transfer tax, same Bëllegen Akt eligibility (subject to occupation conditions: non-residents must undertake to move into the property; third-country residents get the credit only once they produce a Luxembourg residence certificate — guichet.lu)
- **Mortgage practice (lender-imposed, not legal)**:
  - EU non-resident: lenders typically demand 20-30% deposit
  - Non-EU buyer: 30-40% deposit, often plus LU-domiciled income evidence
  - Local mortgage rates trail ECB; tightened sharply 2022-23, easing 2024-25; the ECB then raised its deposit rate from 2.00% (since 11 Jun 2025) to 2.25% (17 Jun 2026) and 2.50% (16 Sep 2026) (2026 data, source ECB key-rates table)
- **Legal LTV caps (CSSF Regulation 20-08, in force 1 Jan 2021)**: 100% for first-time buyers of a primary residence, 90% for other primary-residence buyers (lenders may go up to 100% for 15% of that portfolio), 80% for other loans incl. buy-to-let; applies to any private borrower buying Luxembourg residential property, including through an SCI (2026 data, source CSSF technical FAQ version 9 Mar 2026 + ESRB notification Dec 2020)

## Country-specific quirks

- **Cross-border worker volume**: ~228,000 (~47% of LU workforce). FR ~53.6%, DE ~23.4%, BE ~23% (STATEC frontaliers split — figures may have shifted; verify current quarterly release at STATEC). **Implication**: rental market is LU-resident-only (commuters can't rent here cheaply enough to bother), but sale demand has tail-wind from anyone whose situation flips from commuter to resident. Conversely, rental yields are squeezed because high-income tenants frequently pivot to buying. **Pressure on LU property prices comes from in-country residents and EU/finance new arrivals, not from commuters reverse-pressuring the market**
- **Trilingual notary acts** (clarification): Acts drafted in **one** of LU's official languages, most often French, sometimes German, occasionally Lëtzebuergesch. **NOT literally trilingual on the page.** English is not an official notarial language. Foreign buyers without French/German need an interpreter (€200-900) — strongly advised
- **SCI / SARL ownership**: SCI transparent for tax (partner-level, like France). SARL minimum capital €12,000, fully paid up at incorporation (guichet.lu), week to register, separate corporate income (23.87% combined nominal rate for a company seated in Luxembourg-Ville from TY 2025 = 16% CIT + 7% employment-fund surcharge on that CIT (1.12 points) + 6.75% municipal business tax, per ACD « Charge fiscale globale »; the 2027 budget release announces a CIT cut from 16% to 15%, 14% to 13% for SMEs, not enacted). For rental investment, SARL/SA can win on capital-gains rate vs personal but loses Bëllegen Akt and principal-residence CGT exemption. SCS/SCSp common for institutional. Personal ownership default for principal residence
- **Kirchberg EU-quarter premium**: ~€12,500/m² but tenant pool institutional (EU staff); lease cycles tied to EU staff rotations (3-5yr typical)
- **Echternach / Vianden / Müllerthal**: rural / tourism-coded, lower €/m² (~€4,500-6,000), STR potential constrained by 89-night zone rule. Heritage rules in Vianden castle precincts
- **Esch-sur-Alzette / Belval**: cheaper entry but contamination/remediation history matters; commuter-belt for cross-border French (Thionville/Metz line)
- **PAP / PAG / PAP-NQ zoning**: every commune has Plan d'Aménagement Général (PAG) and project-level Plans d'Aménagement Particulier (PAP). PAP-NQ ("nouveau quartier") above a size threshold must reserve **15%–30%** of the gross floor area for housing to affordable homes, by project size and zoning history (10%–30% until 31 Dec 2025; art. 29bis loi modifiée du 19 juillet 2004 as amended with effect from 1 Jan 2026 — 2026 data, source Chambre des Députés question n°4120, July 2026, + logement.public.lu art. 29bis workshop slides). **Always pull on geoportail.lu PAG layer before committing**
- **Notaire fees on top of taxes**: ~1-1.5% of price, regulated by Grand-Ducal règlement. https://notaryfees.lu/ provides calculator
- **No private mortgages / Notarisation mandatory**: all real-estate sales pass before a notaire — non-notarial private deeds not enforceable against third parties
- **Compromis de vente**: usually signed before notarial act, with 10% deposit standard. Cooling-off period less generous than French "loi SRU" 10-day right
