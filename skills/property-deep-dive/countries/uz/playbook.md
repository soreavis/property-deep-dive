# Uzbekistan (UZ) — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `uz`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05).

## Country profile

- **Population**: ~37.5 million (2025 State Statistics Committee — `stat.uz` / Davlat statistika qoʻmitasi); largest in Central Asia. Tashkent (capital, separate "city of republican significance") ~3.0M; Samarkand ~580k; Namangan ~650k; Andijan ~470k; Bukhara ~290k; Nukus (capital of Karakalpakstan) ~330k; Fergana ~320k. Median age ~28 — youngest population in the post-Soviet space; ~50 % of population under 30.
- **GDP per capita**: ~US$3,000 nominal (2024 IMF / World Bank); real GDP **+6.5 % 2024** + **+7.7 % 2025** (CBU 2025 outcomes statement, [INVEXI summary](https://invexi.org/press/central-bank-of-uzbekistan-2025-outcomes-7-7-economic-growth-with-inflation-easing-to-7-3/)). Largest Central Asian economy by population, second by output (after Kazakhstan).
- **Currency**: **UZS** (Uzbekistan soʻm / so'm / sum / сум). 1 USD ≈ **11,800–12,230 UZS** (CBU official rate, month-start samples Jan–Oct 2026; 12,012.30 on 21 Oct 2026 via [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/)); 1 EUR ≈ 13,200–14,100 UZS. **Dual-rate "black market" gap closed September 2017** under Mirziyoyev liberalisation (Decree UP-5177); on 5 Sep 2017 the official rate was unified with the parallel rate — CBU 4,210.35 → 8,100 UZS/USD (IMF cites 4,250 → 8,100). Since then the regime is de jure floating, IMF de facto crawl-like (IMF 2021 Article IV); 8,100 (Sep 2017) → 12,012 (21 Oct 2026) = **+48 % UZS per USD** (computed; +272 % from the end-2016 official rate of 3,231). CPI inflation **7.3 % in December 2025** and 6.2 % y/y in August 2026 (CBU figures via [Kun.uz 28 Jan 2026](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/01/28/central-bank-keeps-key-rate-unchanged-at-14-amid-persistent-inflation-risks) and [Interfax 16 Sep 2026](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/119147/), secondary; CBU forecast about 6.5 % for end-2026); CBU policy rate **14.0 %** since 24 March 2025 (raised from 13.5 %), held at every 2026 meeting through 16 Sep 2026 (Interfax, citing the CBU press release); the 28 Oct 2026 decision is not confirmed — cbu.uz was unreachable on 2026-10-29, verify at [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz/en/).
- **Languages**: **Uzbek** (state language, oʻzbek tili — Latin script per **2023 Constitution Art. 4** + phased Latin transition originally 1995 deadline, repeatedly deferred; Cyrillic still widely used in legacy documents and older signage); **Russian** (de facto language of business, finance, real-estate listings, government technical documents — no longer constitutionally protected after 1995 but ubiquitous in practice); **Karakalpak** (state language within Republic of Karakalpakstan); English limited to Tashkent CBD + IT Park residents + tourism + diaspora-return circles. **Real-estate listings + cadastre + tax filings predominantly in Russian**, with Uzbek-Latin parallel where state portal compels.
- **EU/regional status**: **No EU candidate status**. EU **Enhanced Partnership and Cooperation Agreement (EPCA)** signed 2018 (in force since 1 July 2025 per Council of the EU), expanding the 1996 PCA. **GSP+ beneficiary** since 10 April 2021 (preferential trade access). **Not in EAEU** (declined membership; observer status since December 2020 — Russia-led customs union). **SCO observer 2001 → full member 2001 → continues**; **Organization of Turkic States** member; **CICA** (Conference on Interaction and Confidence-Building Measures in Asia) member. Multi-vector foreign policy with active reorientation toward "Middle Corridor" Trans-Caspian trade route post-2022.
- **Postcode**: 6 digits (e.g., Tashkent central districts `100000`–`100214`; Samarkand `140100`–`140199`; Bukhara `200100`–`200199`).
- **Admin levels**: **12 regions (viloyatlar — singular viloyat / область in Russian)** + **1 autonomous republic (Republic of Karakalpakstan / Qoraqalpogʻiston Respublikasi)** + **1 independent city of republican significance (Tashkent / Toshkent shahri)** = **14 first-level units**. Subdivided into **175 districts (tumanlar — singular tuman / район)** + city districts (туманлар) + 119 cities + ~1,000 urban-type settlements. The 12 viloyats: Andijan, Bukhara, Fergana, Jizzakh, Namangan, Navoi, Qashqadaryo, Samarkand, Sirdaryo, Surxondaryo, Tashkent (region — separate from Tashkent city), Khorezm. Source: [Subdivisions of Uzbekistan, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Uzbekistan) cross-checked against [stat.uz administrative classification](https://stat.uz/en/).
- **Cadastre**: **Cadastre Agency under the Committee for Urbanization and the Housing Market (Oʻzbekiston Respublikasi Urbanizatsiya va uy-joy bozori qoʻmitasi huzuridagi Kadastr agentligi)** at [kadastr.uz](https://kadastr.uz/en) — established by **Presidential Decree UP-6061 of 7 September 2020** (item 2: formed under the State Tax Committee on the base of the State Committee on Land Resources, Geodesy, Cartography and State Cadastre — GKZGDK; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4985030), 2026-10-21 verified), and **transferred from the Ministry of Economy and Finance into the system of the National Committee for Sustainable Development of Urbanization and the Housing Market by Presidential Decree PF-219 of 19 Nov 2025 (item 12; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-7851209)) and Resolution PQ-347 of 19 Nov 2025 (item 3; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-7850383))** (2025 data, source lex.uz; 2026-10-21 verified); a restructuring of the agency's chamber network was presented on 4 Aug 2026 (president.uz) with no instrument located. Operates the **unified e-Cadastre / e-Kadastr** national digital register of immovable property, land parcels, addresses + cadastral plans. **Modernisation backbone**: World Bank "Modernization of Real Property Registration and Cadastre Project" (P151746, US$20M IDA credit approved 24 May 2016, extended; full nationwide digitisation of cadastral + registration documentation completed 2.5× ahead of original scope per Bank ISR — see [WB project page](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/690831467989526521/uzbekistan-modernization-of-real-property-registration-and-cadastre-project)). Rosreestr (Russia) cooperated on cadastral valuation methodology starting 2021.
- **Identifier**: **Cadastral number (kadastr raqami / кадастровый номер)** identifies each immovable object; format used by Cadastre Agency follows region.district.block.parcel hierarchy (verify exact format per parcel at kadastr.uz). Each apartment, house, commercial unit, land plot has a unique cadastral record cross-referenced to a **technical passport (tekhpasport)** maintained by State Unitary Enterprise "Yer Kadastri" / regional cadastre offices.
- **Primary legal information system**: **Lex.uz (Milliy huquqiy axborot bazasi)** — official national legal database under Ministry of Justice: [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/) — Constitution, Codes (Civil, Land, Tax, Criminal, Family, Labour), Presidential decrees (PF/UP), Cabinet resolutions (PQ/QM), all subordinate acts. Russian + Uzbek (Latin + Cyrillic) versions; partial English. **NormaTiv (Norma)** at [norma.uz](https://www.norma.uz/) is a widely-used commercial legal aggregator for practitioners.
- **Foreign-buyer rule**:
  - **Buildings + structures (apartments, houses, commercial premises)**: foreigners may own the building, not the land (Tax Code Art. 418 counts foreign citizens among owners liable for individuals' property tax). **Cabinet of Ministers Resolution (Vazirlar Mahkamasining qarori) No. 384 of 13 July 2022** (in force 14 July 2022; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/6106940), Uzbek official text) lets citizens of **108 listed countries** (among them the UK, US, Germany, France, Japan, Korea, the UAE, Türkiye, Russia and Kazakhstan) buy **new** real estate, excluding land, **without a residence permit** — "new" means under construction, or commissioned no more than **3 years** earlier (item 3). Minimum prices (item 1): **≥ US$150,000** (contract signed during construction) / **≥ US$180,000** (commissioned object) in **Tashkent region and the cities of Tashkent and Samarkand**; **≥ US$70,000 / US$85,000** in other regions. The purchase gives **no basis for residence registration or a residence permit** (item 1, last paragraph, as worded by Cabinet Resolution No. 224 of 5 May 2026), and payment must go through bank accounts with proof shown to the notary (annex 2: new point 32¹ of the notarial regulation of Cabinet Resolution No. 726 of 2020). Purchases of objects commissioned more than 3 years ago fall outside this resolution — the rule for them is not confirmed in sources read, verify with a notary. ([Kun.uz 16 Jul 2022](https://www.kun.uz/en/news/2022/07/16/government-citizens-of-108-countries-can-buy-real-estate-without-a-residence-permit-in-uzbekistan), secondary; lex.uz verified 2026-10-29.)
  - **Land**: per **Land Code Art. 17 pt 3** (as worded by Law ZRU-871 of 23 Oct 2023), foreign citizens and legal entities, stateless persons and **enterprises with foreign investment** may hold land plots **only on lease (ijara)**; the sole exception (Art. 18 pt 2) is ownership by diplomatic missions and equivalent accredited international organisations. Leases to foreign persons and enterprises with foreign investment run for **up to 25 years**, with a priority right to renew on equal terms (Land Code Art. 24). Land is national wealth under **2023 Constitution Art. 68**, which also allows private land ownership on conditions set by law; ownership arises only on privatisation of non-agricultural plots (Land Code Art. 18 pt 1, as worded by Law ZRU-708 of 16 Aug 2021), a route closed to the foreign persons listed in Art. 17 pt 3 ([lex.uz Land Code](https://lex.uz/docs/152653), 2026-10-29 verified).
  - **Practical implication**: a foreigner buying an apartment **owns the building unit**; the plot under and next to a multi-apartment building is given to the owners for common, non-commercial use on a permanent-use right and is not a land-tax object (Tax Code Art. 434 pt 2). For a standalone house ("hovli") purchase, the foreigner buys the structure and can only lease the plot (Land Code Arts. 17, 24 — up to 25 years); an LLC with ≥ 15 % foreign capital is an enterprise with foreign investment (Investment Law ZRU-598 Art. 3) and is lease-only too, so an LLC vehicle does not deliver land ownership (see Caveats). Source: [Azizov & Partners legal memo](https://azizovpartners.uz/en/memos/acquisition-of-real-estate-by-foreigners-in-uzbekistan/) + [Gratanet land rights brief](https://gratanet.com/publications/land-rights-for-foreign-investors-in-uzbekistan).
  - **Agricultural land**: BANNED for foreigners (citizens-only); also restricted to specific agricultural-purpose entities even for citizens. Permanent prohibition under Land Code.
  - **Border / strategic / military zones**: additional restrictions per Cabinet zoning lists (verify per parcel via cadastre extract).
- **Visa**:
  - **Visa-free 30-day entry** for ~**90 nationalities** (EU/EEA, UK, CA, AU, NZ, JP, KR, IL, Türkiye, GCC, most Latin America + Asia per [Visa policy of Uzbekistan, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_Uzbekistan)). 2019 + 2020 + 2024 expansions; verify per nationality at **MFA Consular Department**: [mfa.uz](https://www.mfa.uz/) or [e-visa.gov.uz](https://e-visa.gov.uz/).
  - **e-Visa** (introduced 15 July 2018): up to 30 days for ~58 additional nationalities; **US$20** single-entry fee (not re-verified: e-visa.gov.uz could not be read on 2026-10-29); since 15 March 2019 also **double-entry (US$35)** and **multiple-entry (US$50)** 30-day e-visas (Presidential Decree UP-5611 of 5 Jan 2019, point 4(b); [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/4143186)); online application via [e-visa.gov.uz](https://e-visa.gov.uz/).
  - **5-year "Golden Visa" (Investor Residence Permit)** — entered into force **1 June 2025** per **Presidential Decree PF-67 (also cited as UP-67) of 18 April 2025** ([Astana Times Apr 2025](https://astanatimes.com/2025/04/uzbekistan-to-offer-five-year-residence-permits-to-foreign-investors-starting-june/) + [Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor 27 Jun 2025](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2025-06-27/uzbekistan-new-program-aims-to-enhance-foreign-investments-in-exchange-for-residence-permits/)). The decree (point 26) calls it a **payment** and names no fund or account; IMI Daily (Apr 2025) calls it a **donation / one-off contribution to a state-designated account**, Fragomen a **one-off payment**; neither source nor the decree says it is non-refundable — **US$250,000** for the main applicant + **US$150,000 per accompanying family member** (spouse/child/parent), paid to a state-designated account — **with NO real-estate, business, or job-creation condition** (INVEXI, quoting the decree text: "The Decree does not stipulate additional conditions, such as investments in the economy, real estate purchases, or job creation"; [IMI Daily Apr 2025](https://www.imidaily.com/central-asia/uzbekistan-introduces-new-5-year-donation-based-golden-visa) + [Fragomen 22 Apr 2025](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/uzbekistan-new-golden-visa-pathway-forthcoming.html)). **Operational status unconfirmed**: per the [Azizov & Partners memo 13 Oct 2025](https://azizovpartners.uz/en/memos/investment-visa-and-residence-permit-in-uzbekistan) the regulatory act for this route had not been adopted and the procedure + document list were not determined; IMI Daily (Apr 2025) reported banks cannot legally accept payments until the implementing resolution is published; Pactum (Tashkent law firm, secondary) found no published payment-procedure act as of 12 Oct 2026, and none was located on lex.uz on 2026-10-21 — data not publicly available, verify at lex.uz / MIA Migration Agency before relying. Point 26 is unchanged in the decree's current redaction of 10 Jun 2026 (Decree UP-108 of 9 Jun 2026 amended point 18 only). (2026-10-21 verified — corrects earlier brief that mislabelled this as a real-estate route and cited "UP-89".)
  - **Separate pre-existing real-estate-for-residence route** (distinct from the PF-67 payment-based Golden Visa): citizens of the **111 countries** in appendix 6 of Presidential Decree **UP-5611 of 5 Jan 2019** may obtain a residence permit on buying real estate worth at least the equivalent of (point 5, para 3 as worded by Decree UP-101 of 8 Apr 2022; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/4143186), 2026-10-29 verified; the same tiers appear in point 32¹(a) of the notarial regulation as amended by Cabinet Resolution No. 384 of 2022):
    - **US$300,000** in **Tashkent city + Tashkent region**
    - **US$200,000** in **Samarkand, Bukhara, Namangan, Andijan, Fergana, or Khorezm regions**
    - **US$100,000** in **Karakalpakstan + all other regions not named above**
  - **3-year investment visa** (separate, pre-existing; a multiple-entry VISA, not a residence permit): investment of at least **8,500 × the base calculation value (BCV) in force at the time of investment** in shares / stakes of Uzbek business companies or in creating an enterprise with foreign investment; extendable without limit and without leaving Uzbekistan ([Presidential Decree UP-5495 of 1 Aug 2018, para. 9, first paragraph as amended by UP-5894 of 13 Dec 2019](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3845276)). BCV = UZS 440,000 since 1 Sep 2026 ([Decree UP-115 of 23 Jun 2026](https://www.lex.uz/uz/docs/8283680)), so the threshold for an investment made now is **UZS 3.74 bn** (computed: 8,500 × 440,000), est. ≈ US$311k at the CBU rate of 12,012.30 UZS/USD on 21 Oct 2026 (computed: 3.74 bn ÷ 12,012.30); it rises with each BCV indexation — recompute at the next one. Founders / participants of enterprises with foreign investment and their families also get a 3-year "investment visa" (no amount stated), and a **10-year residence permit** is issued by simplified procedure for at least **US$3M** invested in enterprises producing goods or providing services ([Decree UP-5635 of 17 Jan 2019, para. 11](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4168757)). (2026 data, lex.uz, verified 2026-10-23 — corrects the earlier "3-year residence permit, ~US$250,000" wording; see shared/regulatory-watch.md.)
  - **IT Visa** — 3-year multi-entry visa for IT specialists, founders, investors of **IT Park residents**. Income threshold US$30,000/year for IT specialists; US$10,000 minimum for investor route. **Allows real-estate purchase at any value without minimum threshold** — IT-visa holders and their spouse, parents and children are exempt from the purchase-price floors and the residence-permit requirement (point 32¹ of the notarial regulation, as amended by Cabinet Resolution No. 384 of 13 Jul 2022; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/6106940)) + reside any region without re-registration (not re-verified) ([IT Park Uzbekistan IT Visa page](https://www.it-park.uz/en/) + [outsource.gov.uz/en/explore-opportunities/it-visa](https://outsource.gov.uz/en/explore-opportunities/it-visa)). **IT Park residency framework**: the IT Park portal lists 0 % corporate, social, property + land tax and 0 % VAT for residents, with **7.5 % PIT** for their employees ([outsource.gov.uz](https://outsource.gov.uz/en/why-uzbekistan/government-support), read 2026-10-29); the residents' exemption and the 7.5 % employee rate run until **1 Jan 2028** (Presidential Decree UP-5099 of 30 Jun 2017, points 5 and 8, as amended by UP-6268 of 24 Jul 2021; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3249654)); from **1 Jan 2028 to 1 Jan 2040** the Tax Code exempts residents from all taxes except VAT only where exports exceed 50 % of total income, or for qualifying IT-education providers (Tax Code Art. 483; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674893)) — major draw for tech founders considering relocation.

### Price trend (2020 → 2025)

- **Tashkent secondary market**: nominal prices roughly **doubled in USD terms 2020 → 2025** (~US$550/m² → ~US$1,082/m²), driven by post-COVID recovery + Russian-relocation wave 2022–2024 + IT Park-driven Tashkent gentrification + persistent inflation. Cumulative ~+96 % nominal USD over 5 years.
- **2025 dynamics**: prices began **cooling** in Tashkent secondary (Stat.uz Q3 2025 print showed mild MoM declines after the spring peak per Gazeta.uz Jul 2025), while regional cities **continued accelerating** — the strongest 2025 H1 growth was **Bukhara +25.6 %** + Sirdaryo **+23.1 %** + Samarkand **+19.2 %** (UzDaily Q4 2025 release). This reflects post-tourism-boom in heritage cities + Tashkent's mid-segment saturation.
- **2025 H1 housing volume +10.6 %** vs H1 2024 (Gazeta.uz); 2025 full-year Tashkent housing sales **+15 %** YoY ([Kun.uz Q3 2025](https://kun.uz/en/51877141)).
- **Russian-relocation impact**: ~100,000–150,000 Russian nationals relocated to Uzbekistan 2022–2024 (estimates vary; Tashkent + Samarkand absorbed the bulk); structural lift to mid-segment Tashkent rents + sale prices, partial unwinding 2024–2025 as cohort either naturalised, moved on, or returned.

### Verdict bands (residential US$/m², Tashkent reference)

- 🟢 within ±10 % of district segment median (per Uybor.uz + Stat.uz, ≥ 5 comparables same district + condition class)
- 🟡 ±20 %
- 🟠 ±30 % — investigate: pre-1991 panel building seismic retrofit status? Cadastral value vs market gap (under-declared transaction)? Land-lease term remaining for the underlying parcel? IT-Visa or Investor-residence-tied minimum threshold ($100k/$200k/$300k)?
- 🔴 > ±30 % — significant red flag

### Compute

1. US$/m² = USD listing ÷ m² advertised. Convert to UZS at CBU rate on contract day if needed.
2. Compare to Stat.uz quarterly print + Uybor.uz district median.
3. **Trap — "общая площадь" (total area, общая) vs "жилая площадь" (living area)**: Soviet/post-Soviet convention typically advertises "общая" including walls + balconies + corridors. 100 m² total ≈ 70–75 m² living. Confirm which figure before per-m² comparison.
4. **Trap — "черновая отделка" (rough finish / shell+core) for new-builds**: Tashkent + regional new-builds frequently sold without internal finishing (no flooring, internal walls in some cases, no plumbing fixtures). Finishing budget: typically **US$200–US$400/m²** (est., Tashkent contractor quotes 2024–2025) to reach habitable; verify per developer schedule.
5. **Trap — under-declared transaction value**: historically common practice to declare a notarial purchase price below true contract value to reduce notary fee + future CGT base; tightening 2024–2026 with cadastral value floors (Tax Code Art. 412 per-m² minimum value — Tashkent 3.53M UZS/m² from 1 Jan 2026; it applies to legal-entity property and individuals' non-residential business premises, not to flats). For foreign buyers, **always declare full true value** — under-declaration creates AML + future-sale CGT exposure.
6. **Trap — parking**: rarely included in Tashkent new-build prices; expect US$8,000–US$25,000 for an underground space in central Tashkent (est., developer schedules 2024–2025 — verify per development).
7. **Trap — real-estate-for-residence threshold (NOT the PF-67 payment-based Golden Visa)**: the **PF-67 5-year "Golden Visa" is a payment route — decree point 26 says "payments", names no fund and does not call it a donation (IMI Daily does)** (US$250k + US$150k/family member, **no property condition**) — buying property does **not** earn it. The property-purchase residence pathway is a **separate pre-existing real-estate-for-residence route** (Tashkent US$300k, Samarkand/Bukhara US$200k, other regions US$100k): the contract value must be **at or above** the relevant threshold AND the property must be of a qualifying type — per Azizov & Partners, **secondary-market residential and non-residential property do not qualify** (verify with the Migration Service before signing). Listings that under-quote the cadastral floor or include unfinished status may not qualify. No implementing act for the PF-67 payment route had been adopted as of October 2025 (Azizov & Partners) and none was published as of 12 Oct 2026 (Pactum, secondary; not located on lex.uz 2026-10-21) — that pathway may not be operational; verify before relying.
8. **Trap — "investment under construction" purchase (preliminary contract / dolevoye uchastie)**: pre-completion purchases were liberalised for foreigners by 2022 reforms ([Tashkent Times Apr 2022](https://tashkenttimes.uz/national/8728-foreigners-to-be-able-to-invest-in-uzbekistan-real-estate-under-construction-to-pay-less-income-tax)); verify developer's escrow account compliance + state registration of the preliminary contract before any pre-payment.

## Section: `--tax`

### Currency note

UZS, USD/UZS ≈ 11,800–12,230 (CBU official rate, month-start samples Jan–Oct 2026; 12,012.30 on 21 Oct 2026). All annual property taxes payable in UZS to local Tax Committee account; conversions in playbook use **12,500 UZS/USD** as a rounding figure — above the 2026 CBU range, so USD amounts converted at 12,500 read ~4 % low at 21 Oct 2026 (12,500 / 12,012); verify against current CBU rate at [cbu.uz/en/](https://cbu.uz/en/).

### Tax-code regime context

- **Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan** (Code No. ZRU-599 of 30 December 2019, in force 1 January 2020 as amended through 2025–2026) — comprehensive consolidated code replacing the 2007 Tax Code. Amended annually via the **State Budget Law** + interim **Presidential Decrees** (PF/UP) + **Cabinet Resolutions** (PQ/QM).
- **2019 PIT reform**: from **1 January 2019** a single **12 % PIT rate** replaced the prior progressive scale (Presidential Decree UP-5468 of 29 Jun 2018, point 2(a); [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3802374); the old 7.5 %/16.5 %/22.5 % bands are not re-verified). The current Tax Code keeps 12 % on residents' income (employment, self-employment, rental, capital gains) and 5 % on dividends and interest (Tax Code Art. 381; [PwC Uzbekistan Individual taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income), reviewed 27 Aug 2026).
- **2025 changes**: **property tax indexed +10 %**; per-m² minimum value raised (Tax Code Art. 412 — legal-entity property-tax base, also applied to individuals' non-residential business premises via Art. 420: Tashkent 3.3M UZS/m², Nukus + regional centres 2.2M, other 1.3M; Law ZRU-1014 of 24 Dec 2024) — see [Kun.uz tax-rise summary](https://kun.uz/en/84676729) + [EY Uzbekistan tax updates 2025](https://www.ey.com/en_uz/technical/tax-alerts/2025/01/changes-in-taxation-in-uzbekistan).
- **2026 changes** (effective 1 January 2026; [EY Uzbekistan tax updates 2026](https://www.ey.com/en_uz/technical/tax-alerts/2026/01/uzbekistan-tax-updates-2026) + [Kun.uz Deputy PM Kuchkarov 22 Dec 2025](https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/12/22/deputy-pm-kuchkarov-details-which-taxes-will-rise-and-which-will-stay-unchanged-in-2026)):
  - **VAT**: rate **unchanged at 12 %** (no rise in 2026; AI-driven real-time invoice analysis system launched 1 Jan 2026 — high-risk invoice input VAT recoverable only after supplier remits to budget)
  - **Corporate income tax**: base rate **unchanged at 15 %**
  - **Corporate property tax**: base rate **unchanged at 1.5 %**
  - **Law ZRU-1108 of 25 Dec 2025 (in force 1 Jan 2026; lex.uz)**: individuals' residential property-tax rates 0.34 / 0.45 / 0.6 % → 0.36 / 0.48 / 0.64 % (business-use 1.5 % unchanged); per-m² minimum value (Art. 412) Tashkent 3.3M → 3.53M, Nukus + regional centres 2.2M → 2.35M, other 1.3M → 1.39M UZS; legal-entity reduced rate 0.65 % → 0.7 %; land-tax base rates (Arts. 429 + 437) and all five water-use rate lines (Art. 445) ~+7 %
  - **PIT**: flat **12 %** unchanged

### Annual property tax — Property Tax of Individuals (`Jismoniy shaxslarning mol-mulk solig'i` / Налог на имущество физических лиц)

Governed by Tax Code Section XV, Chapter 60, Articles 418–423 (verify in [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/) Tax Code).

**Rate schedule (Tax Code Art. 422 pt 1; 2025 values per Law ZRU-1014 → from 1 Jan 2026 per Law ZRU-1108)**:

| Property type & area | Rate (% of cadastral value) |
|---|---:|
| Houses + apartments + dachas **up to 200 m²** (urban + rural), parking spaces + other structures | 0.34 % (2025) → **0.36 %** (2026) |
| Urban properties **200–500 m²**; rural **>200 m²** | 0.45 % (2025) → **0.48 %** (2026) |
| Urban properties **>500 m²** | 0.6 % (2025) → **0.64 %** (2026) |
| Premises used for entrepreneurial activity / rented to a legal entity or sole trader + non-residential income property (individuals) | **1.5 %** (2025 and 2026) |

Source: Tax Code Art. 422 ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674893), current + 1 Jan 2025 redactions; 2026-10-21 verified); [Kun.uz tax brief Dec 2024](https://kun.uz/en/84676729); [tax-legal.uz VAT page](https://tax-legal.uz/en/value-added-tax/).

**Tax-base floors for individuals (Tax Code Art. 420)**: houses / flats / dachas, parking spaces + other structures — cadastral value not below **UZS 42,000,000 per object** (unchanged 2025 → 2026); with no valuation, the conditional value is 5 × that sum in Tashkent, Nukus + regional centres and 2 × elsewhere. The per-m² minimum below (Art. 412) applies to individuals only for **non-residential premises used for business / income**:

| Locality | Floor (UZS/m²) | Approx USD/m² @ 12,500 |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Tashkent city** | 3,300,000 (2025) → **3,530,000** (2026) | ~$282 |
| Nukus + regional centres | 2,200,000 (2025) → **2,350,000** (2026) | ~$188 |
| Other towns + rural areas | 1,300,000 (2025) → **1,390,000** (2026) | ~$111 |

Source: Tax Code Arts. 412 + 420 on [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674893) (2026 values per Law ZRU-1108 of 25 Dec 2025, in force 1 Jan 2026; 2026-10-21 verified).

### Example calculation (Tashkent mid-segment apartment)

A Uybor.uz listing: Tashkent Yashnabad 70 m² 2-bedroom apartment, 2018 build, asking price US$95,000 (~UZS 1,187,500,000):
- Tax base: the cadastral value set by the registration authority (Tax Code Art. 420), not below **UZS 42,000,000 per residential object** — the per-m² Art. 412 minimum does not apply to a flat. Illustration only: a cadastral value of **UZS 231,000,000 (~US$18,500)**
- Property tax band: ≤200 m² → 0.36 % (2026) → **UZS 831,600/year (~US$67/year)** on that value (computed: 231,000,000 × 0.0036; 2025 rate 0.34 % → UZS 785,400); at the UZS 42M floor → UZS 151,200/year (computed)
- Karakalpakstan, regional and Tashkent city councils may apply 0.7–1.3 coefficients to these rates (Tax Code Art. 422) — check the coefficient for the parcel's locality
- **Annual property tax in UZ is materially low** (typically US$50–US$200/year for an Tashkent mid-segment flat) — among the lowest in the broader Central Asia / CIS region.

> Effective property-tax rate on **market value** (not cadastral) is typically **0.05–0.15 %** for Tashkent residential — even after the 2026 rate reset (0.34 → 0.36 % for flats up to 200 m², Law ZRU-1108), the tax burden remains negligible by global standards. Verify per parcel with the **Tax Committee** at [gov.uz/en/soliq](https://gov.uz/en/soliq) or via your taxpayer cabinet (`my.soliq.uz`).

### Land tax (Yer solig'i / Земельный налог)

- For **individual landowners** (Uzbek citizens only — foreigners cannot own land): per Tax Code Chapter 62, Art. 433–440 (base rates Art. 437: Tashkent zone 1 1,735 → 1,856 UZS/m² from 1 Jan 2026, Law ZRU-1108); rates differentiated by region + zone + use class; expressed in UZS/m²/year × zoning coefficient
- For **leased land**: rent paid under a registered lease from a district (city) khokim or a state body is **equated to land tax**, and individual lessees follow the individuals' land-tax rates and rules (Tax Code Art. 433 pt 2). **Flat owners pay no land tax**: plots under and next to multi-apartment buildings given to the owners for common, non-commercial use on a permanent-use right are not a land-tax object, except plots used for business and non-residential premises in such buildings (Tax Code Art. 434 pt 2, from 1 Jan 2024)
- Tashkent zone 1 (1,856 UZS/m² from 2026) therefore matters for house plots and business premises, not for residential flats (Tax Code Arts. 434, 437)

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase)

- **Property transfer tax**: **none** — the Tax Code's list of taxes (Art. 17) has no transfer tax. The **state duty (Davlat boji / госпошлина)** on the notarised contract is a fixed sum, not a percentage, except in one foreign-buyer case — see Notary fee below (Law ZRU-600 of 6 Jan 2020)
- **VAT (QQS / НДС)**:
  - **Resale between individuals (secondary market)**: **EXEMPT** (out of VAT scope — individual non-entrepreneur sales)
  - **First sale by VAT-registered developer (primary market)**: the Tax Code's VAT-exempt list (Art. 243) has **no exemption for selling residential property**, and Art. 244 covers financial services only — so a VAT-payer developer's sale falls under the standard **12 %** rate (Art. 258) unless a special act applies; ask the developer whether VAT is in the price (2026 text, lex.uz)
  - Verify exact treatment at [tax-legal.uz/en/value-added-tax](https://tax-legal.uz/en/value-added-tax/) + 2026 Tax Code consolidated text on [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/)
- **Notary fee — state duty (Davlat boji / госпошлина)**: **mandatory notarisation** of the sale contract; the State Duty Law (Law ZRU-600 of 6 Jan 2020, rate annex item 4; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4680955)) sets a **fixed duty of 1 BCV = UZS 440,000** (est. ≈ US$37) for property in Tashkent, Nukus and regional centres and **0.5 BCV = UZS 220,000** (est. ≈ US$18) elsewhere (computed at BCV UZS 440,000 from 1 Sep 2026, Decree UP-115, and CBU 12,012.30 UZS/USD on 21 Oct 2026); half rate for transfers to close relatives. **Exception**: a foreign citizen holding an Uzbek residence permit who buys new-build property worth ≥ 3,300 BCV (UZS 1.452 bn, est. ≈ US$121k) in Tashkent city or Tashkent region pays **10 % of the contract value**, and reselling it within 3 years costs 400 BCV (UZS 176 m) (item 4, sub-items б and в). Any notary's own service charge is not verified — ask the notary
- **State registration fee at Cadastre Agency**: **data not publicly available — verify at the [Cadastre Agency](https://kadastr.uz/en)**; the English portal surfaces the registration services but publishes no tariff table (2026-09-18 verified). Expedited processing is offered at a higher fee.
- **Real-estate agent (rieltor)**: typically **2–4 %** paid by seller; foreign buyers occasionally asked to pay buyer-side commission — negotiate
- **Total transaction cost (buyer side, individual–individual resale)**: est. **under 0.1 %** of price before agent commission on a US$95,000 Tashkent flat — notarial duty UZS 440,000 ≈ 0.04 % (computed: 440,000 ÷ (95,000 × 12,012.30)) plus a cadastre registration fee not verified this pass; the 10 % duty case above is the exception
- **AML / cash-payment limit**: per the Currency Regulation Law (restated by Law ZRU-573 of 22 Oct 2019) + Cabinet decrees, real-estate purchases over thresholds (verify current cap at CBU) require **bank-transfer settlement**; cash payments above limits trigger AML reporting via Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office. **Foreign buyers using the purchase routes without a residence permit (UP-5611 appendix 6 tiers or the 108-country new-build list) must settle through bank accounts and show the payment document to the notary** (point 32¹ of the notarial regulation, as amended by Cabinet Resolution No. 384 of 13 Jul 2022; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/6106940)).

### Capital gains tax (CGT) on sale

- **Resident individual** sale of a home: **exempt if owned for at least 36 calendar months** — Tax Code Art. 378 item 7 exempts income from selling privately owned property except securities / stakes, **non-residential premises** and **residential premises held under 36 months**. Otherwise **12 %** (Art. 381) on the excess of the sale price over the documented acquisition cost; without documents, on the positive difference between sale price and cadastral value (Art. 375 pt 4)
- **Non-resident individual** sale of UZ-located residential: **12 %** on UZ-source 'other income' (Tax Code Art. 382 pt 3, as worded by Law ZRU-785 of 26 Jul 2022, applying from 1 May 2022 — the 20 % shown here before is superseded). Whether the 36-month exemption in Art. 378 item 7 also covers non-residents is not stated there — verify with a tax adviser
- **Resident landlord** rental income: **12 % flat** PIT (subject to filing per below)
- **Non-resident landlord**: **12 %** on UZ-source rental income (Tax Code Art. 382 pt 3; reduced from 20 % from 1 May 2022 by Law ZRU-785) — withheld by the tenant where the tenant is a tax agent paying the income (Tax Code Arts. 385–388), otherwise declared by the landlord

### Personal income tax — general (Tax Code Art. 381)

| Category | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Resident individual (worldwide income)** | **12 % flat**; 5 % on dividends + interest (Art. 381) |
| Non-resident individual (UZ-source income) | **12 %** on employment and other income (Art. 382 pt 3, since 1 May 2022; was 20 %); 10 % dividends + interest |
| Capital gains — resident | **12 %**; homes held ≥ 36 months exempt (Art. 378 item 7) |
| Capital gains — non-resident on UZ real-estate sale | **12 %** (Art. 382 pt 3; was 20 % before 1 May 2022) |
| Individual entrepreneur (IE) / self-employed — turnover tax | **1 %** of turnover, income up to UZS 1 bn a year, from 1 Jan 2026 (Tax Code Arts. 461, 467 row 5, Law ZRU-1108); an IE's **rental income is taxed at 12 % PIT** instead (Art. 465 pt 7) |

- **Tax residency**: **more than 183 days** in Uzbekistan in any consecutive 12-month period starting or ending in the tax year (Tax Code Art. 30)
- **Tax filing**: annual declaration for income not taxed at source (rental, foreign income etc.) by **1 April of year + 1**, payment by **1 June of year + 1**; **rent not taxed at source is paid monthly by the 15th of the following month** on a preliminary declaration filed within 5 days after the first month of rent — none needed if the lease is registered with the tax authority (Tax Code Arts. 397–398); via [my.soliq.uz](https://my.soliq.uz/) taxpayer cabinet
- **TIN (STIR — Soliq toʻlovchining identifikatsiya raqami)**: 9-digit individual taxpayer identification number; **prerequisite for property purchase, banking, IE registration**; foreigners obtain at any Tax Committee branch with passport

### AML / cash limits + currency-payment rules

- **Currency Regulation Law No. ZRU-573 of 22 October 2019** (in force 23 Oct 2019, amendments 2021–2025): Currency Liberalisation Law — replaces the pre-2019 strict capital-control regime. All settlements and payments for goods (works, services) in Uzbekistan are made in **UZS** unless the law says otherwise, prices are set only in UZS and may not be pegged to foreign currencies or 'conventional units' (Art. 9; para 3 as worded by Law ZRU-669 of 22 Jan 2021); currency operations between residents are allowed only in the cases listed in Art. 17 ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4562846)). Non-residents may price contracts in foreign currency but settlement of UZ-real-estate transactions occurs in UZS at CBU reference rate on contract day.
- **Cash-payment limit**: per Cash Discipline rules, large cash payments are restricted; for individuals buying real estate, **bank transfer is the standard**, especially for high-value transactions due to AML scrutiny under the **Department for Combating Economic Crimes (DBEK) under the Prosecutor General's Office**.
- **AML supervisor**: **Department for Combating Economic Crimes (PGO)** + **CBU AML/CFT division** + **Financial Monitoring Department (Ministry of Economy and Finance)** (bodies not re-verified this pass). Notaries — when preparing or carrying out transactions for clients — and persons taking part in real-estate sale transactions are reporting entities under the AML Law (**Law No. 660-II of 26 Aug 2004**, Art. 12; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/284542)).
- **Capital movement**: free transfer of foreign-currency funds in and out, including proceeds from selling foreign investments, is guaranteed once taxes and payments are settled (Law ZRU-598 of 25 Dec 2019 on investments and investment activity, Art. 17; [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4664144)) — subject to tax clearance + AML review at CBU.

### Future risk

- **Property-tax modernisation**: ongoing transition from area-based + cadastral-floor system toward genuine market-aligned cadastral valuation (Rosreestr-supported pilots since 2021, [WB ISR](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/963031624984183169/txt/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Modernization-of-Real-Property-Registration-and-Cadastre-P151746-Sequence-No-13.txt)) — when complete, effective property-tax burden may rise materially; monitor Tax Committee announcements
- **VAT real-time invoice AI system**: launched 1 Jan 2026; high-risk invoice flagging may delay input VAT recovery — affects developers + commercial RE more than individual residential buyers
- **Currency-control evolution**: 2019 liberalisation continues to be refined; 2024 + 2025 amendments tightened reporting on outward transfers >US$10k (not verified in a primary source this pass); monitor [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz/) bulletins
- **Real-estate-for-residence threshold** (separate pre-existing route, distinct from the PF-67 payment-based Golden Visa): $100k/$200k/$300k by region; thresholds may be revised in 2026–2028 budget cycles
- **Per-m² minimum value** (Tax Code Art. 412 — legal-entity property base + individuals' non-residential business premises): Tashkent 3.3M UZS/m² (2025) → 3.53M UZS/m² (2026, Law ZRU-1108); long-run trajectory aligns toward ~50–70 % of market price as Rosreestr methodology matures

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

**Tax options for individual landlord** (foreigner with valid 5-year residence permit / IT Visa / company-tied work permit + STIR):

1. **PIT 12 % flat without IE registration** — simplest path:
   - **12 %** on rental income for residents (Tax Code Art. 381) and non-residents (Art. 382 pt 3); withheld by the tenant only where the tenant is a tax agent, otherwise paid by the landlord
   - Rent not taxed at source: **monthly payment by the 15th** of the following month on a preliminary declaration (not needed if the lease is registered with the tax authority), then the annual declaration by **1 April year + 1** with any balance settled by **1 June** (Tax Code Arts. 397–398)
   - Example: Tashkent 2-bed flat at UZS 5,500,000/month (~US$440) → UZS 66M annual income → **UZS 7.92M PIT (~US$634/year)**

2. **Individual Entrepreneur (IE / Yakka tartibdagi tadbirkor / ИП) — no longer a cheaper route for rent**:
   - From **1 Jan 2026** an IE's or self-employed person's **rental income is taxed at the 12 % PIT rate**, not turnover tax (Tax Code Art. 465 pt 7, Law ZRU-1108); the 1 % IE turnover tax (Art. 467 row 5, income up to UZS 1 bn) covers sales of other goods and services
   - Register at any Tax Committee branch in 1–3 days; ~free
   - Filing via [my.soliq.uz](https://my.soliq.uz/) monthly/quarterly
   - No tax advantage over option 1 for plain letting since 2026; whether short-term guest stays count as rent or as an accommodation service (turnover tax) is not settled in sources read

3. **LLC ("MChJ" — Mas'uliyati cheklangan jamiyat / ООО)** — for portfolio investors:
   - 15 % CIT on profit + 5 %/10 % dividend WHT
   - Foreign founders unrestricted; useful for multi-unit + STR-management portfolio
   - Property held in LLC name avoids personal-name foreign-buyer thresholds + simplifies AML (practitioner claim, not verified against a primary source; an LLC with ≥ 15 % foreign capital is an enterprise with foreign investment — Investment Law ZRU-598 Art. 3)

### Short-term rentals (STR / Airbnb / Booking)

**Status (2025–2026)**: largely treated as ordinary rental for tax purposes; **emerging municipal-level regulation**; no national STR-specific licence regime located in sources read on 2026-10-29. The Law on Tourism (ZRU-549 of 18 Jul 2019) makes certification mandatory for accommodation facilities (Art. 21), a category that includes guest houses (Art. 15); whether a privately let flat counts is not settled in sources read. Some discussion of restrictions in Tashkent / Samarkand / Bukhara — verify current city-level rules before listing.

#### Tax classification

- **Individual operating informally**: 12 % PIT on net rental + risk of being reclassified as "entrepreneurial activity" → triggers IE registration + retroactive penalties
- **IE turnover regime**: from 1 Jan 2026 an IE's rental income is taxed at 12 % PIT (Tax Code Art. 465 pt 7); the 1 % turnover tax (Art. 467) would apply only if the stay is treated as an accommodation service rather than rent — not settled in sources read
- **VAT registration**: required if turnover crosses VAT threshold (verify at [gov.uz/en/soliq](https://gov.uz/en/soliq) — UZS 1 bn of income a year, Tax Code Art. 462 as worded by Law ZRU-1108; ≈ US$83k at CBU 12,012.30, computed)
- **Tourist tax / accommodation fee**: the Law on Tourism (ZRU-549, Art. 12) names a **tourist levy (туристский сбор)** as a revenue source of the off-budget Tourism Support Fund — but its rate, who sets it and whether private STR hosts must collect it were not located this pass; verify with the Tourism Committee / khokimiyat before listing

#### Practical (2025–2026)

- **Building/HOA approval**: newer Tashkent + Samarkand developments increasingly include STR restrictions in bylaws — verify before buying
- **Tashkent Mirabad / Yakkasaray / Yashnabad**: highest year-round STR yields driven by business + diplomatic + diaspora-return traffic
- **Samarkand + Bukhara + Khiva**: highest tourism-season yields (Mar–Nov peak); Dec–Feb thin demand outside heritage festivals
- **Trap — pre-1991 Soviet panel buildings ("xrushchovka" + "brezhnevka")**: entrance security + concierge incompatible with self-check-in; common-area-dues board may reject STR; smart-lock workaround possible but neighbour friction common

### Seismicity — material concern, especially Tashkent + Fergana Valley

- Uzbekistan sits on the **southern Tian Shan + Pamir collision zone** with active deformation. Seismicity is **moderate-to-high in the south + east** (Tashkent, Fergana Valley, Surxondaryo, Samarkand) and lower in the western desert + Karakalpakstan.
- **1966 Tashkent earthquake (26 April 1966, Mw 5.2, depth 3–8 km, epicentre central Tashkent)**: foundational event in Uzbek seismic awareness. Despite moderate magnitude, the **shallow focal depth + dense unreinforced adobe + masonry housing stock** caused **massive destruction** — most pre-1966 buildings were damaged or destroyed, 200,000–300,000 left homeless, 15–200 deaths (low casualty count attributed to early-morning timing + low collapse-fatality of single-storey adobe). Reconstruction was a Soviet all-Union effort 1966–1976; modern Tashkent's wide boulevards + concrete-panel housing dominate as legacy. Source: [1966 Tashkent earthquake, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966_Tashkent_earthquake) + [ReliefWeb 50-year retrospective](https://reliefweb.int/report/uzbekistan/1966-2016-50-years-tashkent-earthquake) + [seismic-risk assessment Nature Sci Rep 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-44585-3).
- **Current seismic risk** ([Nature Sci Rep 2026 paper on Tashkent's residential buildings, 1966 scenario]): approximately **4.0 % of Tashkent housing stock** — primarily brick + adobe buildings constructed before 1966 — may experience severe damage under a **Mw 5.5 earthquake scenario**. Post-1966 Soviet panel + post-2000 frame construction performs significantly better; **independent structural survey strongly advised** for any pre-1966 structure (rare in central Tashkent post-reconstruction but present in old mahalla quarters of Samarkand + Bukhara).
- **Fergana Valley** (Andijan, Fergana, Namangan): high regional seismicity; **1902 Andijan Mw 6.4** earthquake (~4,500 deaths); densely populated with mixed-age housing stock. Buyers should request seismic-zoning certification from regional khokimiyat / Institute of Seismology before deposit.
- **Building code**: **KMK 2.01.03-19 "Construction in seismic regions"** + earlier KMK seismic codes — partial Eurocode 8 alignment ongoing. Seismic-resistance class is required disclosure on building registration certificates per Ministry of Construction order — verify on technical passport.
- **Soviet panel-block stock** (5-storey + 9-storey + 16-storey panels constructed 1970s–1980s): retrofit status uneven; documented vulnerability of poorly-detailed joints in some series. Independent structural survey advised for pre-1991 panel buildings.

### Flood + drought + water-scarcity (climate-driven)

- **Major rivers**: Syr Darya (Fergana Valley → Aral basin), Amu Darya (south + west → Aral basin), Zarafshan (Samarkand–Bukhara), Surxondaryo, Chirchiq (Tashkent region) — all transboundary.
- **Drought + water-stress** is the dominant hydrological hazard, not flood — Uzbekistan has been dramatically affected by the **Aral Sea crisis** (sea volume reduced ~90 % since 1960; eastern Aral basin is now the **Aralqum desert**, ~60,000 km²), and central Asia faces accelerating **water-deficit risk by 2050** ([UNEP Atlas + ADB Climate Adaptive Water Resources Management report](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/linked-documents/53120-001-ssa.pdf)): water supply forecast to drop **57 → 52–54 billion m³** while demand rises **59 → 62–63 billion m³** — water deficit growing from ~2 billion m³ to **11–13 billion m³** by 2050.
- **Glacier loss in Pamir + Tian Shan headwaters**: severe by 2050 — projected **1.75–2.25 °C** mean temperature increase; reduced reservoir inflows; **GLOF (glacial-lake outburst flood) risk** elevated in transition phase.
- **Spring snowmelt + flash-flood events**: increasing intensity per UNEP + Uzhydromet projections; precipitation more extreme but less frequent. Tashkent + Samarkand + Fergana cities historically buffered by reservoir + canal systems but suburban gully-floods + mahalla drainage failures during cloudburst events documented 2020–2025.
- **2025–2026 drought watch**: ongoing reduced precipitation in Surxondaryo + Karakalpakstan; agricultural-water stress affecting rural property utility supply.

### Aral Sea region (Karakalpakstan) — chronic environmental hazard

- **Karakalpakstan + parts of Khorezm**: among the world's most degraded environmental zones — desiccated Aral Sea bed releases **toxic dust** (salts, residual pesticides incl. DDT/HCH from Soviet cotton agriculture, heavy metals); chronic respiratory + cancer + reproductive health impact documented over decades by WHO + UNDP.
- **Property-investment relevance**: Nukus + nearby cities have low absolute property prices (~US$200–US$400/m²) but **acute environmental + health risk** for long-term residence; tourism focus on Aral coast + Mizdakhan necropolis is niche.
- Source: [UNEP press release on Climate Change Atlas Uzbekistan](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/climate-change-drives-rising-temperatures-and-severe-drought) + [Aral Sea Resilience Portal, UNESCAP](https://rrp.unescap.org/aral-sea).

### Air quality — Tashkent winter pollution (chronic concern)

- Tashkent ranks among Central Asia's **most-polluted capitals** in winter (Nov–Mar): coal + gas-fired CHPP emissions, vehicle fleet (~700k cars), residential coal heating in suburban "private sector" (chastny sektor / xususiy sektor), seasonal inversion in the Tashkent basin.
- Winter PM2.5 spikes routinely **>100–250 μg/m³** (vs WHO 24-hr guideline 15 μg/m³); 2024 + 2025 episodes triggered school-closure advisories.
- Property buyers in Tashkent should factor air filtration (HEPA + activated carbon) + double-glazing into renovation budget; suburban + foothill (Bostanlik) cleaner than valley floor.

### Climate change projections

- **Mean annual temperature**: **+1.75–2.25 °C** by 2050 (UNEP Atlas + ADB); summer warming faster (+2 to +2.75 °C) than winter (+1.5 to +2 °C)
- **Precipitation**: shifts to more intense but less frequent events; **+5 to +20 % annual mean** by 2050 but with strong seasonal + regional variation — winter precipitation up 5–25 % across Aral basin; summer slightly down in south, up in north
- **Glacier loss in Tian Shan + Pamir**: severe by 2050; reduced reservoir inflows; long-run water-deficit growing 2 → 11–13 billion m³ by 2050 (UNEP / ADB)
- **Heat**: Tashkent summer days >40 °C projected to **double** by 2050; agricultural-yield + rural-water stress
- **Aral basin desertification**: continuing; salt + dust storms increasing intensity; Karakalpakstan + Khorezm + Bukhara north periphery affected
- **NDC**: Uzbekistan **Updated NDC 2021** targeted 35 % reduction in GHG emissions per unit GDP by 2030 (vs 2010 baseline); Carbon Neutrality strategy 2050 under preparation; National Climate Adaptation Strategy under development

## Section: `--currency` (UZ-specific extension)

Uzbekistan has a **managed-float UZS** since the **2 September 2017 currency liberalisation** (Presidential Decree UP-5177 of 2 Sep 2017) which led to the 5 Sep 2017 unification of the official and parallel rates (CBU official 4,210.35 → 8,100 UZS/USD). Key dynamics:

- **CBU policy rate**: **14.0 % since 24 March 2025** (raised from 13.5 %), held at every 2026 meeting through 16 Sep 2026 ([Interfax 16 Sep 2026](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/119147/), citing the CBU press release; secondary); the 28 Oct 2026 decision is not confirmed (cbu.uz unreachable 2026-10-29) — among the highest in the broader region; persistent high-rate stance to anchor inflation expectations
- **Inflation**: **7.3 % in December 2025** vs target 5 %; **6.2 % y/y in August 2026**; CBU forecast about **6.5 % end-2026** (CBU via Kun.uz 28 Jan 2026 and Interfax 16 Sep 2026, secondary); **disinflation trajectory underway**
- **USDUZS trajectory 2017–2026**: 8,100 (5 Sep 2017 post-liberalisation, CBU) → 12,012.30 (21 Oct 2026, CBU) — **+48 % UZS per USD** (computed); 2026 month-start samples 11,801–12,225
- **Exchange-rate exposure during purchase**: contracts denominated in USD between non-resident parties are common, but **settlement of UZ-real-estate transactions must occur in UZS** at CBU reference rate on transaction day — payments for goods and services in Uzbekistan are made in UZS and prices may not be pegged to foreign currency (Currency Regulation Law Art. 9, restated by Law ZRU-573 of 22 Oct 2019)
- **Capital controls**: relatively liberal post-2019 vs pre-2017 strict regime; banks must report large transfers; AML/CFT scrutiny applies; outward transfers >US$10k flagged (not verified in a primary source this pass); investor-protection guarantee under Investment Law LRU-598 enables repatriation of sale proceeds (subject to tax clearance)
- **Soʻm outlook**: CBU statement Dec 2025 cites **stronger-than-expected appreciation** in H2 2025 dampening import inflation; 2026 trajectory: modest depreciation of ~3–5 % (unsourced est.; not in the CBU statements read) absent external shocks
- **Practical buyer hedge**: keep purchase reserve in USD until 5–10 days before notarial signing; convert at contract-day CBU rate; consider holding USD via Click/Payme/Uzum multi-currency accounts (CBU-licensed) for interim parking
- **Sanctions context**: Uzbekistan is **not under Western sanctions**; UZ banks have correspondent banking with EU + US + UK + GCC; some UZ banks limit USD/EUR services for nationals of sanctioned jurisdictions (Russia, Belarus) due to secondary-sanction risk — generally not an issue for EU/UK/US/CA/AU buyers

## Active fiscal incentives (2025–2026)

- **IT Park resident regime**: portal lists 0 % corporate, social, property + land tax and 0 % VAT, **7.5 % PIT** for employees ([outsource.gov.uz](https://outsource.gov.uz/en/why-uzbekistan/government-support), 2026-10-29), both until 1 Jan 2028 under Decree UP-5099 pts 5 and 8 (as amended by UP-6268); **1 Jan 2028–1 Jan 2040**: all taxes except VAT exempted only for residents exporting > 50 % of income or qualifying IT-education providers; non-resident founders' dividends taxed at resident rates (Tax Code Art. 483)
- **Free Economic Zones (FEZ)**: 21+ FEZs nationally — 3–10 year tax holidays + duty-free import for in-zone manufacturing
- **Law on Investments and Investment Activity (ZRU-598 of 25 Dec 2019)**: investor-protection guarantees, dispute-resolution improvements, free transfer of funds after taxes (Art. 17)
- **5-year "Golden Visa" (Investor Residence Permit, PF-67, in force 1 June 2025)** — **payment route** (decree point 26; IMI Daily calls it a donation): US$250k + US$150k/family member, no property condition (no implementing act published as of 12 Oct 2026 per Pactum, secondary — verify)
- **Separate real-estate-for-residence route** (pre-existing, distinct from the PF-67 payment-based Golden Visa): US$300k Tashkent / US$200k 6 named regions / US$100k other regions
- **IT Visa (3-year multi-entry)** for IT Park resident-company affiliates: real-estate purchase any value
- **IE regime for landlords — withdrawn**: from 1 Jan 2026 an IE's rental income is taxed at 12 % PIT (Tax Code Art. 465 pt 7); IE turnover tax is now 1 % on other income up to UZS 1 bn (Art. 467)
- **Subsidised mortgage programmes**: "**Yengil ipoteka (Light Mortgage)**" + various Ipotekabank programmes — mostly UZ-citizen-only (verify foreigner eligibility per programme)

## Caveats unique to UZ

- **Foreigners cannot own land** under Land Code Arts. 17–18 — only buildings + structures; a plot can be leased for **up to 25 years** (Land Code Art. 24), and flat owners hold the plot under a multi-apartment building for common use. LLC-with-citizen-co-owner workarounds for standalone houses are practitioner-grade but legally contestable — an LLC with ≥ 15 % foreign capital is itself lease-only (Land Code Art. 17 pt 3; Investment Law Art. 3); **verify with Azizov & Partners or equivalent local counsel** before structuring
- **Foreigners can buy new-build apartments + houses without a residence permit** (citizens of 108 countries, Cabinet Resolution No. 384 of 13 Jul 2022: under construction or commissioned ≤ 3 years; ≥ US$150k / US$180k in Tashkent region + the cities of Tashkent and Samarkand, ≥ US$70k / US$85k elsewhere); the purchase gives no right to residence — major liberalisation vs pre-2022 regime
- **5-year "Golden Visa" (Investor Residence Permit, PF-67, June 2025)** — **payment route** (decree point 26; IMI Daily calls it a donation): US$250k + US$150k/family member, **no property condition** (no implementing act published as of 12 Oct 2026 per Pactum, secondary; IMI Daily reported banks cannot accept payments until one is — verify operational status)
- **Separate real-estate-for-residence route** (pre-existing, distinct from the PF-67 payment-based Golden Visa): US$300k Tashkent / US$200k Samarkand+Bukhara+Namangan+Andijan+Fergana+Khorezm / US$100k other regions
- **IT Visa** — alternative pathway, no minimum threshold, 3-year multi-entry for IT Park resident-company affiliates
- **Annual property tax low**: 0.36 %/0.48 %/0.64 % on cadastral value from 1 Jan 2026 (0.34/0.45/0.6 % in 2025; Law ZRU-1108), residential base floor UZS 42M per object; typical Tashkent mid-segment flat US$56–US$200/year
- **Transaction costs low** — no transfer tax (Tax Code Art. 17); notarial state duty fixed at 1 BCV (UZS 440,000) in Tashkent, Nukus + regional centres, 0.5 BCV elsewhere; cadastre fee not verified — but **10 % of the contract value** for a foreigner with a residence permit buying a new build in Tashkent city or Tashkent region worth ≥ 3,300 BCV (Law ZRU-600 annex item 4(б))
- **CGT 12 %** on UZ real-estate sale gain for residents and non-residents (Tax Code Arts. 381, 382 pt 3); a home held ≥ 36 months is exempt (Art. 378 item 7; whether this covers non-residents is not confirmed)
- **2019 PIT reform**: single **12 %** rate from 1 Jan 2019 (Decree UP-5468) on employment + rental + capital gains for residents; **non-resident PIT on employment and other income also 12 %** (from prior 20 %) since 1 May 2022 (Tax Code Art. 382, Law ZRU-785)
- **2026 changes**: VAT 12 % unchanged; CIT 15 % unchanged; Law ZRU-1108 of 25 Dec 2025 (in force 1 Jan 2026) re-set Tax Code rates by ~7 %: individuals' residential property tax 0.34/0.45/0.6 → 0.36/0.48/0.64 %, per-m² minimum value Tashkent 3.3M → 3.53M UZS, land-tax base rates and all water-use rate lines; legal-entity property tax 1.5 % unchanged (2026 data, source lex.uz Tax Code)
- **Currency**: UZS de jure floating (IMF de facto crawl-like), +48 % UZS per USD from 8,100 (Sep 2017) to 12,012 (21 Oct 2026, CBU); CBU policy rate 14 % (held 16 Sep 2026 per Interfax, secondary; 28 Oct 2026 decision not confirmed); inflation 7.3 % (end-2025) → 6.5 % forecast (end-2026)
- **Tashkent seismic exposure**: moderate (post-1966 reconstruction predominates); pre-1966 stock minimal in central Tashkent (mostly destroyed by 1966 earthquake); but Samarkand + Bukhara + Fergana Valley old-mahalla pre-1966 stock requires structural survey
- **Aral Sea region (Karakalpakstan)**: chronic environmental hazard — toxic dust, respiratory + cancer risk; low property prices but acute long-term residence concern
- **Tashkent winter air quality**: chronic PM2.5 spikes; HEPA filtration recommended; gradual coal-to-gas transition ongoing
- **Water-scarcity climate trajectory**: dominant 2050 hazard; +1.75–2.25 °C warming; glacier loss; deficit growing 2 → 11–13 billion m³ — affects rural property utility supply + agriculture
- **Russian-relocation impact (2022–2024)**: Tashkent + Samarkand absorbed ~100k–150k relocants; structural mid-segment price + rent lift; partial unwinding 2024–2025
- **Soviet panel-block stock**: 1970s–1980s panel construction dominates Tashkent mid-segment outer districts; uneven seismic retrofit; commission independent structural survey
- **Self-built (samoyqurma) legalisation history**: pre-2007 unpermitted construction was waved through several legalisation rounds; some titles look clean but lack original building permit — affects future renovation/extension
- **Currency Regulation Law (ZRU-573 of 2019)**: 2017 + 2019 liberalisation post-Karimov-era; UZS settlement of UZ real estate; investor-protection guaranteed repatriation
- **STIR (TIN)**: 9-digit individual taxpayer ID; **prerequisite for property purchase, banking, IE registration**; foreigners obtain at any Tax Committee branch with passport + ~1 day
- **Notarial mandatory**: any property contract must be notarised (Civil Code) — not optional
- **Cadastre digital (e-Kadastr)**: launched + nationwide rollout near-complete (WB ISR confirmed 2.5× ahead of schedule); express 24h registration available in Tashkent

## Quirks to know

- **STIR (TIN) `XXXXXXXXX`** for individuals (9-digit); **prerequisite** for property purchase, IE registration, banking; obtain at any Tax Committee branch in ~1 day with passport
- **Tashkent district names**: Mirabad / Mirobod, Yakkasaray / Yakkasaroy, Yashnabad / Yashnobod, Mirzo Ulugbek / Mirzo Ulug'bek, Chilanzar / Chilonzor, Yunusabad / Yunusobod, Shaikhantakhur / Shayxontohur, Sergeli / Sergeli, Bektemir / Bektemir, Yangihayot / Yangihayot, Almazar / Olmazor, Uchtepa / Uchtepa — **dual transliteration** (Russian-derived vs Uzbek-Latin); confirm cadastral number not just street address

- **Mahalla committee (mahalla qoʻmitasi)**: traditional Uzbek neighbourhood self-government unit; ~10,000+ mahallas nationally; the mahalla raisi (chair) is a useful first contact for parcel-level due-diligence + community context
- **"Korzinka" / "Havas" / "Makro"**: dominant supermarket chains — proxy for "is this neighbourhood adequately served"
- **Click + Payme + Uzum**: dominant fintech super-apps — used for banking + RE classifieds + payments + e-gov interactions; foreign buyers should obtain a local-bank card + Click/Payme account for friction-free transactions (requires STIR + UZ-issued bank card)
- **Notarial mandatory**: any property contract must be notarised — not optional (Civil Code)
- **Cadastre same-day registration**: express 24h service available at major Public Service Centres in Tashkent
- **"Общая площадь" trap**: advertised m² typically includes balconies + walls (общая) — confirm cadastral m² not advertised m²
- **Dual-language documents**: contracts often in Uzbek (Latin) + Russian; legal effect with Uzbek-language version per Constitution but Russian text governs in practice for foreigner contracts
- **Karimov-era → Mirziyoyev-era**: pre-2017 documents may reference defunct ministries (e.g., "GKZGDK" State Committee for Land Resources — replaced by the Cadastre Agency under Presidential Decree UP-6061 of 7 Sep 2020); verify current authority for any pre-2017 record
- **Cyrillic legacy**: pre-2000 cadastral records may be Cyrillic-only; digitisation rollout has bridged most but rural archive verification may require translation
