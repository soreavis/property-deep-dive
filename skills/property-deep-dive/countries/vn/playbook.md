# Vietnam 🇻🇳 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `vn`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-04 / 2026-05).

> **🇻🇳 LEAD WARNING — read before any other section.** Vietnam is a **heavily-restricted** market. **All land in Vietnam is owned by the people (the State)** under Article 53 of the 2013 Constitution and Article 4 of the 2024 Land Law (Law No. 31/2024/QH15). What is bought and sold is the **Land Use Right (LUR / quyền sử dụng đất)** — not the land itself. **Foreigners cannot hold an LUR over land directly**: they may own the dwelling (house or apartment) on a **50-year leasehold** basis with one renewal of up to 50 years (Housing Law 2023, Law No. 27/2023/QH15, Article 20). Quotas (Housing Law 2023 Art. 19(1)): **30 % of the units in any one apartment building** and **250 landed houses (villas plus row houses) across an area of ward-equivalent population** — the ward-equivalence criterion is delegated to Decree 95/2024/ND-CP, so the counting unit is NOT simply the administrative ward (2026-08-07 verified). All numeric/title due diligence requires a Vietnamese-licensed lawyer + notary; DIY is not feasible because all titles and contracts are in Vietnamese and most provincial registries are paper-original. Six- to seven-figure decisions in Vietnam demand verifying current effective dates of the 2024 Land Law and 2023 Housing Law (**both in force 1 August 2024 — NOT 1 Aug 2025**; Law 43/2024/QH15, adopted 29 Jun 2024, replaced Art. 252(1) Land Law and Art. 197(1) Housing Law to bring commencement forward five months from the originally enacted 1 Jan 2025 — 2026-08-07 verified) plus their implementing Decrees (notably Decree 102/2024/ND-CP and Decree 95/2024/ND-CP, the latter issued 24 Jul 2024 and in force 1 Aug 2024) — non-negotiable.

## Country profile

- **Population**: ~100.3M (2023 GSO census update; 2024 estimate ~100.9M). Source: General Statistics Office (Tổng cục Thống kê) `https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/homepage/`. Vietnam is the 15th most populous country worldwide.
- **Postcode (mã bưu chính)**: 5-digit postal codes (introduced by Vietnam Post in 2018, replacing the 6-digit legacy system).
  - Hanoi (Hà Nội): `100000`–`159999` (e.g., `100000` Hoàn Kiếm District)
  - Ho Chi Minh City (Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh, "HCMC" / "Sài Gòn"): `700000`–`799999` (e.g., `700000` District 1)
  - Da Nang (Đà Nẵng): `550000`–`559999`
  - Hai Phong (Hải Phòng): `180000`–`189999`
  - Source: Vietnam Post `https://www.vnpost.vn/`
- **Admin levels — TWO tiers** (Law on Organization of Local Government No. 72/2025/QH15, **Art. 1(1)**): `cấp tỉnh` (provinces / centrally-managed cities) → `cấp xã` (communes / wards / special zones) — the law organises administrative units into those **two tiers only**. The intermediate `cấp huyện / quận / thị xã` (district) tier is not among them: district People's Committees **and their agencies ceased operating nationwide on 1 Jul 2025** (Art. 51(3)), so any pre-Jul-2025 guidance naming a district People's Committee is **void** (2026-08-07 verified; full text `https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-luat-so-72-2025-qh15-to-chuc-chinh-quyen-dia-phuong-119250618161434371.htm`). The pre-reform structure was 63 tỉnh/thành phố → huyện/quận/thị xã → xã/phường/thị trấn — **(superseded)**. **Province mergers — in force**: National Assembly Resolution 202/2025/QH15 (adopted 12 Jun 2025, in force on adoption; merged local governments operating from 1 Jul 2025 per Art. 2(2), read in a secondary full-text transcription) cut the 63 provinces / cities to **34 provincial-level units — 28 provinces + 6 centrally-run cities** (Hà Nội, Huế, Hồ Chí Minh City, Đà Nẵng, Hải Phòng, Cần Thơ), and NA Standing Committee resolutions in force 16 Jun 2025 left **3,321 commune-level units** (xã / phường / đặc khu) (2026-10-23 verified, Government portal `https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/chi-tiet-34-don-vi-hanh-chinh-cap-tinh-tu-12-6-2025-119250612141845533.htm` + `https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/danh-sach-3321-don-vi-hanh-chinh-cap-xa-tai-34-tinh-thanh-sau-sap-xep-sap-nhap-119250710102358656.htm`). A further commune-level rearrangement was tasked to the Ministry of Home Affairs in Sep 2026, so re-check the commune count at `https://www.moha.gov.vn/`. Province names in older addresses may be legacy — map them to the merged province before any lookup.
- **Currency**: VND (Vietnamese đồng, ₫). 1 USD ≈ 25,000–25,800 VND; 1 EUR ≈ 27,500–28,500 VND (Apr 2026 SBV reference rate; rates vary, verify at `https://www.sbv.gov.vn/`). VND is a **managed float** with a daily reference rate set by the State Bank of Vietnam and a ±5% trading band.
- **Capital controls**: Vietnam maintains capital controls under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange 2005 (Pháp lệnh Ngoại hối, amended 2013) and SBV Circular 06/2019/TT-NHNN. Foreign-currency repatriation of property sale proceeds requires SBV approval beyond the original-investment basis (the original inbound capital can be repatriated with bank documentation; profit/capital gain in excess requires explicit licensing). Source: State Bank of Vietnam `https://www.sbv.gov.vn/`.
- **Languages**: Vietnamese (official, tiếng Việt). English is reasonably common in HCMC and Hanoi for premium developers / law firms / English-language brochures. **All registered title documents and notarial deeds are in Vietnamese** — sworn translation + Vietnamese-licensed lawyer/notary are mandatory for foreign buyers.
- **Cadastre / land registry**: **Department of Land Administration / Cục Quản lý Đất đai**, under the **Ministry of Agriculture and Environment (Bộ Nông nghiệp và Môi trường, MAE — formed 1 Mar 2025 by merger of MONRE + MARD under National Assembly Resolution 176/2025/QH15; Department of Land Administration + VPĐKĐĐ re-housed under MAE; 2024 Land Law authorities unchanged in substance — 2026-05-27 verified)** `https://en.mae.gov.vn/`. Provincial-level registry is the **Office for Land Use Rights Registration (Văn phòng Đăng ký Quyền sử dụng đất, VPĐKĐĐ)** under the provincial Department of Natural Resources and Environment (Sở TN&MT). **No unified online national parcel viewer exists** as of 2026-05-01; queries must be made in person at the provincial / district office. Some provinces (e.g., HCMC, Hanoi) have started digital portals — verify at the provincial Sở TN&MT page.
- **Title document — Sổ Hồng (the "Pink Book")**: officially **Giấy chứng nhận quyền sử dụng đất, quyền sở hữu nhà ở và tài sản khác gắn liền với đất** (Certificate of Land Use Rights, Home Ownership and Other Assets Attached to Land). Issued under Decree 88/2009/ND-CP, then unified by Decree 43/2014/ND-CP and now governed by Decree 101/2024/ND-CP (under the 2024 Land Law). Replaced the older split between **Sổ Đỏ ("Red Book", land-use right only, pre-2009)** and **Sổ Hồng cũ (old Pink Book, building ownership only)**. As of 2009 the unified pink book is the standard. **Foreign buyers receive the Pink Book with a 50-year-term notation** and the renewable clause. Verify at MONRE `https://en.mae.gov.vn/`.
- **Major reforms / regulatory landscape** (date-stamped — see `shared/regulatory-watch.md` for full tracker):
  - **2024 Land Law (Luật Đất đai 2024, Law No. 31/2024/QH15)** — passed 18 Jan 2024, in force **1 August 2024** — NOT 1 Aug 2025: Law 43/2024/QH15 (adopted 29 Jun 2024) replaced Art. 252(1) to bring commencement forward five months from the originally enacted 1 Jan 2025 (the previously cited "Resolution 129/2024/QH15" is not the advancing instrument); re-verified in force and unamended on this point 2026-08-07. Replaces 2013 Land Law. Source: National Assembly `https://quochoi.vn/` and Government Gazette `https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/`.
  - **2023 Housing Law (Luật Nhà ở 2023, Law No. 27/2023/QH15)** — passed 27 Nov 2023, in force **1 August 2024** — NOT 1 Aug 2025: the same Law 43/2024/QH15 replaced Art. 197(1) on the identical five-month advance; re-verified 2026-08-07. Replaces 2014 Housing Law. Articles 17–20 govern foreign ownership — Art. 19(1) sets the foreign quotas, Art. 20(2)(c) the 50-year term.
  - **2023 Real Estate Business Law (Luật Kinh doanh Bất động sản 2023, Law No. 29/2023/QH15)** — passed 28 Nov 2023, effective **1 August 2025** — ⚠️ commencement date NOT re-verified in the 2026-08-07 pass, which covered only the Land Law and the Housing Law; verify at `https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/` before relying on it. Strengthens off-plan / pre-sale escrow and developer disclosure.
  - **Implementing Decrees** (effective alongside the laws): Decree 102/2024/ND-CP (general implementation Land Law), Decree 95/2024/ND-CP (implementation Housing Law — issued 24 Jul 2024, in force 1 Aug 2024; carries the ward-equivalence criterion for the 250-house quota and the 50-year extension procedure), Decree 96/2024/ND-CP (Real Estate Business Law), Decree 71/2024/ND-CP (land prices). Verify current text at `https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/`.
  - **Key 2024-Land-Law changes**: streamlined LUR issuance procedures; reformed land-price methodology (Art. 159(3): first new provincial table decided by the provincial People's Council and applied from 1 Jan 2026, then adjusted yearly — overridden by NA Resolution 254/2025/QH15 Art. 7(3), in force 1 Jan 2026: tables applied from 1 Jan 2026 and amended only when necessary); narrowed expropriation grounds; clarified the categories of agricultural land. ⚠️ **WITHDRAWN 2026-08-07** — the item previously listed here, "expanded mortgage rights to allow foreign-owned credit institutions as mortgagees (previously domestic-only)", is NOT verified against the primary text and is withdrawn: Land Law Art. 41 keeps the long-standing formula "credit institutions licensed to operate in Vietnam" and addresses foreign-invested organisations rather than individual foreign home buyers.
  - **Property tax (NOT enacted as of 2026-05-01)**: Ministry of Finance has consulted multiple times since 2017 on a recurring property tax law; as of 2026-05-01 no national recurring property tax exists in Vietnam. Only a small **non-agricultural land use tax** under Law 48/2010/QH12 (rates 0.03%–0.15% on land area × land price table, very low absolute amount). Verify at Ministry of Finance `https://mof.gov.vn/`.

## 🇻🇳 LEAD SECTION: Foreign buyer eligibility

### Bottom line

Foreigners **cannot directly hold a Land Use Right (LUR) over land in Vietnam**. They may own a **dwelling (apartment unit or landed house)** built on land — but the dwelling ownership is capped at **50 years** with one renewal of up to 50 years, and is subject to per-apartment-building (apartment) and per-area-of-ward-equivalent-population (landed house) quotas under Housing Law 2023 Art. 19(1). Land tenure remains with the State; the developer or the underlying landholder retains the LUR. The legal framework, in force since **1 August 2024** (NOT 1 Aug 2025 — Law 43/2024/QH15 of 29 Jun 2024 advanced commencement five months from the enacted 1 Jan 2025; 2026-08-07 verified), is the **2023 Housing Law (Law No. 27/2023/QH15)** and the **2024 Land Law (Law No. 31/2024/QH15)**, with implementing Decrees 95/2024 (24 Jul 2024, in force 1 Aug 2024) and 102/2024.

### Who is eligible (Housing Law 2023, Article 17)

Foreign buyers are eligible if they fall into one of the following categories:
1. **Foreign individuals** legally entering Vietnam (with valid visa or visa-exempt entry stamp; passport is sufficient — no work permit or temporary residence card required, a major liberalization vs. older interpretations).
2. **Foreign-invested enterprises** with an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) issued under the Investment Law 2020.
3. **Foreign branches and representative offices** of foreign organizations operating in Vietnam.
4. **Foreign-invested funds** and credit institutions operating in Vietnam.

Foreigners **do not need to be resident** in Vietnam to qualify — entering legally on a tourist visa is sufficient under Article 17. Verify the buyer's eligibility documentation BEFORE notarization, because the Pink Book will not issue otherwise.

### What foreigners CAN buy

- **Apartment units (căn hộ chung cư)** in commercial housing developments (dự án nhà ở thương mại) — the most common path
- **Landed houses (nhà ở riêng lẻ)** — villas / townhouses — within commercial housing developments — ⚠️ **PENDING, NOT LAW (2026-10-28)**: the Housing Law (amended) bill on the 2nd session of the XVI National Assembly (17 Oct-21 Nov 2026) targets this route — the bill as tabled in plenary on 22 Oct 2026 (Tờ trình 924/TTr-CP) removes foreign ownership of landed houses and keeps apartments only, implementing Resolution 21-NQ/TW of 28 Jul 2026 (NA portal `https://quochoi.vn/tintuc/Pages/tin-hoat-dong-cua-quoc-hoi.aspx?ItemID=102354`); it leaves the foreign apartment ratio (draft Art. 21(1)) to the Government instead of the statutory 30 %, and the NA verifying committee asks for the ratio to be written into the law (secondary reports). Plenary debate held 28 Oct 2026; no adoption vote as of 2026-10-28. Housing Law 2023 Art. 19-20 govern until a new law is adopted AND in force; transition for existing foreign landed-house owners and the effective date not verified in the tabled text.
- Foreigners **cannot buy land directly** (no agricultural plots, no bare land, no rural plots without an attached registered dwelling within an approved housing project)
- Foreigners **cannot buy resale from Vietnamese owners outside the commercial housing project framework** in most cases — the unit must originally have been allocated within a foreign-eligible project's quota

### Quotas (Housing Law 2023 Articles 19–20; Decree 95/2024/ND-CP)

| Restriction | Cap | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Apartment building** | ≤ **30 %** of total units in any single apartment building | Housing Law 2023 Art. 19(1) counts **per building, not per project** — in a multi-building project each block is separately capped (2026-08-07 verified) |
| **Landed houses per area of ward-equivalent population** | ≤ **250** landed houses (villas plus row houses) | Housing Law 2023 Art. 19(1) counts across *an area whose population is equivalent to a ward-level unit* — NOT the administrative ward as such; the ward-equivalence criterion is delegated to Decree 95/2024/ND-CP, so confirm the counting area in writing with the developer (2026-08-07 verified) |
| **Single landed-house project** | ≤ 10 % of project's houses for foreigners (within the 250-house / ward-equivalent-population-area cap) | Decree-level claim NOT covered by the 2026-08-07 primary-text re-verification, which covered Housing Law Art. 19(1) and Art. 20(2)(c) but no Decree-level text — verify against current Decree 95/2024/ND-CP text |

### Quota verification — DO THIS BEFORE PAYING DEPOSIT

1. Ask the developer (chủ đầu tư) for a **Foreign Quota Confirmation Letter (Văn bản xác nhận hạn mức người nước ngoài)** — must be in writing, signed and stamped, and reference the specific project's MOC notification.
2. The developer must have notified the provincial **Department of Construction (Sở Xây dựng)** of the foreign-eligible units list — under Article 79 Decree 95/2024/ND-CP, the Sở Xây dựng publishes the list of eligible projects and remaining foreign quota per project.
3. Cross-check via the provincial Sở Xây dựng public list (HCMC: `https://soxaydung.hochiminhcity.gov.vn/`; Hanoi: `https://soxaydung.hanoi.gov.vn/`). The list of foreign-eligible projects is updated periodically.
4. **Trap**: a unit "marketed to foreigners" by a broker is not automatically in foreign quota; the developer must confirm in writing tied to the **specific unit number**.

### Tenure: 50 years + 50 years renewable (Housing Law 2023 Article 20)

- Initial term: **50 years from the date of Pink Book issuance**.
- Renewable **once** for up to another 50 years, subject to government approval and a re-application process at the end of the first term.
- Renewal procedure: file with the provincial People's Committee 12 months before expiry; renewal is in principle granted unless the property is in a defense / security area.
- **What happens if renewal is denied**: the foreign owner must dispose (sell or transfer to a qualifying buyer) within 12 months. If not, the State acquires the property without compensation beyond the residual value computation.
- **Inheritance window**: foreign heirs of a deceased foreign owner — **12 months** to either sell to a Vietnamese citizen / qualifying foreigner OR transfer to a qualifying heir. After 12 months, the State acquires per Article 8 Housing Law 2023.
- **Vietnamese spouse / Việt kiều (overseas Vietnamese) exception**: a foreign owner married to a Vietnamese citizen, OR a Việt kiều (overseas Vietnamese with proof of Vietnamese ethnic origin per the Law on Vietnamese Nationality), may hold ownership on the **same terms as Vietnamese citizens** (no 50-year cap, no quota). Verify Việt kiều status with the Ministry of Public Security.

### What foreigners CANNOT buy / restricted areas

- **National defense / security areas (khu vực bảo đảm quốc phòng, an ninh)** — defined in Decree 35/2009/ND-CP and updated under Decree 95/2024. Foreign ownership in these areas is blanket-prohibited; the Ministry of National Defense (Bộ Quốc phòng) maintains the list. Pre-2024 had broader bans; the 2024 Land Law narrowed them but they remain firm.
- **Coastal strategic zones** within prescribed distances of military bases or the coastline — verify at the provincial Sở Xây dựng before negotiating.
- **Properties without a clean Pink Book** — in particular, units in projects where the developer has not finished allocation procedures, or where the project itself lacks a valid Land Use Right Certificate.
- **Bare agricultural land (đất nông nghiệp)** — never to foreigners.
- **Resale Vietnamese-owned units outside the project allocation framework** — case-by-case under Article 19 Housing Law 2023, generally not permitted.

### Pink Book mechanics (foreign-buyer specifics)

- The Pink Book issued to a foreign buyer **explicitly notes**:
  - Owner's nationality and passport number
  - Tenure: 50 years from <date>, renewable
  - The legal basis (Housing Law 2023 + project Land Use Right reference)
- Issuance timeline: in practice **3–12 months after notarized sale and full payment** at the provincial VPĐKĐĐ. Delays are common, especially for newer developments where the developer hasn't completed master-Pink-Book allocation. **Never assume Pink Book is "imminent" — get a written timeline from the developer with penalty clauses.**
- A foreign owner CAN mortgage the property — but **only with credit institutions licensed to operate in Vietnam** (this includes branches of foreign banks — pre-existing playbook wording, NOT covered by the 2026-08-07 re-verification; confirm with the specific lender). ⚠️ **Corrected 2026-08-07**: the earlier claim that Decree 101/2024/ND-CP under the 2024 Land Law expanded mortgage rights to include 100 % foreign-owned credit institutions is **withdrawn** — it is not verified against the primary text. Land Law Art. 41 keeps the long-standing formula "credit institutions licensed to operate in Vietnam" and addresses foreign-invested organisations rather than individual foreign home buyers. Treat foreign-buyer mortgage availability as **est.** and confirm with the specific lender.

### Notarization & registration process

1. **Sale and Purchase Agreement (Hợp đồng mua bán, HĐMB)** signed and notarized at a public notary office (Phòng Công chứng) — mandatory for any property transfer (Notary Law 2014).
2. Within **30 days** of notarization, the parties (typically the buyer, via the notary or developer) file the **Application for Land Use Rights Registration / Pink Book transfer (Đơn đăng ký biến động đất đai)** at the provincial / district VPĐKĐĐ.
3. **Tax filing**: 2% PIT on transfer (gross sale price, paid by seller — but practically often agreed as buyer-paid in foreign transactions); 0.5% registration fee (lệ phí trước bạ, paid by buyer).
4. VPĐKĐĐ issues the new Pink Book in the buyer's name. Statutory timeline: 15 working days; practical timeline: 30–90 days for clean transfers, longer for foreign buyers.

### FX / payment requirement

- Foreign buyers must remit purchase funds **from abroad in foreign currency, converted to VND inside Vietnam**, via a Vietnamese commercial bank (Circular 06/2019/TT-NHNN). The bank issues remittance documentation that is required at notarization.
- Cash or third-party payments outside the banking system are **not accepted** for foreign-buyer transfers and will block Pink Book issuance.
- For repatriation of sale proceeds: original capital + reasonable financing returns typically permitted with bank documentation; profit/gain may require additional SBV approval. Plan exit liquidity carefully.

### Confidence

**HIGH** — Land Law 2024 (No. 31/2024/QH15), Housing Law 2023 (No. 27/2023/QH15), Real Estate Business Law 2023 (No. 29/2023/QH15), and implementing Decrees (102/2024, 95/2024, 96/2024, 101/2024, 71/2024) are all primary statutes published in the Government Gazette `https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/`. **MEDIUM** for parcel-level enforcement variation across provinces (HCMC and Hanoi run more digital / faster Pink Book issuance than rural provinces). **MEDIUM** for the practical foreign-quota tracker availability (Sở Xây dựng lists exist but freshness varies — verify project-specific in writing).

### Compute

1. Listing price/m² = listing VND ÷ usable area (diện tích sử dụng / "carpet area"). **Trap**: many Vietnamese listings advertise on **diện tích thông thủy (interior wall-to-wall, similar to carpet)** OR **diện tích tim tường (centerline of walls, ~5–10% larger)** — clarify which. Historically developers preferred tim-tường (lower price/m² appearance); the Housing Law 2023 (27/2023/QH15) itself sets how apartment usable area is calculated — Art. 3(4) bans applying a wrong calculation of usable area 'prescribed in this Law', and VTC News (14 Dec 2024, secondary) cites Art. 143(1): usable area by thông thủy dimensions, counting balconies, loggias and internal partition walls but not party or external walls, columns or technical shafts. The older 'since 2014' and 'used for tax' claims are **not verified**, and listings still vary — confirm which area the sale contract and the Pink Book state. The 5–10 % tim-tường uplift is est.
2. Compare to MOC quarterly housing report for the same district.
3. Compare to nearest 3–5 same-project / same-street listings on Batdongsan.com.vn or Chotot.
4. Pull the provincial **land price table** (current version — amended when necessary, NA Resolution 254/2025/QH15 Art. 7(3)) for the parcel — statutory tax base; est. 30–60 % of market (no primary comparison — varies by street).
5. **Trap (foreign quota premium)**: apartment units sold within foreign quota frequently list est. 5–15 % higher (no primary source) than the same-floor Vietnamese-quota equivalent (limited supply). Verify whether the unit is "foreign-eligible" (suất ngoại) or not — they are NOT interchangeable for foreign buyers.
6. **Trap (developer pre-sale risk)**: many new launches sell off-plan. The Real Estate Business Law 2023 (Law No. 29/2023/QH15, in force 1 Aug 2024 per Law 43/2024/QH15) caps payments for future housing (nhà ở hình thành trong tương lai): a deposit of at most 5 % of the price, taken only once the project qualifies for sale (Art. 23(5)); a first instalment of at most 30 % of the contract value including the deposit, then payments by construction progress, at most 70 % before handover (50 % if the seller is a foreign-invested economic organisation under Art. 10(4)); and at most 95 % until the buyer's certificate (Pink Book) is issued (Art. 25(1), (3)). A Real Estate Business Law (amended) bill is before the Oct–Nov 2026 National Assembly session (see `shared/regulatory-watch.md`), so re-check these caps. Completion delays remain common — discount accordingly.

### Verdict bands (vehicles per day, mixed motorbike + car)

> **Vietnam-specific note**: vehicle counts in Vietnam dominantly mean **motorbikes (xe máy)** — registrations stood at **84,003,180 motorbikes vs 7,134,939 cars** (Traffic Police / Cục CSGT data as reported by VOV, 8 Jul 2026 — state media, secondary). A "10,000 v/d" Vietnamese street is overwhelmingly two-wheeler traffic with very different noise / pollution / safety profile vs an OECD car-dominant equivalent.

- 🟢 < 1,000 v/d: rural lane, ngõ / hẻm (alley), commune road
- 🟡 1,000–10,000 v/d: small provincial road, urban side street
- 🟠 10,000–40,000 v/d: provincial trunk, urban arterial, ring road feeder
- 🔴 > 40,000 v/d: national highway, HCMC/Hanoi major arterial, expressway, ring road

### Coarse fallback (OSM `highway` tag)

- `motorway` / `trunk` = expressway (cao tốc) / national highway 1-digit (Quốc lộ 1A, 5, 51, etc.)
- `primary` = national highway 2-digit
- `secondary` = provincial road (đường tỉnh)
- `tertiary` = district road / urban collector
- `residential` / `living_street` = ngõ / hẻm (alley) — narrow access typical in old-quarter HCMC / Hanoi

### HCMC-specific quirks

- HCMC traffic is severe and chronic; AADT alone understates exposure because of stop-and-go congestion + motorbike density.
- **Air quality** — HCMC arterials in dry season (Dec–Apr) show PM2.5 est. 60–120 µg/m³ on bad days (not re-verified 2026-10-29 — read current station data before relying on it) (WHO 2021 24-hour guideline 15 µg/m³, confirmed). Source: PAM Air `https://pamair.org/` and US Embassy AQI monitor.
- **Hẻm (alley) properties** — historic neighborhoods (Quận 1, Quận 3, Quận 5) are dense alley networks. A property in a deep hẻm has very low traffic but limited vehicle access (motorbike only past a certain depth).
- Elevated highways (e.g., Bến Lức – Long Thành expressway, ~58 km, scheduled to open end to end from the evening of 30 Sep 2026 per a same-day Đại biểu Nhân dân report) over residential areas generate noise + particulate exposure 24/7.

### Hanoi-specific quirks

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax — none on buildings; small land tax

> **Headline**: Vietnam has **no recurring property tax on dwellings** as of 2026-05-01. The Ministry of Finance has consulted multiple times since 2017 on a national property tax law; none enacted. Only a small **non-agricultural land use tax** under Law 48/2010/QH12 applies, computed on land area × statutory price.

**Non-agricultural Land Use Tax (Thuế sử dụng đất phi nông nghiệp)** — Law No. 48/2010/QH12, effective 1 January 2012:

| Use class | Rate |
|---|---|
| Residential land within statutory limit (hạn mức) | 0.03 % |
| Excess up to 3× statutory limit | 0.07 % |
| Above 3× statutory limit | 0.15 % |
| Commercial land | 0.03 % |
| Land used for wrong purpose / abandoned | 0.15 % |

- **Tax base**: land area (m²) × **statutory land price** per the provincial land-price table (Bảng giá đất, Decree 71/2024/ND-CP under 2024 Land Law), **stabilised over a 5-year cycle** (Law 48/2010/QH12 Art. 6(3) as amended by 2024 Land Law Art. 249).
- **Practical absolute amount**: typically **VND 50,000 – 500,000 / year (~$2–$20)** for a standard apartment unit's land share — negligible by international standards.
- Source: Ministry of Finance `https://mof.gov.vn/` and General Department of Taxation `https://www.gdt.gov.vn/`.

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase)

| Tax | Rate | Base | Who pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal Income Tax on transfer (Thuế TNCN từ chuyển nhượng BĐS)** | **2 %** of gross sale price | Sale price OR statutory land price + dwelling value, whichever higher | **Seller** (statutorily) — practically negotiable; in foreign-buyer purchases often agreed as buyer-paid |
| **Registration fee (Lệ phí trước bạ)** | **0.5 %** | Sale price (subject to floor at the statutory price table value) | **Buyer** |
| **Notary fee (Phí công chứng)** | sliding scale, ~0.05–0.1 % of transaction | Contract value | Negotiable; commonly buyer |
| **VAT (Thuế GTGT)** | **10 %** | New build sale price (excluded for resale of existing buildings, excluded for land value) | Embedded in developer pricing — buyer ultimately bears |
| **Brokerage fee** | typically **1–2 %** (foreign-facing brokers may charge 2–3 %) | Sale price | Negotiable; commonly seller for resale, embedded for new-build |
| **Translation + lawyer fees (foreign buyer)** | $1,000–$5,000 typical | Flat or hourly | Buyer |

### Worked example — foreign buyer of a 5,000,000,000 VND (≈$196,000 at 25,500/USD) HCMC apartment from a Vietnamese individual seller (resale)

- PIT (seller, statutorily): 5,000M × 2 % = 100M VND (~$3,920) — often shifted to buyer in foreign-purchase negotiations
- Registration fee (buyer): 5,000M × 0.5 % = 25M VND (~$980)
- Notary: ~3–5M VND (~$120–$200)
- Lawyer (foreign-buyer due diligence + translation): ~25–75M VND (~$1,000–$3,000)
- **Buyer-side total (assuming PIT borne by seller)**: ~30–105M VND (~$1,200–$4,100), roughly **0.6–2.1 % of price**
- **Buyer-side total (assuming PIT shifted to buyer)**: ~130–205M VND (~$5,100–$8,000), roughly **2.6–4.1 % of price**

### Worked example — foreign buyer of a 5,000,000,000 VND new-build HCMC apartment from a developer

- VAT: already in price (10 % on building value, excluded land value share); developer breaks down on invoice
- Registration fee (buyer): 25M VND (~$980)
- Notary: ~3–5M VND
- Lawyer + translation: ~25–75M VND
- **Buyer-side total** (excluding VAT, which is in headline price): ~55–105M VND, roughly **1.1–2.1 % of price**

### Rental income tax

**Personal Income Tax (PIT) on rental** — Article 4 Circular 92/2015/TT-BTC (revised by Circular 40/2021/TT-BTC):

- **Resident or non-resident landlord** with rental income > **100M VND/year (~$3,920)**: **5 % PIT** + **5 % VAT** = **10 % flat on gross rental** (no expense deduction).
- Below 100M VND/year: **exempt** from both PIT and VAT.
- Source: General Department of Taxation `https://www.gdt.gov.vn/` and Ministry of Finance Circular 40/2021/TT-BTC.
- Filing: monthly OR quarterly OR annual at choice (most landlords annual). Form 01/CNKD (cá nhân kinh doanh).

### Inheritance & gift tax

- **Inheritance tax**: **10 % PIT** on the inherited property value above 10M VND threshold per estate, EXCEPT for direct family (spouse, parents, children, siblings, grandparents) which are **EXEMPT**.
- **Gift tax**: same — 10 % above 10M VND threshold, family exemption.
- Source: Article 4 Personal Income Tax Law 2007, amended.

### Future risk

- **National property tax law** — Ministry of Finance consultation ongoing since 2017; multiple draft bills; none enacted as of 2026-05-01. If enacted, expected rate range 0.3 %–1 % on second-and-subsequent properties or on properties above a value threshold. Verify periodically at `https://mof.gov.vn/` and National Assembly `https://quochoi.vn/`.
- **Land-price tables under the 2024 Land Law** — the 2013-law tables ran to 31 Dec 2025 (Art. 257(1)); new tables apply from 1 Jan 2026. Art. 159(3) provided yearly adjustment, but NA Resolution 254/2025/QH15 Art. 7(3) (in force 1 Jan 2026) has the provincial People's Council amend them only when necessary. The non-agricultural land-use-tax base is the table price **stabilised over a 5-year cycle** (Law 48/2010/QH12 Art. 6(3) as amended by Land Law Art. 249), so the tax follows table revisions only at cycle resets — next cycle start and which table it adopts: data not publicly available — verify at the provincial tax office. Pending: the Land Law (amended) bill before the 2nd session of the XVI National Assembly (17 Oct-21 Nov 2026; Government targets enactment before 1 Mar 2027) may change the table mechanism again.

### Confidence

**HIGH** for the statutory framework (PIT Law, VAT Law, Non-agricultural Land Use Tax, Registration Fee Decree are all primary statutes published by the National Assembly and Government). **MEDIUM** for the practical incidence of who actually pays which tax (heavily negotiated, especially in foreign-buyer transactions). **MEDIUM** for the timing of any future national property tax (consultation ongoing).

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential rental income tax

(See `--tax` above for the headline 5 % PIT + 5 % VAT = 10 % flat on gross rental above 100M VND/year threshold.)

### Short-term rentals (Airbnb / Booking)

**The headline rule**: Vietnam does **not have a national STR-licensing framework** equivalent to Spain's VTV or Portugal's AL. STR operates in a regulatory grey zone, governed by:

1. **Tourism Law 2017** (Luật Du lịch, Law No. 09/2017/QH14) — defines categories of accommodation businesses (homestay, hostel, hotel) and registration requirements with the provincial Department of Tourism.
2. **Investment Law 2020** + **Enterprise Law 2020** — for individuals running STR commercially, registration as a household business (hộ kinh doanh) or enterprise is required if scale exceeds individual hobby threshold.
3. **Apartment building bylaws (Quy chế quản lý chung cư)** — under Housing Law 2023 and Decree 95/2024, the apartment building's management board (Ban Quản trị) sets bylaws; **most premium HCMC and Hanoi buildings explicitly prohibit short-term rental** to maintain residential character and security.
4. **Provincial and ward people's committee rules** — HCMC has codified STR as registration-conditional tourist accommodation (Decision 19/2026/QĐ-UBND); for Hanoi / Da Nang no city-level STR decision equivalent to HCMC's was identified at the 2026-07-02 check — verify at each People's Committee whether short stays must register as "tourist accommodation". **District-level enforcement framing is OBSOLETE**: `cấp huyện` (district) People's Committees and their agencies **ceased operating nationwide on 1 Jul 2025** (Law on Organization of Local Government No. 72/2025/QH15, **Art. 51(3)**), and **Art. 1(1)** leaves two tiers only — `cấp tỉnh` (province) and `cấp xã` (commune / ward / special zone). STR enforcement now sits with **provincial and ward (phường) People's Committees**, and any pre-Jul-2025 source naming a district People's Committee is void. **HCMC has codified the rule city-wide rather than leaving it to ward discretion** — Quyết định 19/2026/QĐ-UBND: **Art. 12(3)** requires a tourist apartment let in a condominium to register a tourist-accommodation establishment (đăng ký hoạt động cơ sở lưu trú du lịch) and comply with tourism law; **Art. 12(4)(a)** adds tax registration and declaration plus residence, security, e-commerce, sanitation and fire-safety duties; **Art. 12(4)(b)** requires temporary-residence registration for tenants; **Art. 17** assigns state housing-management responsibility to the `cấp xã` People's Committee — the decision text contains zero references to `cấp huyện` or `quận`. The widely repeated Pink-Book-plus-business-licence ward-practice claim is **anecdotal — no primary source located as of 2026-08-07** (that is a search result, not a finding that no such requirement exists anywhere); treat any ownership-certificate demand as **building-specific** and verify per ward. Source: Provincial PCs + MOCST + `https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-luat-so-72-2025-qh15-to-chuc-chinh-quyen-dia-phuong-119250618161434371.htm` (2026-08-07 verified). **National fine (2026-10-21 verified)**: Decree 339/2026/NĐ-CP (issued and in force 26 Aug 2026; repeals Decree 16/2022/NĐ-CP) Art. 62(3)(d) fines VND 100–130 million for using a condominium apartment for a non-residential purpose, except letting it as a residence under housing law — individual rate per Art. 5(4)(b), organisations double; the clause does not itself address registered day/week tourist lets — verify per case.

**Practical reality (2026)**:
- **HCMC** — many premium buildings (Vinhomes, Masteri, Estella, Diamond Island) actively police and forbid Airbnb. Enforcement is via the Ban Quản trị + building security; non-compliant unit owners face escalating fines from the management board and ultimately legal action.
- **Hanoi** — similar; Vinhomes Ocean Park, Royal City, Times City buildings forbid STR.
- **Da Nang / Nha Trang / Phu Quoc / Hoi An** — STR is more tolerated in foreign-buyer-marketed condotels (designed for hotel-style operation) and in tourist-facing developments. Specific projects market themselves with developer-managed rental pools (similar to Thailand condotel model).
- **Foreign owners specifically**: per Article 19 Housing Law 2023, foreign owners may rent out their property — but must **declare and pay rental tax** at the GDT, and must comply with local accommodation business registration if STR. Failure to declare rental income is a tax offense (administrative penalty + back tax + interest).

**Tax treatment of STR**:
- If treated as rental income: 5 % PIT + 5 % VAT (above 100M VND/yr threshold) — same as long-term rental.
- If formally registered as a tourist accommodation business: PIT progressive up to 35 % OR flat 1.5 % presumptive (lump-sum tax / thuế khoán) under Circular 40/2021/TT-BTC for household business; VAT 5 % accommodation rate; tourist tax (if any) per provincial ordinance.
- Multi-unit professional operators must register as enterprise, file CIT 20 %.

### Strategic notes

- **HCMC central apartments (Quận 1, 2, 3, 7)**: long-term yields est. 3–5 % gross (no primary yield statistic; Batdongsan's Q3 2026 report, listing-based, puts the city-wide old-HCMC apartment rental yield at 3.4 % and street-front shophouse yields at 2.3 % central / 3.1 % near-central); STR explicitly forbidden by most premium-building Ban Quản trị.
- **Hanoi central apartments**: est. 3–5 % gross long-term (no primary yield statistic; Batdongsan's Q3 2026 report, listing-based, puts the city-wide Hanoi apartment rental yield at 2.4 %, below this range).
- **Da Nang beachfront condotels**: developer-managed pool yields est. 6–10 % gross (no measured source; Batdongsan's Q3 2026 report puts ordinary Da Nang apartment rental yields at 4.5 %), but condotel legal structure (separate from standard residential) involves additional disclosure risk — many condotels were marketed pre-2018 with guaranteed returns that subsequently failed (Cocobay Đà Nẵng — its developer Empire Group, which had promised up to 12 %/yr, said in Nov 2019 it would stop paying committed returns from 1 Jan 2020). Verify any condotel deal with VN-licensed lawyer.
- **Phu Quoc, Nha Trang resort developments**: similar — high-yield marketing, real returns vary widely; many failed-completion cases since 2018.
- **Hoi An**: limited foreign-eligible projects; old-quarter homestays popular but legal complexity high (UNESCO heritage zone restrictions + foreign-eligibility limits).

### Confidence

**HIGH** for the rental income tax framework (PIT + VAT statutes are primary). **HIGH** for *which authority* enforces STR (Law on Organization of Local Government No. 72/2025/QH15 + HCMC Quyết định 19/2026/QĐ-UBND are primary — provincial and ward tier only; no district tier since 1 Jul 2025). **MEDIUM** for STR enforcement intensity (varies by city, building, and political cycle). **MEDIUM** for condotel operating-pool yields (developer disclosure quality variable; multiple historical failed projects).

### Work permit & visa

**Foreign nationals working in Vietnam require a work permit (Giấy phép lao động)** issued by the provincial People's Committee (Ủy ban nhân dân cấp tỉnh), which may delegate the task to a competent agency (Decree 219/2025/NĐ-CP Art. 4); the labour portfolio sits under the **Ministry of Home Affairs (Bộ Nội vụ)** since 1 Mar 2025 (MOLISA's portfolio was dissolved into it). Governed by the **Labour Code 2019 (Bộ luật Lao động 2019, Law No. 45/2019/QH14, effective 1 January 2021)** and **Decree 219/2025/NĐ-CP** (signed and in force 7 Aug 2025), whose Art. 35(2) ended the foreign-worker provisions of Decree 152/2020/ND-CP as amended by Decree 70/2023/ND-CP; permits issued under the old decrees stay valid until they expire (Art. 34(1)).

Key requirements:
1. Sponsoring Vietnamese employer (registered enterprise)
2. The employer's report explaining the need for the foreign worker goes inside the work-permit dossier; the competent authority approves the need and issues the permit within 10 working days of a complete dossier (Decree 219/2025/NĐ-CP Art. 18(1), Art. 22(3))
3. Category tests (Decree 219/2025/NĐ-CP Art. 3): expert = university degree + at least 2 years' relevant experience (1 year in finance / science / technology / innovation / national digital transformation or other designated priority fields); executive director heading a field = at least 3 years in that field; technical worker = at least 1 year's training + 2 years' experience, or at least 3 years' experience; manager = enterprise manager under Enterprise Law Art. 4(24) or head / deputy head of an organisation
4. Health certificate, criminal record check, work-experience confirmation
5. Validity: up to **2 years** (Decree 219/2025/NĐ-CP Art. 21(1)), extendable **once only** for up to 2 more years (Labour Code Art. 155)

**Exemptions** (no work permit needed; Decree 219/2025/NĐ-CP Art. 7, whose clause 1 incorporates Labour Code Art. 154 clauses 3-8): foreign lawyers licensed under the Law on Lawyers; foreigners married to a Vietnamese citizen and living in Vietnam (no TRC condition in the text); heads of representative offices / projects of international organisations and foreign NGOs; stays under 3 months to offer services or to fix complex technical incidents; owners / members of an LLC and board chairs / members of a JSC with a capital contribution of VND 3 billion or more (Art. 7(2)-(3)); managers, executive directors, experts and technical workers in Vietnam for under 90 days in a calendar year, or intra-corporate transferees in the 11 WTO-committed service sectors employed by the foreign enterprise for at least 12 continuous months (Art. 7(13)); students / trainees with an internship agreement or job offer (Art. 7(7)); people confirmed by a ministry or provincial People's Committee to work in finance, science, technology, innovation, national digital transformation or priority fields (Art. 7(15)); plus other listed cases (ODA advisers, accredited journalists, volunteers, official-passport holders). Labour Code Art. 154(8a), added by Law 71/2025/QH15 from 1 Jan 2026, also exempts high-quality digital-technology-industry personnel. (Re-verified item by item 2026-10-29; 'senior managers of foreign-invested companies' is not an exemption as such.)

### Self-employment / freelance reality

- A foreign freelancer in Vietnam still legally needs a work permit if working physically in Vietnam for Vietnamese clients.
- Working remotely for foreign employers from Vietnam is in a grey zone — increasingly tolerated under tourist / DT visa, but no formal "digital nomad visa" exists as of 2026-05-01 (multiple proposals tabled in 2024–2025; verify at `https://chinhphu.vn/`).
- Foreign entrepreneur incorporating a Vietnamese enterprise: needs IRC + Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) + minimum capital (varies by sector; est. USD 10k–50k in practice — no general statutory minimum verified), then can self-sponsor a DT visa.

### Flood & typhoon (lũ lụt và bão)

- **Annual typhoon season**: June–November, with peak August–October. Vietnam's coast receives est. **6–10 typhoons or tropical storms per year** (not matched to an NCHMF long-term-average table in the 2026-10-29 pass — verify at `https://nchmf.gov.vn/`), mostly affecting central coast (Da Nang incl. former Quang Nam, Hue, Quang Ngai) and northern coast (Hai Phong, Quang Ninh).
- **HCMC flooding**: chronic during the rainy season (May–Nov). HCMC subsidence is est. **2–5 cm/yr** in some districts (heavy groundwater extraction + sediment compaction in the Mekong Delta; HCMC Climate Change Bureau / HIDS figure, not re-located in the 2026-10-29 pass) — combined with coastal sea-level rise of about 3.34 mm/yr at Vietnam's tide gauges in 1993–2014 (3.5 ± 0.7 mm/yr by satellite; MONRE 2016 scenario report), parts of HCMC face increasing tidal flooding (triều cường) at full-moon high tides.
- **Hanoi flooding**: Red River basin flood risk; major historical floods (1971, 2008). Hanoi inner-city has improved drainage post-2008 but outlying districts remain exposed.
- **Mekong Delta**: low-lying (mostly < 2 m above sea level); existential long-term climate risk — with 100 cm sea-level rise and no adaptation, **38.9 %** of the Mekong Delta would be at high risk of inundation (MONRE Climate Change and Sea Level Rise Scenarios for Viet Nam, 2016, which projects coastal rise of about 22 cm (RCP4.5) to 25 cm (RCP8.5) by 2050 and 53 to 73 cm by 2100). The earlier "IPCC projects 50 cm sea-level rise + saltwater intrusion may displace 20 % of population (Mekong Delta Plan 2013)" claim is withdrawn — not verified in the 2026-10-29 pass.
- **Verdict bands** (parcel-level requires provincial Sở TN&MT flood-zone map):
  - 🔴 Mekong Delta provinces (Cần Thơ city incl. former Sóc Trăng, Cà Mau, Vĩnh Long incl. former Bến Tre — 2025 province merger, NA Resolution 202/2025/QH15); HCMC low-lying (District 7 outer, District 4, Nhà Bè, Bình Chánh, parts of District 8 and Bình Tân)
  - 🟠 HCMC inner mid (Quận 1, 3, 4 — protected by drainage but tidal flooding episodes); central coast cities (Da Nang incl. Hoi An — former Quảng Nam merged into Đà Nẵng city in 2025 — and Hue; typhoon-exposed)
  - 🟡 Hanoi inner districts (post-2008 drainage improvements, but Long Biên / Tây Hồ riverbank exposed)
  - 🟢 Northern hilly upcountry (Sapa, Da Lat highlands, central highlands Buôn Ma Thuột); HCMC elevated areas (Thủ Đức eastern, Quận 9 high ground)

### Typhoon-specific exposure

- **Central coast (Da Nang incl. Hoi An, Hue)**: direct typhoon hits 2–4 per decade; storm surge + structural wind risk. New construction post-2010 typically built to higher wind-load standard.
- **Northern coast (Hai Phong, Quang Ninh, Hanoi)**: typhoon tail-ends; flooding risk dominant
- **HCMC and southern coast**: rarely directly hit by typhoons; tropical depressions and monsoon flooding instead. (The earlier "Sunda Trench shielding similar to Bangkok" explanation is withdrawn — no source located in the 2026-10-29 pass.)
- 2020 Typhoons (Linfa, Nangka, Saudel, Molave, Etau, Vamco — sequential October-November; the earlier "est. USD ~1.3B damage" figure is withdrawn — not matched to an official source in the 2026-10-29 pass); 2024 Yagi (storm No. 3, Sep 2024) was a major typhoon impacting Hải Phòng and the Hanoi area severely. **National disaster totals** (NSO annual socio-economic reports): **2024** — 570 dead and missing, property damage nearly VND 89,253.6 billion; **2025** — 489 dead and missing, 340.2 thousand houses collapsed or damaged, property damage nearly VND 102,665.3 billion (+15.0 % on 2024).

### Seismicity (động đất)

- Most of Vietnam: low-to-moderate seismic risk; **NOT a major earthquake country** by global standards.
- **Higher-risk fault zones** (per VIGMR active-fault map):
  - **Northwest fault zones — Điện Biên – Mường Lay and Sông Mã – Tuần Giáo – Lai Châu** (Sơn La / Điện Biên / Lai Châu provinces): Điện Biên basin 1935 M6.9 (the 6.75 carried here earlier differs — magnitude estimates for this event vary), Tuần Giáo 1983 M6.7 (the earlier "Mường Lay 1983" label is corrected), Điện Biên Phủ 2001 M5.3 (Tiền Phong, 15 Aug 2022 — secondary; Institute of Geophysics catalogue not read)
  - **Sông Hồng (Red River) fault zone** (north): low-to-moderate
  - **Central coast faults**: Mw 4–5 occasional; rarely felt at urban level
  - HCMC and Hanoi: very low direct seismic source; soft-soil amplification possible for distant events
- Building seismic standards: TCVN 9386:2012 (Vietnamese version of Eurocode 8 for seismic design). Compliance variable in older / informal construction.

### Air quality (chất lượng không khí)

- **Hanoi**: among the worst PM2.5 in Southeast Asia in winter inversions (Dec–Feb). Daily averages est. 100–200 µg/m³ on bad days (no primary monitoring summary located in the 2026-10-29 pass — check live readings at Cục Môi trường / PAM Air). WHO Air Quality Guidelines (2021): PM2.5 24-hour average 15 µg/m³, annual average 5 µg/m³ (WHO, 2026-10-29 verified).
- **HCMC**: chronic but lower than Hanoi; est. 40–80 µg/m³ typical, peak 150+ during dry-season inversions (no primary monitoring summary located 2026-10-29).
- **Northern provinces** (Bắc Ninh, Hưng Yên, and the former Vĩnh Phúc and Hải Dương — merged into Phú Thọ and Hải Phòng city respectively in 2025): industrial + crop-burning + Hanoi spillover; among worst in Vietnam.
- **Da Nang, Nha Trang, Phu Quoc**: significantly cleaner due to coastal ventilation — est. PM2.5 20–50 µg/m³ (no primary monitoring summary located 2026-10-29).
- Source: Cục Môi trường (formerly VEA) `https://vea.mae.gov.vn/`; PAM Air `https://pamair.org/` is a private sensor network (PAM Air Solutions, founded 2018 — secondary).

### Climate-change projections (MONRE 2016 scenarios + National Adaptation Plan, Decision 1055/QĐ-TTg of 20 Jul 2020, updated by Decision 1422/QĐ-TTg of 19 Nov 2024)

- **Temperature** (vs 1986–2005): mid-21st century +1.3–1.7 °C under RCP4.5 and +1.8–2.3 °C under RCP8.5 (2.0–2.3 °C North, 1.8–1.9 °C South); late century +1.7–2.4 °C (RCP4.5) and +3.0–4.0 °C (RCP8.5). Source: MONRE Climate Change and Sea Level Rise Scenarios for Viet Nam (2016 edition, summary hosted by IMHEN `https://imh.ac.vn/files/doc/KichbanBDKH/CCS_SPM_2016.pdf`). The 2020 update was not readable on 2026-10-29 (DCC host unreachable) and an MAE refresh after the 2025 merger is pending.
- **Sea-level rise** (Vietnam coast, vs 1986–2005): by 2100 about **53 cm (32–76 cm) under RCP4.5** and **73 cm (49–103 cm) under RCP8.5** (MONRE 2016 — RCP scenarios, not SSP). Mekong Delta + HCMC + Hai Phong / Quang Ninh coast highly exposed; with 100 cm rise and no adaptation, 38.9 % of the Mekong Delta, 17.8 % of HCMC and 16.8 % of the Red River Delta would be at high risk of inundation (same source).
- **Extreme rainfall**: typhoon intensity expected to increase; central coast wettest events increasing.
- **Drought**: NE region dry-season severity increasing; Central Highlands coffee-region affected.
- **Saltwater intrusion**: Mekong Delta — already a major issue 2016, 2020, 2024 dry seasons; long-term threat to agricultural land and freshwater supply.

### Build-era hazards

| Era | Hazards |
|---|---|
| **Pre-1995** (early reform / Đổi Mới legacy construction) | Unreinforced masonry, asbestos in roofing/sheet, lead pipes, weak foundations, poor seismic detailing |

### Sewer / wastewater

**Vietnam has limited mains-sewer coverage compared to OECD baselines.**

- **HCMC**: as of Nov 2023 only **12.6 %** of urban wastewater (193,350 of about 1.5 million m³/day) was being treated, at Bình Hưng phase 1 (141,000 m³/day), Bình Hưng Hòa (30,000) and Tham Lương–Bến Cát (15,000) plus four decentralised stations; Bình Hưng phase 2 (469,000 m³/day) was then in trial operation and Nhiêu Lộc–Thị Nghè (480,000 m³/day) under construction; the city then expected 34 % for 2023 and 40.2 % by 2025 (HCMC Vice-Chairman, SGGP 2 Nov 2023 — secondary; whether those rates were reached not verified). The city's 2030 target is about 80 % collection and 40–45 % treated to standard (HCMC Department of Construction plan, Đại biểu Nhân dân 27 Jun 2026 — secondary). The earlier "40–55 % centrally collected and treated" figure is withdrawn, and Yên Xá is a Hanoi plant, not an HCMC one. Older / outer districts often on individual septic + soakaway.
- **Hanoi**: Yên Sở plus **Yên Xá** (270,000 m³/day, inaugurated 19 Aug 2025, serving 20 inner wards — Tiền Phong, secondary); the city aims to lift the treated share above 50 %. The earlier "inner districts ~50–60 % mains coverage" figure was not confirmed by a primary source in the 2026-10-29 pass — data not publicly available; outer areas much lower.
- **Provincial cities**: coverage drops sharply outside provincial seats. Most condo/apartment developments use **on-site treatment plants (Hệ thống xử lý nước thải tập trung của tòa nhà)** required by Vietnamese building code (QCVN 14:2025/BTNMT, which replaced QCVN 14:2008/BTNMT from 1 Sep 2025 under Circular 05/2025/TT-BTNMT; facilities already operating or permitted before then may keep QCVN 14:2008 until 31 Dec 2031 — luatvietnam summary, secondary) for residential buildings above a threshold.
- **Rural / villa areas**: septic tank + soakaway is the norm. Modern villas typically have **3-stage septic + biological filter + soakaway**; older / informal may have simple cesspit (bể tự hoại đơn giản).
- **Wastewater regulator**: MAE — Bộ Nông nghiệp và Môi trường (formerly MONRE) — through **Cục Môi trường / Department of Environment (formerly VEA)** `https://vea.mae.gov.vn/`.

### Verification path

1. Listing language scan:
   - "Hệ thống cấp thoát nước chung" / "Đấu nối hạ tầng đô thị" = mains drainage
   - "Hệ thống xử lý nước thải tại chỗ" = on-site building treatment (typical apartment)
   - "Bể tự hoại" / "Hầm tự hoại" = septic tank
   - "Bể phốt" = older cesspit
2. **HCMC**: contact the SAWACO member company serving the ward for water (SAWACO head office (028) 3829 1777; e.g. Bến Thành 1900 068868, Gia Định 1900 1024, Thủ Đức 1900 1012 — SGGP 27 Mar 2025, secondary); for sewer, the HCMC Department of Construction (Sở Xây dựng — drainage and wastewater treatment under Decree 150/2025/NĐ-CP, which replaced Decree 45/2025/NĐ-CP from 16 Jun 2025). District framing is obsolete since 1 Jul 2025.
3. **Hanoi**: contact HAWACOM (1900 4600) for water; Hanoi Sewerage and Drainage Co for sewer.
4. **Province**: contact provincial Công ty Cấp nước for water; provincial Sở Xây dựng for sewer.
5. **Da Nang / Nha Trang / Phu Quoc villas**: assume on-site treatment (apartment) or septic (villa); confirm with seller; verify condition.

## Caveats unique to VN

- **Foreign land ownership prohibition is constitutional** — Article 53 of the 2013 Constitution makes all land "owned by the people" with the State as administrator. Foreigners can never own LUR over land directly.
- **50-year leasehold cap with one renewal** — applies to all foreign-owned dwellings. Renewal is procedurally available but not guaranteed; budget the property as a 50-year asset with optional extension.
- **30 % per-apartment-building cap + 250 landed houses per area of ward-equivalent population** (Housing Law 2023 Art. 19(1); the ward-equivalence criterion is delegated to Decree 95/2024/ND-CP and is not simply the administrative ward — 2026-08-07 verified) — non-tradable; if the project has reached its cap, no further foreign sales until existing foreign owners sell back to Vietnamese.
- **Pink Book (Sổ Hồng) issuance delays** — 3–12 months post-payment is common; some developers have multi-year delays due to incomplete Land Use Right Certificate at the project level. **Never assume Pink Book is "imminent" — get a written timeline with penalty clauses.**
- **Off-plan / pre-sale risk** — historical failures: HCMC's Bavico, multiple Co Co Bay condotel projects, Empire Group developments. Buy from established developers (Vinhomes, Novaland, Sun Group, Masterise, Phu My Hung, CapitaLand, Keppel — though 2024 Novaland debt-restructuring was a sector wake-up call) and verify project-level Land Use Right Certificate exists BEFORE deposit.
- **Hanoi vs HCMC variation** — different price dynamics, different STR-enforcement intensity, different fire-code re-inspection completion. HCMC is more developer-driven; Hanoi has more state-affiliated developers. Don't apply HCMC heuristics to Hanoi or vice versa.
- **Sinking fund (Quỹ bảo trì 2 %)** scandals — multiple historical cases of developers absconding with the 2 % statutory maintenance fund. Verify joint control of the bank account.
- **Fire-code post-2018** — Carina Plaza fire (March 2018, HCMC, 13 deaths) prompted re-inspections of existing high-rises. Pre-2018 buildings may carry remediation costs to bring up to current code; verify Giấy chứng nhận PCCC.
- **HCMC subsidence + sea-level rise** — compound flood risk for low-lying districts (7, 4, Bình Chánh, Nhà Bè).
- **Mekong Delta long-term climate risk** — MONRE's 2016 scenarios put 38.9 % of the Mekong Delta at high risk of inundation with 100 cm sea-level rise and no adaptation (projected rise by 2100: 73 cm, range 49–103 cm, under RCP8.5); saltwater intrusion compounds it; foreign buyers should not consider Mekong Delta property for long-horizon holding.
- **Visa-linked property structures** (e.g., "buy a condo, get a visa") do NOT exist in Vietnam — there is no Golden Visa via real estate.
- **FX requirement** for foreign buyers is mandatory — funds must be remitted from abroad in foreign currency, converted to VND inside Vietnam, with bank documentation at notarization. Cash will not work.
- **All title documents are in Vietnamese** — sworn translation + Vietnamese-licensed lawyer are mandatory for foreign buyers; do not skip.
- **STR (Airbnb) is in a regulatory grey zone outside HCMC** — most premium HCMC and Hanoi apartment buildings explicitly prohibit STR via Ban Quản trị bylaws; tax declaration is required if you rent. **HCMC: rule replaced Apr 2026 (registration-conditional)** — Quyết định 19/2026/QĐ-UBND of HCMC People's Committee (issued 11 Apr 2026, effective **25 April 2026**, signed Bùi Xuân Cường) supersedes the earlier Quyết định 26/2025/QĐ-UBND (eff. 27 Feb 2025, which had barred STR in ordinary residential apartments — Art. 13 — allowing only mixed-use condotels). The new rule reframes day/week apartment letting as tourist accommodation (căn hộ du lịch), permitted only if the owner registers a tourist-lodging operation and complies with housing / real-estate / tax / residence / security / tourism / fire-safety rules (Art. 12); scope widened to residential-only + mixed-use buildings — Vietnamese press frames it as both a loosening and a tightening. Whether an ordinary residential unit now qualifies turns on approved function + registration — verify the full decision text per building. The decision is **city-wide, not ward discretion**, and enforcement sits with the **provincial and ward (phường) People's Committees**: Art. 12(3) requires tourist-accommodation registration, Art. 12(4)(a) adds tax registration/declaration plus residence, security, e-commerce, sanitation and fire-safety duties, Art. 12(4)(b) requires tenant temporary-residence registration, and **Art. 17 assigns state housing management to the `cấp xã` People's Committee** — the text never mentions `cấp huyện` / `quận`, because district People's Committees ceased operating nationwide on 1 Jul 2025 (Law No. 72/2025/QH15, Art. 51(3)). Hanoi / Da Nang: no city-level STR decision equivalent to HCMC 19/2026 was identified at the 2026-07-02 check — re-verify at the Hanoi / Da Nang People's Committee portals. Verify full text at `https://luatvietnam.vn/` (2026-07-02 verified; enforcement-tier framing 2026-08-07 verified).
- **Hague Apostille Convention in force for Vietnam since 11 September 2026** (Công ước Apostille; HCCH status table row Viet Nam / 31-XII-2025 / A\*\* / 11-IX-2026 (the double asterisk is HCCH's objection marker), verified 2026-09-17) — POA + foreign-issued marriage / birth / corporate documents from Apostille-member jurisdictions now need a single apostille stamp instead of the 3-step consular chain (notary in origin country → foreign ministry → Vietnamese embassy). Domestic implementation: **Decree 293/2026/NĐ-CP** (23 Jul 2026, effective 11 Sep 2026) + **Circular 12/2026/TT-BNG** (11 Sep 2026); Vietnamese apostilles are issued by the MFA Consular Department (Hanoi) and the HCMC Department for External Relations (HCCH authority record, updated 23 Jul 2026), with delegation to provincial People's Committees scheduled for 2027-2030 by Circular 12/2026/TT-BNG Art. 6(1), only after the two central issuers run stably and each by its own ministerial legal instrument (Art. 6(3)); Decree 293 Art. 9(1) charges the existing consular-legalisation fee — no separate apostille fee; the MFA notice of 11 Sep 2026 sets 30,000 per copy under Circular 157/2016/TT-BTC (VND; the notice prints no currency unit), processing 1 / 2 / 4 working days for 1-4 / 5-9 / 10+ documents (2026-10-21 verified). **Exception — Germany (20 May 2026), Austria (19 Jun 2026) and Czechia (2 Jul 2026) each objected to Vietnam's accession under Art. 12(2), so the Convention does NOT enter into force between Vietnam and those three states**: documents moving between VN and DE / AT / CZ still require the full 3-step consular-legalisation chain (Decree 293 Art. 5(4) codifies the pairwise exclusion) — the three are the only objections on the HCCH notifications page and none is withdrawn as at 17 Sep 2026; verify the current objector list before relying on apostille. Source: [HCCH status table (cid=41)](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41) + [HCCH authority record aid=1240](https://www.hcch.net/en/states/authorities/details3/?aid=1240) + Decree 293/2026/NĐ-CP (2026-09-17 verified).
- **Capital controls on repatriation** — original capital can be repatriated with bank documentation; profit/gain may require explicit SBV approval. Plan exit liquidity carefully.
- **Inheritance window 12 months** — foreign heirs of a deceased foreign owner have 12 months to sell or transfer to a qualifying buyer. Plan estate succession explicitly.

## Quirks to know

- **Sổ Đỏ vs Sổ Hồng vs Sổ Hồng cũ**: three historical title-document colors are widely referenced —
  - **Sổ Đỏ (Red Book, đỏ)** = pre-2009 land-use right only certificate (rural land typically). Issued by MAE (formerly MONRE) predecessor.
  - **Sổ Hồng cũ (Old Pink Book)** = pre-2009 building ownership only certificate (urban housing). Issued by Ministry of Construction predecessor.
  - **Sổ Hồng (current Pink Book, hồng)** = post-2009 unified Certificate of Land Use Rights, Home Ownership and Other Assets Attached to Land. Decree 88/2009/ND-CP unified the previous two; current legal basis Decree 101/2024/ND-CP under 2024 Land Law.
  - **Foreign buyers receive only the current (post-2009) Pink Book** with the 50-year-term notation. If a seller offers an old Red Book or old Pink Book, insist on conversion to the unified current format BEFORE transfer.
- **Quota tracker for the 250-landed-house cap** (counted across an area of ward-equivalent population under Housing Law 2023 Art. 19(1), the criterion being delegated to Decree 95/2024/ND-CP rather than to the administrative ward boundary): there is no public real-time tracker; verification requires written confirmation from the project developer + provincial Sở Xây dựng. If pursuing a landed-house purchase, confirm in writing BEFORE deposit.
- **Apartment block 30 % cap**: applies per single building, not per project. A multi-building project may have all blocks separately capped.
- **Foreign-eligible projects list**: provincial Sở Xây dựng publishes the list periodically. Freshness varies; HCMC Sở Xây dựng list is most-frequently updated.
- **Communist title chain pre-1990**: properties with title chain going back to before Đổi Mới (1986) may have ambiguous ownership history due to land collectivization in the 1950s–1970s and gradual restitution after 1993 Land Law. **For older urban properties, verify the post-1993 chain of title carefully** — pre-1993 ownership claims may be unenforceable.
- **Land-price tables (from 1 Jan 2026)** — the first tables under the 2024 Land Law apply from 1 Jan 2026 and, under NA Resolution 254/2025/QH15 Art. 7(3), are amended by the provincial People's Council when necessary (not a fixed annual rebuild); the non-agricultural land-use-tax base stays stabilised over a 5-year cycle (Law 48/2010/QH12 Art. 6(3) as amended by Land Law Art. 249). Verify the current table with the provincial land authority (Sở Nông nghiệp và Môi trường, ex-Sở TN&MT).
- **Mortgage to foreign-owned banks — claim WITHDRAWN 2026-08-07** — the earlier statement that the 2024 Land Law explicitly allows foreign-owned credit institutions to act as mortgagees, and that this was domestic-only pre-2024, is not verified against the primary text and is withdrawn. Land Law Art. 41 keeps the long-standing formula "credit institutions licensed to operate in Vietnam" and addresses foreign-invested organisations rather than individual foreign home buyers. Foreign-buyer mortgage availability is **est.** — confirm with the specific lender.
- **Notarization is mandatory** — all property sale contracts must be notarized at a public notary office (Phòng Công chứng) under the Notary Law 2014. Private notaries (Văn phòng Công chứng) also exist and are equally valid.
- **VNeID / electronic ID** — the Sổ Hộ khẩu (paper household registration book) was officially abolished as a standalone document on 31 Dec 2022; replaced by the VNeID national digital ID app + chip-CCCD card. For Vietnamese counterparties, accept VNeID as identity proof.
- **HCMC vs TP.HCM vs Sài Gòn naming**: official name is Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh (TP.HCM); "Sài Gòn" colloquially refers to the city or specifically District 1. Address notations vary; standardize on TP.HCM in legal documents.
- **Đường vs Phố vs Hẻm vs Ngõ**: street types matter for valuation. **Đường** = formal street; **Phố** = formal street (Hanoi term); **Hẻm** = alley (HCMC term); **Ngõ** = alley (Hanoi term). Hẻm/Ngõ properties have different access profile (motorbike-only past a depth, no fire-truck access for deep hẻm).
- **District renaming / merger — and then district abolition** — HCMC's Districts 2, 9, and Thủ Đức were merged into "Thủ Đức City" in 2021. Hanoi has had similar restructuring. The district tier itself was then **abolished**: `cấp huyện` / `quận` People's Committees and their agencies ceased operating nationwide on **1 Jul 2025** (Law on Organization of Local Government No. 72/2025/QH15, Art. 51(3)), and administrative units are now province (`cấp tỉnh`) → ward / commune (`cấp xã`) only (Art. 1(1)). ALL district names in older addresses are therefore legacy — verify the current ward-level administrative unit, not just the boundary (2026-08-07 verified).
- **Sea / coastline buffer for foreigners** — military / national-defense exclusion zones along the coast; verify the parcel is NOT within a defense buffer at provincial Sở Xây dựng.
