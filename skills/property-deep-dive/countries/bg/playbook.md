## Country profile

Bulgaria (~6.4M pop: 6,423,207 on 31 Dec 2025, NSI final data released 29 Apr 2026) joined EU 2007 and **completed Schengen integration 1 Jan 2025** alongside Romania, when checks on persons at the internal land borders were lifted (European Commission DG HOME, 3 Jan 2025). **Bulgaria entered the eurozone 1 Jan 2026** — major event of the 2026 due-diligence cycle (conversion rate BGN 1.95583/EUR, the rate at which the currency board introduced on 1 Jul 1997 has pegged the lev to the euro since 1 Jan 1999; ECB Convergence Report, Jun 2025, and ECB, 1 Jan 2026). Cadastre and Land Register are still **separately maintained** (AGCC vs Registry Agency). Black Sea coast is the country-defining foreign-buyer market — historically Russian-heavy: the Property Register lists 10,954 properties linked to Russian citizens, 5,106 of them in Nessebar municipality, which includes Sunny Beach (2026 data as of 23 Apr 2026, Registry Agency via Sega, 28 May 2026 — secondary news citing the register). Owner citizenship is recorded only when the notarial deed states it, so the count is a floor. 8,723 of the 14,229 register entries linked to Russian citizens were made after 24 Feb 2022 (same source); no mass sell-off. **Toplofikatsiya Sofia in active financial crisis**: its debts to Bulgargaz and Bulgarian Energy Holding exceed EUR 1.28 billion, and the Energy Minister says Sofia's district heating for the 2026/27 winter is secured "insofar as it depends on the Energy Ministry" (2026 data, BTA, 11 Sep and 23 Oct 2026 — secondary news citing the minister). Sofia panel-block buyers carry district-heating supply and price risk (see `--mains`). **2024 worst wildfire season in over a decade** (2024 data, source JRC EUR 40516: 595 forest fires per the Executive Forest Agency; 45,435 ha EFFIS-mapped) — rates/figures may have changed since; rural Pirin/Rhodope cheap properties carry elevated fire risk.

## Section: `--tax`

### Currency note (2026)

BGN no longer Bulgaria's currency. **EUR is legal tender from 1 Jan 2026** (Council Decision (EU) 2025/1407 of 8 July 2025). Conversion rate **1.95583** locked. Lev cash circulated alongside the euro only 1–31 Jan 2026. Mandatory dual EUR/BGN price display ran 8 Aug 2025 – 8 Aug 2026 and has ended: since 9 Aug 2026 sale and payable prices are in EUR, and a BGN equivalent may be shown only voluntarily, for information. Lev amounts in existing legal instruments, including pre-2026 deeds and contracts, are deemed euro amounts at 1.95583. Commercial banks exchange lev cash until 31 Dec 2026; the BNB exchanges it free, with no amount limit and no deadline (2026 data, source BNB Q&A + evroto.bg 5 Aug 2026). All amounts below in EUR.

### Transfer / acquisition costs (individual buyer)

- **Local acquisition tax (данък при придобиване)**: Set by each of 265 municipalities **between 0.1% and 3.0%** (Local Taxes and Fees Act (ЗМДТ) Art. 47(2); 265 municipalities per NSI at 31 Dec 2025). Applied to the agreed price, or to the **tax assessment value** where the price is lower (ЗМДТ Art. 46(2)(1)) (2026 data, source ЗМДТ consolidated text, lex.bg)
  - **Sofia**: 3.0% (Sofia ordinance Art. 35(2), unchanged since 1 Jan 2020; 2026 data, source Ciela Norma consolidated text). **Plovdiv, Varna, Burgas**: their ordinance texts also read 3.0% (municipal ordinance texts, version dates not shown — confirm the current ordinance)
  - **Elsewhere**: 2026 district-level figures run from 2.58% (Lovech) to 3.00% (Sofia city, Yambol) (2026 data, source IME Regional Profiles — secondary aggregator, aggregation method not stated); a single municipality can sit lower — check its ordinance
- **Notary fee**: progressive (capped) per the regulated Tariff of Notary Fees (item 8). Marginal bands fall from 1.5% to 0.1%; the effective rate is ~0.74% at EUR 50,000, ~0.47% at EUR 100,000 and ~0.21% at EUR 500,000 (computed from the tariff, e.g. EUR 373.50 + 0.2% above EUR 51,129.19 = EUR 471.24 on EUR 100,000), capped at **EUR 3,067.75** (BGN 6,000 at 1.95583) (2026 data, source Notary Chamber tariff + pravatami.bg euro scale, 2 Oct 2026). **20% VAT is added** when the notary is VAT-registered — the tariff amounts exclude VAT (not on property price itself for resales)
- **Registration fee at Registry Agency**: 0.1% of price, minimum EUR 5.11 (BGN 10) (Registry Agency fee tariff Art. 2(1); 2026 data, source Registry Agency)

**Total typical acquisition costs**: ~**3.4–4.0%** of price in a 3% municipality for EUR 50,000–400,000, before agent and lawyer fees (computed: Sofia, EUR 100,000 = EUR 3,000 tax + EUR 471.24 notary + EUR 94.25 VAT + EUR 100 registration = EUR 3,665.49, 3.67%).

### Annual immovable property tax

- Municipal rate: **0.01% to 0.45%** (0.1–4.5‰) of **tax-assessed value**, per ЗМДТ Art. 22 (2026 data, source ЗМДТ consolidated text, lex.bg). The assessment is NOT market value — est. ~30–60% of market, not source-stamped; the Annex 2 base value for buildings and urban land has not changed since 1997 (2026 draft motives, accountingnews.bg summary). **Sofia: 1.875‰ (0.1875%)** (Sofia ordinance Art. 15, unchanged since 1 Jan 2020; 2026 data, source Ciela Norma consolidated text, last amended 18 Dec 2025)
- Levied on land + buildings within development limits (urban + nucleated rural settlements)
- Paid in 2 instalments (30 June and 31 October). **5% prompt-pay discount** if full year paid by 30 April (ЗМДТ Art. 28(1)-(2); 2026 data, source ЗМДТ consolidated text)
- **Main residence: 50% relief** (ЗМДТ Art. 25(1)), claimed by tax declaration (Art. 27)
- **PROPOSED, not law — 2027 assessment rise**: a Ministry of Finance draft amendment to ЗМДТ (public consultation No. 12735-K, 23 Sep – 23 Oct 2026) would raise the Annex 2 base values to 1.3x (2027), 1.65x (2028) and 2x (2029 onward) the current ones, so at unchanged municipal rates the 2027 property tax rises ~30% (computed: 1.3 x assessment x same rate). For 2027 the tax would be notified by 30 Apr and the 5% discount would apply to full payment by 31 May 2027. The new assessments would also feed acquisition tax and inheritance tax, but not the household waste fee. The tripartite council backed the package on 23 Oct 2026; Council of Ministers approval and a National Assembly vote are still needed (2026 data, source accountingnews.bg summary of the draft and motives + Mediapool 23 Oct 2026 — secondary)

**Household waste fee (такса за битови отпадъци, 'taksa smet')**: separate municipal fee, not the tourist tax. Since 1 Jan 2026 ЗМДТ Art. 67 sets it on the polluter-pays principle, with waste quantity as the leading base unless the council records objective reasons for another base; for 2026 only, a council could instead keep its old method and rate (§ 16(3)-(5) of the transitional provisions to ДВ бр. 88/2017, added by ДВ бр. 113/2025) (2026 data, source ЗМДТ consolidated text, lex.bg). Only 8 municipalities adopted the new method for 2026 (2026 data, source BNR Radio Shumen, 8 Jun 2026 — secondary), so most 2026 fees still follow the pre-2026 base, which can be the property's tax assessment. Sofia bills it in 4 instalments (30 Apr, 30 Jun, 30 Sep, 30 Nov) (2026 data, source sofia.bg). Wisdom, not data: it is often comparable to or larger than the property tax itself, especially in resort areas — compare both lines on the municipal tax notice.

### Capital Gains Tax (individuals)

- **Flat 10%** on the gain after a 10% statutory cost deduction (PIT Act (ЗДДФЛ) Art. 33(1), Art. 48(1) → effective 9% of the gain, computed)
- **Exemptions** (BG residents, and EU/EEA tax residents via ЗДДФЛ Art. 37(7)-(8) with a foreign tax-residence certificate):
  - **One residential property/year** exempt **if more than 3 years passed between acquisition and sale** (Art. 13(1)(1)(a))
  - **Up to 2 further properties of any type, plus any number of agricultural and forest properties**, exempt **if more than 5 years passed** (Art. 13(1)(1)(b))
- Non-EU/EEA tax residents: 10% final tax on the gain less 10% costs (Art. 37(1)(10), (3)), no holding-period exemption (2026 data, source ЗДДФЛ consolidated text, lex.bg)

### Rental income tax

- **Residents: 10% flat** on rental income after a 10% statutory cost deduction (ЗДДФЛ Art. 31(1), Art. 48(1) → effective 9% on gross, computed)
- **Non-residents: 10% final tax on the gross rent**, no deduction (ЗДДФЛ Art. 37(1)(4), (2), Art. 46(1)); EU/EEA tax residents may elect to have it recalculated as for a resident in the annual return (Art. 37a) (2026 data, source ЗДДФЛ consolidated text, lex.bg)
- Health/social contributions can apply to residents
- **Short-term letting can fall outside this 10% rate**: places of up to 20 rooms, registered under the Tourism Act or categorised one or two stars, are a patent activity, with an annual patent tax of **BGN 25–250 per room** by location, if prior-year turnover is at most BGN 100,000 and the owner is not VAT-registered, a registration only for ЗДДС art. 97a services or intra-EU acquisitions (arts. 99, 100(2)) not counting (ЗМДТ Art. 61з(1), Annex 4 item 1; 2026 data, source lex.bg consolidated text, which still states these amounts in leva) → EUR 12.78–127.82 per room (computed at the fixed 1.95583: 25 / 1.95583, 250 / 1.95583). Sole-trader business income is taxed at 15% (ЗДДФЛ Art. 48(2); 2026 data, source Ciela consolidated text). ### VAT new-build

- **Standard 20%** (VAT Act (ЗДДС) Art. 66(1)) applies to supplies of new buildings by a VAT-registered seller: buildings at rough-construction (груб строеж) stage, or less than 60 months after the use permit (разрешение за ползване) or commissioning certificate (ЗДДС Additional Provisions § 1 item 5) — the clock runs from that permit, not from "Akt 16". Land adjoining a new building and regulated plots (УПИ) are taxable too (Art. 45(5))
- Resales of "old" buildings (not new under that definition) and their adjoining land: VAT-exempt (Art. 45(3)); the seller may opt to tax them (Art. 45(7))
- **No reduced rate for housing**: the 9% rate (Art. 66a) covers hotel and holiday accommodation, books and periodicals, and baby food and hygiene items only (2026 data, source ЗДДС consolidated text, lex.bg)
- VAT registration trigger: annual turnover in Bulgaria in the **calendar year** above **EUR 51,130** (statutory euro figure, formerly BGN 100,000); apply within 7 days of exceeding it (ЗДДС Art. 96(1), (6) and Art. 168в, as amended by ДВ бр. 115/2025 from 1 Jan 2026 — the 12-rolling-month test is gone). EU-established sellers outside the EU SME scheme and non-EU-established sellers register before their first taxable supply in Bulgaria, with no threshold (Art. 96(3)-(4)) (2026 data, source ЗДДС consolidated text, lex.bg + NRA). History: BGN 166,000 applied only 1 Jan – 31 Mar 2025, reverted to BGN 100,000 from 1 Apr 2025 (2025 data, source VATupdate / Taxually / BCCI)
- **PROPOSED, not law — 2027 VAT changes**: a Ministry of Finance draft amendment to ЗДДС (public consultation No. 12733-K, 23 Sep – 23 Oct 2026) would raise the threshold to EUR 75,000 from 1 Jan 2027; after the 23 Oct 2026 tripartite council, Deputy Finance Minister Petkova announced a phased rise instead — EUR 60,000 (2027), EUR 70,000 (2028), EUR 75,000 (2029). New art. 97г would require any taxable person making taxable supplies of new buildings (with adjoining land), or transferring ownership of or a building right over a regulated plot, to register at least 7 days before VAT on the supply becomes chargeable, whatever its turnover; natural persons only when acting in an independent economic activity. Council of Ministers approval and a National Assembly vote are still needed; EUR 51,130 stays the law until promulgation in Държавен вестник (2026 data, source accountingnews.bg 24 Sep 2026 + Mediapool 23 Oct 2026 + sdk2008.bg 26 Oct 2026 — secondary)

### STR / Airbnb

- **Tourism Act**: a typical Airbnb flat is a **class "В" place of accommodation** (guest rooms and guest apartments). It is registered, not categorized: the municipal mayor enters it in the National Tourist Register and issues a registration certificate (art. 123(1) item 3, art. 128(2)-(4))
- **Class "А"**: hotels, motels, apartment tourist complexes, holiday villages, tourist villages, villas, categorized by stars
- **Class "Б"**: family hotels, hostels, pensions, rest stations, guest houses, bungalows, campsites, categorized by stars (art. 123(1)-(3); consolidated text to ДВ бр. 41/2024, later amendments not checked)
- **EU Reg 2024/1028** applies from 20 May 2026: the registration number must appear in every platform listing, and Bulgaria applies it through the existing Tourism Act registration (dpc.bg, 2 Jun 2026 — law-firm analysis, secondary). Bulgarian implementing amendment: data not publicly available — verify with the Ministry of Tourism
- **ESTI (Единна система за туристическа информация)**: the host's guest register must be filled in **immediately on check-in** (art. 116(1)). For citizens of BG, the EU/EEA, Switzerland and states applying the OECD Codes of Liberalisation, ESTI receives full names, personal identifier, identity-document type, number and issue date, and check-in and departure dates; for other nationals, guest numbers, identifying data under Foreigners Act art. 28(4) and the same dates (art. 116(5), item 1 as amended by ДВ бр. 52/2025, in force 1 Oct 2025; lex.bg consolidated text to ДВ бр. 87/2026). Municipalities compute the monthly tourist tax from ESTI data (ЗМДТ art. 61с(2))
- **Tourist tax (туристически данък)**: the municipal council sets it at **BGN 0.20–3.00 per night**, by settlement and by the category or registration of the accommodation (ЗМДТ art. 61с(1); lex.bg consolidated text to ДВ бр. 55/2026 still states the band in leva) → EUR 0.10–1.53 per night (computed at the fixed 1.95583: 0.20 / 1.95583, 3.00 / 1.95583)
- **Sofia / Sunny Beach / Varna**: same regime nationwide; resort municipalities (Nesebar, Sozopol, Pomorie, Varna, Burgas) enforce rigorously
- **VAT trigger**: for an operator established or habitually resident in Bulgaria, annual turnover in Bulgaria for the **calendar year** above **EUR 51,130** → mandatory VAT registration, applied for within 7 days of crossing it (ЗДДС art. 96(1) and (6)(1), art. 168в, in force 1 Jan 2026; lex.bg consolidated text to ДВ бр. 69/2026). An owner established outside the EU, or elsewhere in the EU without the EU small-enterprise scheme, must register before the first taxable supply in Bulgaria, whatever the turnover (art. 96(3)-(4)). Accommodation, including holiday accommodation, is then taxed at the **9% reduced rate**, not 20% (ЗДДС art. 66а item 1). Steep cliff for full-time mid-scale operators. Before 2026 the test was BGN 100,000 over 12 rolling months, back from BGN 166,000 on 1 Apr 2025 (BCCI Infobusiness relaying the NRA, 4 Apr 2025). **PROPOSED, not law**: a Ministry of Finance VAT Act draft (consultation 12733-K, 23 Sep–23 Oct 2026) would raise the threshold to EUR 75,000 from 1 Jan 2027 (secondary — lookuptax.com quoting the draft)
- **No Sofia-specific cap** on STR (unlike Barcelona, Amsterdam, Paris)

## Section: `--risks`

### Seismic

- **Main zones**: Sofia, Kresna (SW), Maritsa (Plovdiv area), Gorna Oryahovitsa, Shabla (Black Sea coast)
- Plovdiv has highest provincial seismic hazard ranking (ranking not source-stamped here — verify with NIGGG-BAS); a large share of Plovdiv residential buildings sit in the highest PEI category (specific percentage not publicly verified — confirm with NIGGG-BAS)
- Sofia: historical reference earthquake **18/30 September 1858 (Julian/Gregorian), M~6.5, epicentral intensity IX-X MSK-64** (Vitosha north-margin fault) (intensity per NIGGG-BAS, EGU2013-9690; 18 September date per the 1858 press quoted in Rangelov et al., Annual of the University of Mining and Geology vol. 55, 2012). Probabilistic 475-year (10% in 50 years, the Eurocode 8 metric) PGA for Sofia **156 cm/s² (≈0.16 g)** (2013 data, source Burton & Bayliss, Natural Hazards 68:1155-1201 — a peer-reviewed estimate, not the Eurocode 8 national-annex design value — rates/figures may have changed since). The design acceleration for a given plot comes from the national-annex zoning map — verify with a structural engineer / NIGGG-BAS
- **NIGGG-BAS (National Institute of Geophysics, Geodesy & Geography)**: https://ndc.niggg.bas.bg/en/ — live seismic data, catalogue
- EFEHR European Seismic Hazard Map integrates Bulgaria

### Flood

- **Coastal**: Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas, Pomorie) — surge + storm + sea-level rise. Dobruja (NE) flat plains: river flood (Danube tributaries)
- **River**: Maritsa, Iskar, Struma basins. Reference events: **17-20 Jun 2014 Varna-Asparuhovo flash flood** (13 dead in Asparuhovo; Varna, Dobrich, Shumen and Veliko Tarnovo provinces hit) and **end-Jul 2014 Mizia** (Skat river overflow, 2 dead, 100+ houses destroyed or severely damaged) (2014 data, sources Wikipedia '2014 Bulgarian floods' + Global Voices 7 Aug 2014 — secondary) — rates/figures may have changed since; 2017 floods (not source-stamped here — verify with NIMH). Southern Black Sea coast: **5 Sep 2023 Tsarevo flash flood**, 4 dead (2023 data, source BNT 6 Sep 2023) — rates/figures may have changed since
- INSPIRE Bulgaria flood layers via https://inspire.egov.bg/en

### Forest fire (CRITICAL — 2024 worst season in over a decade)

- **2024: 595 forest fires burned 17,116 ha of forest** — 136% of the 2014-23 average fire count and 371% of the average burnt area; biggest single fire 4,191 ha; RFD Sliven (18 fires, 4,692 ha) and RFD Kardzhali (74 fires, 4,478 ha) held more than 53% of the burnt forest (2024 data, source Executive Forest Agency via JRC 'Forest Fires in Europe, Middle East and North Africa 2024', EUR 40516, published 5 Dec 2025) — rates/figures may have changed since. EFFIS satellite mapping, which also counts farmland and other land, gives **45,435 ha from 256 fires** — Bulgaria's worst season in over a decade, two fires of ~8,000 ha each, 48% of the mapped area on agricultural land (same report). Bulgaria is among the five EU countries most affected in 2024, with Greece, Italy, Portugal and Spain (BTA 5 Dec 2025, citing the JRC report)
- Hot zones: **Rhodopes (south), Strandzha, Sakar, Pirin** (rural/mountain), Black Sea hinterland (Burgas province)
- 2024 causes (EFA): carelessness 66%, arson 3%, natural 6%, unknown 25% — computed: human causes in 409 of the 447 fires with a known cause ≈ 91.5% (2024 data, source EFA via JRC EUR 40516) — rates/figures may have changed since. Climate-prepared landscape (heatwave, drought) is the multiplier
- **Implication**: rural Pirin/Rhodope cheap properties sit in the hot zones above: 12 settlements in the Smolyan, Kardzhali and Blagoevgrad regions, which reach beyond the two ranges, show a median asking price under EUR 30k (computed: 5 + 1 + 6 = 12; alo.bg house and villa listing medians, 4 Nov 2026 — listing platform, few listings per village). Home cover is sold by nearly all general insurers, with fire and natural disasters among its most common risks (ies.bg insurance-law guide, published Jun 2014, last edited Mar 2025 — secondary) — rates/figures may have changed since; whether an insurer accepts a given rural house, and at what premium: data not publicly available — verify at https://www.fsc.bg/ and with the insurer

### Mandatory diagnostics

- **Energy Performance Certificate** (Сертификат за енергийни характеристики): on sale of a building the seller hands over its certificate; on sale of a unit in a building, a certified copy of the **building's** certificate (Energy Efficiency Act Art. 41(1)); for a new building, a copy of the new-building certificate (Art. 34(2)). Where a certificate exists, every sale or rental ad must state its primary-energy figure in kWh/m² and the buyer must be shown the certificate before the contract (Art. 34(4), 41(3)). Issued by persons entered in the SEDA (АУЕР) register (Art. 43-44). Valid up to 10 years (Art. 47(1)) (2025 data, source Energy Efficiency Act consolidated to ДВ бр. 97/14.11.2025, seea.government.bg)
- **Akt 16 / Razreshenie za polzvane**: Akt 16 is form 16 under Ordinance No. 3 of 31.07.2003 on acts and protocols during construction. The building is legally put into use by a **use permit (разрешение за ползване) from DNSK** (National Construction Control Directorate) for category I-III works, or a **certificate of commissioning (удостоверение за въвеждане в експлоатация)** from the authority that issued the building permit for category IV-V works; the investor's application must include the technical passport and the new-building energy certificate (ZUT Art. 177(1)-(3), lex.bg consolidated text). **Buying new-build before Akt 16 and the use permit is high-risk** (developer can fail finalization). Verify the Akt 16 and use-permit numbers; DNSK use permits are published in the Unified Public Register under ZUT Art. 5a
- **No mandatory radon test, no mandatory seismic-risk class certificate** at sale (unlike Romania's de-facto Bucharest scrutiny). Asbestos / lead also not in mandatory diagnostic stack
- **Condominium book (книга на етажната собственост)**: kept on paper or electronically in every building or entrance under the condominium regime (Condominium Management Act ZUES Art. 7(1)) by the management board (Art. 23(1)6); records each unit's use and area, its ideal shares of the common parts, owners, occupants and residents staying more than 30 days (Art. 7(2)) (2024 data, source ZUES as amended to ДВ бр. 81/2024, trudipravo.bg — secondary) — rates/figures may have changed since. Since 31 Dec 2024 the management board must be entered in the Unified Centralised Condominium Register through the municipality within one month of its election, stating the monthly 'Repair and renovation' fund contribution (ZUES Art. 47е) (2026 data, source Sofia Municipality — Nadezhda district notice). Ask for both; verify the book is up to date

### Cadastre / Land Registry — TWO institutions

- **AGCC (Агенция по геодезия, картография и кадастър)**:

## Foreign-buyer rules

- **Buildings/apartments**: **All foreigners (EU and non-EU)** can buy buildings, apartments, condominium units freely as individuals (Property Act Art. 29(4): buildings and limited real rights, unless a law provides otherwise)
- **Land**:
  - **EU/EEA**: can buy land directly (Property Act Art. 29(2)-(3)). The accession transitional periods are over: 5 years for land for secondary residences by non-resident EU/EEA buyers (to 1 Jan 2012) and 7 years for agricultural land, forests and forestry land (to 1 Jan 2014) (Accession Treaty Annex VI, free movement of capital; source European Commission text). The agricultural-land rule requiring more than 5 years' residence or establishment in Bulgaria (ЗСПЗЗ Art. 3c, ДВ бр. 38/2014) is still in the statute, but CJEU C-562/22 (18 Jan 2024) held that Art. 63 TFEU precludes it (source EUR-Lex mirror + Global Lex — verify with the notary how it is applied); agricultural-land buyers also file a source-of-funds declaration with the notary (Art. 3c(3))
  - **Non-EU/EEA**: **cannot buy land directly** — including regulated urban plots. The Constitution (Art. 22(1), as amended by ДВ бр. 18/2005, in force 1 Jan 2007) and Property Act Art. 29(1) allow them land only under a ratified international treaty (adopted by a two-thirds majority, Art. 22(2)) or by inheritance by law; a non-qualifying heir must transfer inherited agricultural land within 3 years (ЗСПЗЗ Art. 3b). Standard workaround: register a Bulgarian **EOOD** (sole-owner LLC) or **OOD**; the company can own non-agricultural land. Setup est. ~EUR 1,500–2,000 (lawyer quotes vary, not source-stamped); minimum capital **EUR 1** (Commercial Act Art. 117(1), in force 1 Jan 2026; Ministry of Justice). 100% non-EU shareholder allowed for **buildings/regulated land**, **NOT for agricultural land**: companies with non-EU/EEA partners or shareholders cannot acquire or hold it (ЗСПЗЗ Art. 3(7)(2)); for forest land, verify under the Forest Act (Закон за горите) with the notary (2026 data, source lex.bg consolidated texts)
- **Russian buyers post-2022**:
  - Pre-2022: large Russian ownership on the Black Sea coast, but no complete count exists — the Property Register has recorded owner nationality only since 10 Dec 2018; by 15 Aug 2023 it listed 7,472 properties with Russian-citizen owners, 5,298 of them in Burgas region and 997 in Varna region (2023 data, Registry Agency via the Justice Minister's answer to a parliamentary question, reported by Burgas24 — figures may have changed since)
  - Post-Feb 2022: BG banks closed/refused new accounts for Russian nationals (not source-stamped); sales by Russians ~EUR 22M in 2022 (listing-agency figure citing BNB data — cross-check with BNB; 2022 data — figures may have changed since) — significant but no mass sell-off
  - Implication: complexes with high Russian-owner concentration may have under-maintained common areas, unpaid maintenance dues, distressed sellers — opportunity AND risk
  - Buyers should ask explicitly whether seller is on EU sanctions list; notaries are AML-obligated entities
- **Source-of-funds checks**: notaries are obliged entities under the Measures Against Money Laundering Act (ЗМИП). Under the Notary Chamber's uniform AML rules they run customer due diligence before any deal of EUR 15,000 or more (EUR 5,000 or more if paid in cash) and take a written source-of-funds declaration for every such deal with a money transfer (Notary Chamber rules Art. 5(1), Art. 32; version date not shown). A separate tier for transactions >EUR 500,000 (since 2025) is not source-stamped — verify with notary / DANS

## Country-specific quirks

- **"Bearer share" exclusion for agricultural land**: Joint-stock companies (AD) with bearer shares cannot acquire or hold farmland (ЗСПЗЗ Art. 3(7)(3)); neither can companies with partners or shareholders registered, directly or indirectly, in preferential tax jurisdictions (Art. 3(7)(1)). Rules out many holding structures
- **Soviet-era panel apartments (panelki)**: Built 1960s-1980s, prefabricated concrete. Quality varies; many are cold, poorly insulated; renovation grants from EU funds (NRRP) available. Energy class often F-G. **CORRECTION**: Bulgarian panelki are **condominium-owned (NOT cooperatives)** — Condominium Management Act (ЗУЕС, ДВ бр. 6/2009). Each block is managed by a general meeting of owners and/or an owners' association (ЗУЕС Art. 9). The cooperative form (TKZS) was an agricultural-collectivization legacy
- **Black Sea closed complexes (apart-hotels)**: hundreds of thousands of holiday units in Sunny Beach, Sveti Vlas, Pomorie. Typically: (a) historically Russian-owned; (b) over-supplied; (c) burdened with annual maintenance fees EUR 300-1,500+; (d) prices stagnant or falling 2014-2024 in real terms. Re-sale liquidity low outside summer
- **Pirin / Rhodope rural cheap properties**: Genuinely cheap (EUR 10–50k whole houses) but: limited services, fire risk (see §risks), depopulation, often no good road access in winter, traditional houses may have no formal building permit (zaverena regulatsia missing), and ownership chains can be confused (post-restitution sub-divisions among heirs)
- **Dobruja flood plain (NE)**: flat agricultural region; flood risk on Danube tributaries; agricultural land — non-EU/EEA buyers blocked from direct ownership except by treaty or inheritance by law, and so are companies with non-EU/EEA partners or shareholders (ЗСПЗЗ Art. 3(4), 3(7)(2))
- **Land vs building on plot**: Legal in BG for the building to be owned by one party while the underlying land is owned by another (`pravo na stroezh` / superficies, Property Act Art. 63). A non-EU/EEA individual can buy the building (Art. 29(4)) but the land needs a separate BG-company structure, a treaty basis or inheritance by law (Art. 29(1))
- **AML cash limit**: payments of **BGN 10,000 (EUR 5,112.92 at the fixed rate) or more** — including linked payments on the same ground — must go by bank transfer, not cash (Limitation of Cash Payments Act Art. 3(1); nominal unchanged since 1 Jan 2016) (2026 data, source Ciela Norma consolidated text + Fiscal Council via BTA, 24 Mar 2026). A raise to EUR 10,000 to match the EU-wide cap of Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 from 2027 is a Fiscal Council proposal, not law. Property deals settle by bank transfer; notary refuses cash above threshold
- **Currency switch transition (2026)**: the changeover is complete — lev cash stayed legal tender alongside the euro only 1–31 Jan 2026 and mandatory dual price display ended 8 Aug 2026, so deeds, mortgages and payments are in EUR; lev amounts in pre-2026 deeds and contracts are deemed euro amounts at 1.95583. Last open step: commercial banks exchange lev cash until 31 Dec 2026, the BNB with no deadline (2026 data, source BNB Q&A + evroto.bg)
