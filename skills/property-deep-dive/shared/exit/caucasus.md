## 🇬🇪 Georgia
- **DOM**: Tbilisi (Vake/Saburtalo/Vera) 60-120 / Batumi 90-180 (Russian + Iranian + Israeli demand) / regions 150-300+
- **Commission**: 3-5% seller-side typical (often split or buyer-pays in Batumi off-plan); 18% VAT only on services where applicable
- **Contract**: agency contracts informal; Public Service Hall (House of Justice) registration takes ~1-3 days
- **MLS**: no MLS; MyHome.ge + SS.ge dominate
- **Discount**: 5-15% off asking; off-plan Batumi narrower
- **Sell-side**: **NO CGT after 2-yr ownership** (residents and non-residents alike); <2 yrs taxed at 5% of gain (with documented basis) or 1.5-2% of sale price (without); **transaction costs ~0.1-0.5% (lowest in region)**; commission 3-5%
- **Liquidity**: 🟢 MEDIUM-HIGH Tbilisi Vake/Saburtalo/Vera (Russian + Israeli + diaspora demand 2022-2026); 🟠 MEDIUM Batumi (seasonal coastal + STR); 🔴 LOW regions
- **Quirks**: **Lowest friction in Eurasian region** — fast registration, no CGT after 2 yrs, foreigners can buy freehold (except agricultural land); **2022-2024 Russian relocation surge** drove Tbilisi prices up 40-60% (digesting 2025-2026); Batumi off-plan rental-pool deals often disappoint vs. marketed yields; foreign-buyer pool sensitive to regional geopolitics + Russian capital cycle

## 🇦🇲 Armenia
- **DOM**: Yerevan (Kentron / Arabkir / Nor Nork / Davitashen) ~90-180 (post-2022 Russian-relocant wave compressed) / Gyumri 120-250 / Vanadzor 150-300 / regional 200-365+ [verify with State Committee of Real Estate Cadastre / SCREC]
- **Commission**: **3-5% seller-side typical** (often split or buyer-pays); 20% VAT only on services where applicable; informal market beyond major Yerevan brokers
- **Contract**: bilateral sale agreement via notary → SCREC (State Committee of Real Estate Cadastre) registration takes ~3-7 days
- **MLS**: NO MLS; **List.am + Estate.am + MyRealty.am** dominate; Yerevan-centric; **Russian-language portals** (cian.am-style cross-listings) for Russian-relocant segment
- **Discount**: 10-25% off asking 2024-2026 (post-Russian-relocant wave appreciation digestion); regional deeper
- **Sell-side**: **CGT integrated into PIT** — flat 21% on gain (2026 — verify SRC); **NO CGT exemption equivalent to GE 2-yr rule**; commission 3-5%; State Registry fee modest; primary residence partial exemption — verify SRC
- **Liquidity**: 🟢 MEDIUM-HIGH Yerevan Kentron / Arabkir (Russian + IR + LB diaspora demand 2022-2026); 🟠 MEDIUM Gyumri / Vanadzor; 🔴 LOW regional; **Russian-relocant wave 2022-24 drove ~50% peak Yerevan appreciation** (digesting 2025-2026)
- **Quirks**: **HIGH SEISMIC 1988 Spitak M6.8 baseline (~25,000 deaths) + Yerevan/Gyumri/Vanadzor microzoning**; **post-2020/2023 Karabakh war + ~120k displaced Sep 2023 + CSTO suspension Feb 2024**; agricultural land barred for foreigners; STR Decision 1158-N (2018); coworking IT-based hubs Tumo + Impact Hub Yerevan; Beeline / VivaCell / Ucom fiber gigabit; **Investor Residency AMD 50M (~USD 130k) deposit OR property → 5yr renewable**; **Citizenship by descent (Diaspora) Art. 13 streamlined** for global Armenian diaspora (~7M); naturalization 3yr PR + dual since 2007; **Romanian-style EU-passport-by-descent NOT applicable** — AM is not EU candidate

## 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan
- **DOM**: Almaty (Bostandyk / Medeu / Almaly central) ~90-180 / Almaty secondary (Auezov / Turksib / Alatau) 150-300 / Astana (Esil / Saryarka / Almaty district) 90-180 (post-2017 capital-relocation maturity) / Shymkent / Karaganda / Aktobe / Aktau Caspian regional 180-365+ [verify with NJSC "Government for Citizens" USREC unified cadastre + eGov.kz]
- **Commission**: **3-5% seller-pays typical** (often split or buyer-pays); 12% VAT (НДС / ҚҚС) on commission where applicable; informal market beyond major Almaty / Astana brokers
- **Contract**: bilateral sale agreement notarized → USREC (Unified State Real Estate Cadastre — launched 1 July 2023) registration via Government for Citizens / ЦОН public service centers; **foreigners on TRP CANNOT own residential housing** (Law on Legal Status of Foreigners Art. 9 — PRP required); LLP/ТОО workaround common
- **MLS**: NO formal MLS; **Krisha.kz + OLX.kz + nedvizhimostpro.kz** dominate; Russian + Kazakh language listings; AIFC English-law option for Astana International Financial Centre participants
- **Discount**: 10-20% off asking 2024-2026 (post-2022 Russian-relocant-wave appreciation digestion + KZT volatility cycle); **Investor Visa US$300k 10 May 2025 — REAL ESTATE EXPLICITLY EXCLUDED** as qualifying investment shifts foreign-buyer pool composition (no longer residence-pathway-buyers)
- **Sell-side**: residential held >1 year EXEMPT from PIT (Tax Code Art. 331); held ≤1 year taxed at PIT rate (10% flat current; from 1 Jan 2026 progressive 10%/15% above 8,500 MCI under New Tax Code); **non-resident individual sale of KZ-located residential 15% flat** on gross or net (Tax Code Art. 644 + 645) / 20% if non-resident from tax-haven; commission 3-5%; USREC registration fee modest
- **Liquidity**: 🟢 MEDIUM-HIGH Almaty Bostandyk / Medeu / Almaly prime (Russian + IT + AIFC + diaspora demand 2022-2026); 🟠 MEDIUM Astana Esil / Saryarka prime; 🟠 MEDIUM Almaty secondary; 🔴 LOW Shymkent / Karaganda / Aktobe / Aktau regional; VERY LOW rural; **Almaty HIGH SEISMIC + mudflow + winter PM2.5 smog** dominant physical risk; **2022 January Bloody Tragedy (Қаңтар / Janury)** demographic series-break for Almaty + Mangystau
